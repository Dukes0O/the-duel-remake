# Plane-school reference photos

Generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen on 6 November 2026 for round 6 of
TOWN-ART-OUTPOSTS. The side, front, rear and overhead are separate images, each
asked for a single isolated, dead-on orthographic view with the entire plane
inside the frame, on a plain pale studio background, with no text or logos.
The side photo also shows the separate yellow/red slide. The clean side-wall
and overhead-wall photos remove the slide and lettering from the fuselage
surfaces. Prompts asked the images to depict the same salvaged airliner school.

The images are generated references, not photographs of a real aircraft. The
front and rear do not fully agree with the side and plan views; the review
sheet documents the exported model's visible faults for Claude's verdict.
