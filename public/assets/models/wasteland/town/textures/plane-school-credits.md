# Plane-school reference photos

Generated with OpenAI ImageGen on 6 November 2026 for TOWN-ART-OUTPOSTS, round 5.

The original board prompt requested one wide board of four equal, uncropped orthographic photographs of the same salvaged airliner-fuselage school: dead-on side, dead-on front, dead-on rear, and straight-down plan. It specified a pale studio ground, an enlarged window row, red painted SCHOOL letters, the doorway and slide, weathered metal, no real brands or logos, and matching proportions and details.

The clean-wall board was made by editing the original board. Its prompt preserved the same four-panel cameras and object while removing only the slide from the side and overhead views. Grid overlays at 50 px spacing show the clean and original side/overhead fuselage edges and roof landmarks align within a few pixels. The end panels are unchanged crops from the original board.

`plane-school-side.jpg`, `plane-school-front.jpg`, `plane-school-rear.jpg` and `plane-school-overhead.jpg` are original photo views. `plane-school-side-wall.jpg` and `plane-school-overhead-wall.jpg` are the clean carve views. All are project-owned generated references.
