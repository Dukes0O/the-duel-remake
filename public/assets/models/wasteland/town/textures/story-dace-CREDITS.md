# Dace of the salt pans

OpenAI ImageGen made an original three-view character reference for STORY-PEOPLE on 18 November 2026. Dace is Rell's fourteen-year-old nephew in *Five Cold Nights*.

The reference asked for the same lean teenage salt raker front-on, in profile and from behind, standing on a white studio floor. Short uneven dark hair, sun-touched freckled skin, a mended indigo overshirt, oatmeal undershirt, charcoal work trousers with one patched knee, ankle boots and a small canvas satchel distinguish him from the other Walkers. Each 512 × 1024 panel was reduced to 256 × 512. The rear panel was reflected to keep the satchel on the hip visible in the front view.

The three JPEGs here feed `tools/blender/story-walkers.py`. The released town Walker references, the GFX-00 fighter skeleton and the in-game Cinder, Auntie Solder and Ash Man rigs supply the rest of the cast.
