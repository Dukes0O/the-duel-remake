# Town Works material credits

All eight JPGs in this folder are original generated photographic material
references and runtime texture sources for The Duel, created 5 November 2026
with the built-in image generator. They were cropped from two generated
photographic material atlases, converted to 512 x 512 RGB JPG at quality 84,
and packed into the single town-works runtime atlas by
`tools/blender/town-works.py`. No external source art, brands, or licensed
marks are used.

## Prompts

Atlas A: "One square image split into four equal clean quadrants: rusted round
steel pipes with dark hollow ends; pale cut timber logs with clear rings and
grey weathered bark; red-orange stacked bricks with mortar flecks; grey-blue
mill steel with rusty edges. Flat orthographic close-up surfaces in bright
overcast daylight, sharp realistic wear, no shadows, perspective, lettering,
or borders."

Atlas B: "One square image split into four equal clean quadrants: weathered
corrugated galvanised tin with rust streaks; pale fine sand; wet red-brown
clay; mixed dusty salvage of tyre rubber, bent painted metal, bolts and cloth.
Flat close-up textures in bright overcast daylight, sharp photoreal detail,
no shadows, perspective, lettering, or borders."
