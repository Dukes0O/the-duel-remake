# School bus photo credits

Four original AI-generated studio reference photographs made for the TOWN-ART-SERVICES school building with OpenAI image generation on 8 November 2026. The side view is the scale reference. The front and rear were regenerated separately from it after measurement so their bus width and roof height agree. No third-party source material was used.

- `school-bus-side.jpg`: dead-on orthographic long-side elevation.
- `school-bus-front.jpg`: dead-on orthographic front elevation.
- `school-bus-rear.jpg`: dead-on orthographic rear elevation.
- `school-bus-top.jpg`: straight-down orthographic plan view.

The references depict the bus body alone. The lean-to and chalkboard are modeled as separate pieces. The side view controls the tyre radius because the generated end views still depict smaller tyres.
