# School bus photo credits

Four original AI-generated studio reference photographs made for the TOWN-ART-SERVICES school building with OpenAI image generation on 8 November 2026. The side view was generated as the subject reference; the front, rear and top views were generated separately using it to keep one bus design. No third-party source material was used.

- `school-bus-side.jpg`: dead-on orthographic long-side elevation.
- `school-bus-front.jpg`: dead-on orthographic front elevation.
- `school-bus-rear.jpg`: dead-on orthographic rear elevation.
- `school-bus-top.jpg`: straight-down orthographic plan view.

The references depict the bus body alone. The lean-to and chalkboard are modeled as separate pieces.
