# Town dome art credits

The Scraptown exterior reference and material pictures were generated with
OpenAI image generation on 5 November 2026. The full generated reference stays
outside the repository. The checked-in JPGs are crops or compressed material
copies. No third-party images, brands or marks are used.

Every image below is a separate repeating JPEG material in `dome.glb`; none is
packed into an atlas. The brushes on the sign are part of its photographed face.

## Crops from the Scrapdome gate reference photo

Prompt:

> Photorealistic documentary photograph of an original fictional desert scrap arena gate in bright flat overcast daylight. Two complete faded red double-decker city buses, each 11 m long, 2.5 m wide and 4.4 m tall, stand upright on end as the gate piers, noses facing the approaching road. Their real proportions are unmistakable: two decks of narrow dark broken windows, curved metal bodywork, front radiator grille, paired lamps, wheels, scratched red paint over dark rust. A thick rusted plate bridges them with rough black brush lettering exactly SCRAPDOME. Behind them a very high wall of tightly stacked, visibly distinct wrecked cars and a tall irregular hill of overlapping real car wrecks. Scaffold tube rail and posts, torn hanging canvas and flags. A road-level three-quarter location view with the gate filling the frame; realistic 1:1 scale, crisp surface detail, no miniature look. Original setting, no recognizable brands, film motifs, people, watermark or interface.

- `bus-face-left.jpg` and `bus-face-right.jpg`: close crops of each bus nose, including the radiator, lamps and broken front glazing.
- `bus-side-left.jpg` and `bus-side-right.jpg`: separate side crops with the two deck window rows.
- `bus-red-paint.jpg`: worn red body paint crop used on the rounded bus bodies.
- `scrapdome-brush-sign.jpg`: the photographed, rusted SCRAPDOME sign and its rough black brush letters.
- `heap-wrecks.jpg`: stacked wrecks behind the gate, wrapped around the uneven Heap.
- `broken-glass.jpg`: dark broken bus glazing.
- `tire-rubber.jpg`: worn tire rubber from the bus wheels.

## Generated rusted steel material

Prompt:

> A square seamless repeating albedo texture photographed close and straight-on: old dark rusted steel plate made from overlapping welded repair sheets. Chipped dusty faded charcoal paint over dark brown iron oxide, fine scratches, shallow dents, a few small rivets and short weld seams. The plate grain and patina continue naturally across all four edges. Fine detail is sharp; no large unique focal object. Bright diffuse overcast light, flat and even, no cast shadows, directional highlights, text, logos, cars, objects, border, grid, watermark or perspective.

- `rusted-steel.jpg`: compressed 384 x 384 repeat texture for the sign backing, steel work and welded salvage.
