# Town dome art credits

The round 2 model textures use two original images generated with OpenAI image
generation on 5 November 2026. Their source PNGs remain outside the repository.
The committed JPG crops and their uses are:

- `crushed-car-wall.jpg`: a direct crop of the three-row car-wall photograph,
  used as the rusted wall material. 1024 x 256 px.
- `scrapdome-sign.jpg`: a 4:1 crop of the rusty orange SCRAPDOME plate, used on the
  sign material.
- `bus-front.jpg` and `bus-side.jpg`: crops of the two complete red buses in the
  reference, kept as source pictures for the upright gate bodies.
- `ticket-booth.jpg`: the TICKETS booth and roof crop, used on weathered boards.
- `corrugated-tin.jpg`: the booth awning crop, used on galvanized sheet metal.
- `dirt.jpg`: a tire-tracked road crop, used on the ground.

The reference photo and the separate car-wall photo are original fictional
scrapyard scenes. There are no real brands or third-party marks. The model
exports one embedded 1K JPEG wear atlas and remains below the card's 3 MB limit.

## SCRAPDOME reference photo prompt

> Photorealistic wide documentary photograph of an original fictional desert scrap arena entrance in bright, flat overcast daylight, shot from road level. The full truck-sized opening is formed by two complete old faded-red city buses standing upright as gate piers, with real bus fronts, windshields, grilles, headlights and wheels visible. A broad rusty orange sign plate bridges them, exact black hand-painted brush lettering SCRAPDOME. Beside it is a small battered timber-and-corrugated ticket booth with a clear TICKETS board. A curved outer bank fills the frame behind and to both sides: three continuous rows of recognizable real crushed cars, visibly different faded red, blue, cream and rust paint, broken glass and dark tyres, not repeated abstract shapes. Thin dark steel walkway rail, crossed scaffold poles, scraps of canvas, ragged flags, four lattice floodlight towers. A steep irregular heap of crushed cars visible through the opening. Dusty tire-marked tan earth. Real proportions, crisp surface detail, muted dusty colors and natural overcast shadows, like a location photo, no toy miniature, no film franchise references, no game UI, no watermark.

## Three-row crushed-car wall prompt

> Photorealistic, wide straight-on documentary photograph for a 3D game material: exactly three horizontal rows of crushed real cars stacked tightly as a continuous scrapyard wall, filling the full frame edge to edge. Each row contains distinct old compact and sedan bodies crushed flat but still clearly recognizable as cars, with hoods, doors, broken windows, wheels and tires visible. Mix many dusty faded paint colors: muted red, faded blue, old cream, dull white and rust, dark brown rust blooms and grime, all under powdery desert dust. No cars in front of the wall, no ground, no sky, no perspective convergence; camera perpendicular to the wall, neutral long lens. Bright flat overcast daylight, natural photographic detail, believable 1:1 scale, clearly separate silhouettes and real gaps between bodies. Avoid uniform repeated shapes, toy or miniature look, orange-only palette, text, signs, logos, people, watermark, stylization, illustration, dramatic shadows.
