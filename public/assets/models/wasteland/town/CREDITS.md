# Town dome art credits

The embedded 1K wear atlas in `dome.glb` uses an original image generated with
OpenAI image generation on 5 November 2026. It is used as a color tile on
salvaged steel and galvanized panels. There are no third-party marks or source
images. The packed-ground tile reuses the project's `public/assets/textures/rally-gravel.png`,
generated on 19 September 2026; its source credit and prompt are in
`public/assets/textures/CREDITS.md` and `docs/IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`.

Prompt:

> Photorealistic seamless square material reference texture, orthographic straight-on macro photograph of salvaged exterior vehicle body metal for a post-apocalyptic demolition arena. Dark rusty corrugated bus steel with broad recognizable patches of old faded oxide-red and ochre yellow paint remaining over iron, chipped and peeled irregular edges exposing bare silvery steel, deep reddish-brown rust blooms, narrow rust drips below fasteners, greasy dark seams and fine scratches. Moderate contrast, crisp specific material detail at real-world scale, a few panel joints and rivets but no large distinctive objects. Even bright overcast diffuse daylight, no directional shadows or highlights baked in. Flat material scan fills the whole square, seamless repeat at every edge, no perspective, no vehicle silhouette, no scene, no letters or logos, no border. This image will be used as an albedo tile inside one shared 1K material atlas for a Blender model.

The Scrapdome photographic reference is a look-study source only. It is cropped
on the comparison sheet and is not embedded in the model.
