# Generated Rattler art

`rattler.glb` embeds the four original generated color pictures in `textures/`:
faded paint with dark rust, dusty rubber, blackened steel, and ground dust. OpenAI's built-in
image generator made dusty rubber and blackened steel on 2026-11-05, and the
body rust and ground dust textures on 2026-11-06. Their full prompts and use are in
`docs/WASTELAND_ART.md`. They are development artwork, not physical scans.

Falcone F42 (`public/assets/models/classics/falcone_f42.glb`), an original in-project model, was inspected as a cab and fender donor for CAR-ART-RATTLER round 6. The cutout trial was not retained in the final Rattler model.
