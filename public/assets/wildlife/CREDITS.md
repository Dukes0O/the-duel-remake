# Wildlife model credits

Every model here is built by a recipe in `tools/blender/wildlife/`; `sources.json` there
holds each one's source, licence and file hash. The credits page shown to players is
`public/assets/audio/credits.html`.

- **Quaternius**, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
  [Deer](https://poly.pizza/m/T6Cs7tmMHJ) and [Wolf](https://poly.pizza/m/P1gU3Qkr9r), meshes,
  rigs and animation clips. Reshaped and given new coats as the white-tailed deer, moose, mule
  deer, elk, bighorn sheep, roe deer, red deer, chamois, ibex and **goat** (from the deer) and
  the eastern wolf, cougar, coyote, red fox and **camp dog** (from the wolf). No Quaternius
  texture is shipped.
- **Poly by Google**, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/):
  [Black bear](https://poly.pizza/m/56ym_pyVnel) and [Beaver](https://poly.pizza/m/fwtA7VLrXPr).
  Scale, coats, rigs and Idle and Walk animation adapted for The Duel. No author endorsement
  is implied.
- **Original recipes for The Duel** (no third-party source): kangaroo, echidna, the marmots,
  wild boar, the birds, and from WILD-ART-WASTE the **chicken**, **turkey vulture**, **desert
  monitor** and **scorpion**.

The WILD-ART-WASTE animals were judged against photographs from Wikimedia Commons (public
domain or CC0, listed in `tools/blender/wildlife/waste-references.json`). The photographs
appear only on the review sheets under `docs/board/looks/wild-art-waste/`; no game file
contains them.

**WILD-ART-CITY** (the animals of Harbour Lights and the city courses):

- **Quaternius**, CC0 1.0, the Wolf above: reshaped and given a new coat as the **street cat**
  (`street-cat.glb`), with the donor's rig and clips.
- **Original recipes for The Duel** (no third-party source): **black kite**, **little egret**,
  **black-faced spoonbill**, **pigeon**, **owl** (a barn owl) and **raccoon**.
- Judged against photographs from Wikimedia Commons (public domain or CC0, listed in
  `tools/blender/wildlife/city-references.json`). The photographs appear only on the review
  sheets under `docs/board/looks/wild-art-city/`; no game file contains them.
