THE DUEL / REDLINE

Crafting the open road.

Detailed car model

Car Concept by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH, 2024. Used under CC BY 4.0. Adapted with paint variants, wheel animation, mesh batching and crash deformation. Original emblem meshes are omitted and plate artwork is replaced in the game. The upstream license excludes logos and trademarks. No author or Khronos endorsement is implied.

Landscape materials and lighting

Gravelly Sand by Dario Barresi, and Zwartkops Curve Sunset by Dimitrios Savva / Jarod Guest, from Poly Haven under CC0. Ground maps are tiled and tinted. The HDR supplies car reflections. Canyon, granite, meadow, bark, concrete and road textures, transparent pine needles and grass, clouds, crowd cutouts, tire smoke and the scene/vehicle references were generated for this game. Full prompts are recorded in the project documentation.

Recorded revs and acceleration

V8 engine rev by overmedium, and Acceleration Car by Fabrizio84, both released under CC0 1.0. Public HQ previews were decoded to mono, filtered, trimmed, level-matched and crossfaded into idle, acceleration and coasting layers. Idle, load and coast loops also receive slow volume leveling. Short recording excerpts provide throttle and gear-change effects. These are edited field recordings, not recordings of the fictional cars. No author endorsement is implied.

Steady engine and fallback

Racing car engine sound loops by domasx2. Released under CC0 1.0. Edited here to remove DC offset, normalize volume and smooth the loop. A filtered, level-matched version supplies the steady high-speed engine without extra pitched engine layers. The earlier version remains as a fallback.

Tire recording

Car tire squeal skid loop by audible-edge / Tom Haigh, loop edit by qubodup. Used under CC BY 3.0. Resampled, normalized and crossfaded here. Pitch and volume vary during play. No author endorsement is implied.

Recorded landscape ambience

Sea waves on the beach, some seagulls in background by felix.blume, Sunny Forest Ambience 1 by deadrobotmusic, and Stadium Crowd by stomachache. All three are real field recordings released under CC0 1.0. Public HQ previews were decoded to mono, trimmed, filtered, level-limited and crossfaded into quiet landscape loops. Source recipes are retained; downloads are cached outside the game. Runtime loops are compressed losslessly. No author endorsement is implied.

Original sound design

Explosion, impacts, engine underlay, wind, gravel, the two-voice police siren, landing thumps, harmless junk-car crunches, boost, chicken-flock flutter, bonus chime and musical sequence were synthesized for The Duel. Seven original car voicings change the pitch, gain and filters of the shared licensed recordings. Tunnel reflections use short filtered delays. These are not recordings of seven separate vehicles.
