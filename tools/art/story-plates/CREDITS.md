# Night 2 · Strangers at the gate

`night-2-painting.jpg` is an original illustration made for *The Duel* with OpenAI image generation on 18 November 2026. The references comprised the corrected Blender frame, its visible terrain outline, its cast at a larger scale and a small painted steel swatch from an earlier trial. The returned image measured 1672 × 941. It was scaled to the camera's 1920 × 1080 and encoded as a 379,199-byte JPEG. The one selected illustration is retained here for the Part 1 review. Its SHA-256 is `03371ce198ed0babcb04b9db92deb86ce0f13e4d673cc370a0dade5414f9a39e`.

The game supplies the Tin Town gate, shacks, carts, Walker models, rig, wardrobe, native ground and East Road. The offline stage uses the existing town steel texture and two Poly Haven CC0 photographs with their relief maps: *Gravel Road* by Amal Kumar and *Dry Ground Rocks* by Rob Tuytel.
