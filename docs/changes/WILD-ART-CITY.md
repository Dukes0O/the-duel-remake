# WILD-ART-CITY change note

- **What changed.** Seven city animals pass round 1 and join `public/assets/wildlife/`: black kite, little egret, black-faced spoonbill, pigeon, owl (a barn owl), raccoon, street cat. Six are original recipes (five on `bird.py` with new parts in `city_parts.py`; the raccoon with its own rig); the street cat is reshaped from the CC0 Quaternius wolf. None left out. Sheet and verdicts: `docs/board/looks/wild-art-city/round-1.jpg` and `round-1-review.md`. Lessons: `docs/ART_LINE.md`, "City animals".
- **On no course.** `src/wildlife.js` already names these animals on Harbour Lights and the four city courses, so a model row alone would put them there on merge (Harbour Lights at night is the frame check's scene). They are listed in `HELD_FROM_COURSES` in `src/wildlife-models.js`; `APPROVED_MODEL_IDS` leaves held ids out, so courses and the wildlife count are unchanged. The town reads `WILDLIFE_MODELS` and may use `pigeon` and `cat` now. A placement card takes ids off the list.
- **Ids.** The rows use the ids the course tables already have: `egret` is `little-egret.glb`, `cat` is `street-cat.glb`, `owl` is the barn owl. `src/wildlife.js` gains comments only.
- **Shared tools, all additive:** `bird.py` (optional `eyes`, `idle_pose`, `fly_pose`), `adapt.py` (one block of set additions), `waste_sheet.py` and `waste_references.py` (card name, folders and ground are settable; `city_sheet.py` sets them). No earlier animal's recipe output changes.
