# Release 9 October 2026 (prepared for Claude, Release Manager)

Kyle approved in chat on 9 October 2026: everything in the Preview goes into the main game.

## What changed

- src/feature-flags.js: Convoy Raid, the Tollkeeper, Baron Blackiron, Warlord
  cards, Arsenal wave three, Fight Voices, computer crews, Ambush Alley and
  Gearhead Gunn are `on`. `career-backup` stays `dev` (a save-protection gate,
  not a Preview feature). The switches stay in the catalog until retired.
- start-preview.bat is unchanged. Its flag list may keep the now-on switches,
  as it did after the 4 October release.
- README.md and docs/VERIFICATION.md carry the player-facing and the evidence text.

## Changed assertions (each because a switch's state changed on purpose)

Thirteen test files failed on the first full run, all for that one reason. No fingerprint,
number, tolerance or expected value was changed. After the changes the full tier
passes, 363 of 363.

- Checks that said "this switch starts in dev" or "a released player cannot
  enable it" now say `on`, and the same development-switch rules are still
  tested with that one switch pinned to `dev` in a test catalog:
  test-feature-flags (the catalog count and state check, and the production,
  URL and QA rules for all nine, now looped over a dev-pinned catalog),
  test-wasteland-beta (the whole catalog), test-arena-convoy (Convoy Raid has
  its own switch; native admission is default-off, explicit-off, explicit-on),
  test-arena-ambush-alley, test-arsenal-wave3, test-cpu-crews (two checks),
  test-fight-voices, test-vesper-gear (the warlord-cards state check) and
  test-warlord-tollkeeper (the Tollkeeper's switch state).
- Older baselines that relied on a switch being off now say so:
  test-warlord-blackiron names `'arsenal-wave3': false` (the player and the
  ordinary computer car keep the wave-three gate); test-warlord-gunn compares
  the yard and Armory against Gunn off by override or pinned to dev, and adds
  one check that the released yard shows his FIGHT button; test-territory-screen
  pins `gunn` to dev for the "Warlord fight coming later" status;
  test-warlord-settlement pins `tollkeeper` to dev for the saved future defeat
  that must not claim an unbuilt reward; test-warlord-tollkeeper pins
  `blackiron` to dev for the separate unbuilt-fight status.

## Browser scenarios

Run in the lane with private ports and memory-only saves: convoy-raid,
gunn, blackiron, arena-ambush-alley, cpu-crews, arsenal-wave3, new-warlords,
fight-voices and frame-pacing. All nine passed with no warnings or issues
(details in docs/VERIFICATION.md).
