# WEAPON-WATER-CANNON: the water cannon, on the fire truck and as town gear (switch `water-cannon`, dev)

**What changed.** One stream, `src/arsenal/water-cannon.js`: a straight 14 m line from the nose, about a car wide, stopping at the first car. It shoves that car along the water (7 m/s a second, at most 4 m/s a stream; a game number), puts out a burning car and ends a flamethrower burst by the ash rule, and never damages. Counter: leave the line, or shield (stops the shove). On the fire truck H sprays it (`world-job.js`, free drive and jobs), water flowing only while flames or a car are in front; one tank: the fire call's (`douse.js` drains it for `waterOnCar`) or the truck's own. In the Armory it is a slot weapon (9 s, 2 s burst, 400 scrap, upgrades recharge only) offered at any rank once the player's own town has a finished Fire Station (`town-gear.js`; the app reads it once and the town screen tells it on load and save; nothing new saved). Computer cars and the Baron never carry it. The look is TOWN-FIRE-JOB's jet, moved unchanged to `src/arsenal/water-stream-view.js`, drawn by the truck and `combat-scene.js`. Cues: `weapon.water.spray` (the pour of `town.spray`), `weapon.water.douse` (the hiss of `town.fire.out`); on the truck, `town.spray`. Design in `docs/ARSENAL.md` "Town gear".
