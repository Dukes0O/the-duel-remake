# Rustwall salvage depth and canyon strata

## Starting point and design choice before code

The merged EGG-02-P1 wall and wash are development assets. The round-10 independent review scored wall likeness 3/5, wash likeness 3/5, readability 4/5, grounding 3/5 and scene consistency 3/5. The wall is 7,777,248 bytes, 58,914 triangles and 13 draws; the wash is 3,898,584 bytes. The wall has a 35 m structural core spanning over 400 m and a real 9 by 7 m movable gate. Its facing is still based on 30 regular bays, six rows of panels and many repeated rivets. The wash uses a joined ribbon with three fixed cross-slope bands, leaving broad, repeated stripes. These are the two modeling methods to replace.

Build a nonperiodic facade from an explicit table of roughly 10–12 unequal salvage sections over the existing wall span. Each section sets its own roof crown, foreground depth, car-stack placement, large brace and material region. Spend geometry on a few distinct tall mass shapes and grounded load paths, while leaving sky breaks between them. Reclaim triangles from the repeated rivets, interior tile faces, duplicated ladders and small flat filler before adding macro geometry. The structural core, wall placement, physical course and 9 by 7 m clear gate do not change. No decorative piece may block the opening in its closed or fully raised state.

The prominent salvage uses the proven **offline** welded-car method from `tools/blender/scrapdome-yard.py` as a read-only recipe. Import the existing Falcone F42 and Banshee production GLBs in Blender; weld coincident vertices at 0.1 mm before decimation; preserve a connected body with hood, cabin and trunk, a separate dark glass region and source-measured wheel/ring hollows. Crush and tilt copies with repeatable authored section transforms, stacking each visible lower body on the wall foot or another measured car surface. Batch the result into one new GLB mesh named `welded-car-hulks` using the existing `hulks` atlas/material; this adds at most one draw. Its exported node extras record each source GLB path and SHA-256, the 0.0001 m weld threshold, pre/post weld connected-component counts, per-template body/glass/wheel triangle counts and actual placed hulk count. Tests must inspect the exported mesh geometry and bounds as well as the extras, so metadata alone cannot pass. The existing `hulk-stacks` mesh may retain only small distant filler. Runtime loads no source car for the wall. Material paint uses broad connected oxide, dark steel, soot and worn-edge regions rather than the current checker rhythm. The same three 512-square wall atlas sets, at most 60,000 actual GLB triangles and 24 actual material draws remain hard contracts. Record the per-object triangle inventory **before** editing the generator and remeasure after every export; do not raise a bound to fit art.

The wash remains one joined runtime ribbon per side, no more than 30,000 rendered triangles and two draws, using the same single 1024-square rock atlas. Keep the current two segments per physical bank and three cross-slope strips per segment. At each **shared** station, deterministically shift the two interior contour levels from the fixed 0.38/0.72 ratios within a narrow ordered range; use the same world station and side to produce identical XYZ and UV values on both sides of a join. Map atlas V by normalized distance along the actual slope/height, with a bounded low-frequency warp, so dark erosion seams bend with the wash instead of forming broad horizontal stripes. Paint broken rock strata and weathered colors into the existing atlas. All preparation happens once when the scene loads, never in a frame update. Every rendered vertex and triangle centroid stays inside the 2 mm union of the original oriented physical bank boxes on Routes A, B and C. Joined edges must match exactly, normals face daylight on both sides, and the original collision objects, route, RNG and replay fingerprints stay unchanged.

The wash visual target is the **background canyon context**, not a close geology study, in the approved `public/assets/reference/wasteland-art-direction.png`. Use its top-right crop at about x1010–1470, y15–310 in the 1774×887 image, retaining the 460:295 aspect and labeling that scope on every P2 comparison sheet. The existing `rustwall-gate.png` remains the wall/gate reference. A reviewer scores actual High and Performance game views; no geometry count or texture check stands in for likeness.
