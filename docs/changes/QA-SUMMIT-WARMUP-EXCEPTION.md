# QA-SUMMIT-WARMUP-EXCEPTION (cloud/QA-SUMMIT-WARMUP-EXCEPTION)

Cause: Three r171 compileAsync polls `properties.get(material).currentProgram.isReady()` every 10 ms for each material it collected. A material disposed while that poll is open has no program, so the poll throws an uncaught TypeError and its promise never settles (the 15 s timeout then lets the draw through). Reproduced with real Three in Chromium (scratch, not committed; no GPU extension needed): dispose one collected material right after compileAsync, one pageerror "reading 'isReady'", promise pending; control run resolves.
Sites that freed materials at once, outside the existing deferral (retireObject, combat effects): render3d build() disposing snowmenView and avalancheView (they live in the retiring world: the Summit cases), Bowl view clear() (stadium, crush props) and Bowl weapon rigs (disposeRig when a truck leaves the fight: the Bowl cases).
Fix: all three now leave the scene at once and free their materials through warmup.releaseWhenIdle. render3d: 2 small edits (build, createBowlView({retire})); bowl-view and bowl-weapons-view take an optional `retire` (default: immediate, so nothing changes without warmup). No Three patch, no filtering, no new dependency.
