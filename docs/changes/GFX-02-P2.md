## Sewn sleeve geometry

Use the existing camera-local authoring frame and the same elbow-to-wrist centreline, bones, grip and mounted RPG/wrench poses. Axial `t` is the current elbow-to-wrist interpolation used by the P1 sleeve stations. `t>=0.82` and the hand/grip envelope are frozen. The first ring at the offscreen start and the wrist/glove contacts also remain fixed. These physical dimensions are inferred garment construction limits, not pixel measurements from the differently framed RPG photo.

Each side gets one connected cloth shell with a broad outer/front panel and a taut inner/underside panel. They share actual boundary vertices/edges at longitudinal seams; UV seams may split exported vertices but must weld to one connected surface within 0.1 mm with no edge having more than two incident faces. There is no second overlapping sleeve tube or hidden closed cap. Panel boundaries are measured in authoring XY azimuth from +X toward +Y: R front/outer panel `[40°,200°]`, L `[-50°,125°]`, with the complementary arc as the underside. The two longitudinal seam paths may drift at most 12° across the forearm and sit 1–2 mm proud of the local surface; they must not become bright painted stripes. The visible front panel uses the existing cloth chart with continuous longitudinal UV, and the underside samples the same padded cloth material with altered grain direction only through UV orientation. Keep existing exact skin and tool charts untouched.

Freeze these first shape controls in `sleeves.R/L`, with each path defined by ordered `(t, radialOffsetMetres)` stations and a broad angular falloff that reaches zero at the sewn boundary. R outer panel: elbow bulge `+0.018 m` over `t=.34..52` with peak near `.43`; gathered pinch `−0.009 m` over `.60..68`; diagonal compression ridges `+0.012 m` near `.69` and `+0.010 m` near `.77`. L outer panel: bulge `+0.021 m` over `.34..52`, pinch `−0.010 m` over `.60..68`, ridges `+0.013 m` near `.68` and `+0.009 m` near `.78`. A front/outer radial falloff spans roughly 105–140°; the underside stays close to the P1 radius and can narrow by at most 4 mm to show cloth tension. The absolute bulge envelope is 15–25 mm, pinch 8–12 mm, and ridge height 8–15 mm. Smooth the transition across neighbouring axial rings without periodic whole-circumference bands. Reuse or replace the P1 small fold paths deliberately: broad panel volume should dominate the silhouette, while fine folds may remain only if they do not restore a regular tube/ring rhythm.

The source JSON must store these values, the angular domains, seam clearance and the frozen `t>=.82` boundary. The builder may choose triangulation but cannot invent different controls without updating the written design before code. Start within the P1 4,260-hand triangle allocation; RPG adds 3,276, so at most 464 extra hand triangles remain under the 8,000 loaded limit. Reclaim uniform hidden ring/subdivision detail before spending them. One hand material primitive plus the unchanged tool must stay within three draws. The 1024 color/surface/normal set, padded actual-face UVs and original selected image identity remain fixed for this first proof.
