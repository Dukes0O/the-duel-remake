# STORY-TALK

The `talk` job step freezes the world. Its camera frames the speaker over the player’s shoulder or bonnet. Radio, low speech and thoughts have distinct bubbles. `R` recalls spoken lines. A number previews stock while held and chooses on release. Questions can open other questions or choices. The screen receives the words, one thought and an explicit ember or gas preview. The `storyChoice` event returns the choice ID, its effects and effects from asked questions when the player commits.
