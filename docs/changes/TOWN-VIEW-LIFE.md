# TOWN-VIEW-LIFE

**What changed.** `src/town/view/town-walkers.js` draws a sample of the town's real people walking the road graph between home, work, the nearest tap and shop by the game hour, workers bending at open sites, children at school and play, carts and trucks chasing their real haulers with turning wheels, and goats, hens and the camp dog once their `WILDLIFE_MODELS` rows exist. A click on a walker rings that person and opens their card beside them. `src/town/view/town-fx.js` draws fire in the helicopter's fire look with a flickering light and steam when it goes out, chimney and work smoke leaning with the real wind, dust days, swaying reeds, and turns `windmill:rotor`, `pump-house:flywheel` and the water tower's `fill-1` to `fill-3` gauge. Hooks: `town-scene.js`, `town-entry.js`, `src/world/scablands-scene.js` (the town drive draws the same life near the car, people living at their layout spots on a working-day clock). All behind `wasteland-town`; sound cues `town.fire`, `town.crowd`.

**The sample and its cost.** Each person has a fixed hash from the view's own seed. Once a second the view scans people in hash order and takes the first who are out of doors near the camera's ground point (1.3 times the camera's distance, at least 70 m): up to 160 at the mayor's zoom, none past 900 m, at most 40 joining a second. 10,000 people in Node: choosing 5 to 7 ms once a second, a frame 0.5 ms; the first far look 12 ms (1,000 new people a second at most). In the browser with 432 people: 0.4 to 0.8 ms a frame and 100 to 120 draw calls (11 to 16 parts per walker model); frames were the same with the people and effects hidden. No frame check was run.

**Pictures, judged by hand** (real game, memory saves, a 432-person town): from the mayor's camera at 90 m, from 28 m and from the driver's seat. Fixed from them: feet sank 15 cm into built roads; the person card covered the person; the drive's people all lived at the camp and walked in one column, and stood still under a fixed noon.

**Checks** (commit in the merge message). `tools/test-town-view-life.mjs` (new, 77 checks): a town stepped 30 days with the view drawing, a fire and picks matches the same town stepped without it byte for byte; the view's seed is its own; walks keep to roads and turn at corners; the standing foot stays planted in the drawn matrices; walkers face the way they walk and stand on road tops; a click finds the person; carts only with their part; no animal file asked for before its row. `test-town-view`, `test-town-map-markers`, `test-town-hooks`, `test-town-visits`, `test-town-ui` pass.

**Removed.** Nothing: the town had no walkers or effects before.
