# OPEN-T-RUNAWAY-2: the Runaway's browser scenario passes

- **Cause.** Three faults, all in the scenario `tools/scenarios/open-runaway.mjs`. (1) It parked the car at (47, 85) outside Tin Town's ring fence and steered straight at (46, 66), so the car stopped against `tin-town-fence-29` at (49.5, 76), on flat ground (height -0.06 m) at 0.8 mph. (2) It set the time of day in `definition.open.partOfDay`, but the open roam keeps its own clock (`dayClock`), whose `open.daySeconds` decides the part of day. The gate therefore stayed at dawn, and Clinker's talk could not open. (3) Its `choose` loop checked for Continue in one call and clicked it in the next. Continue hides when the options appear, so one run in two failed with "A conversation button was unavailable."
- **Could a player meet it?** No. A player drives in through the gate and reaches dusk by playing on. Every Runaway place a player must walk to was checked against the solids, and each can be reached within its range (the shed and loft spots sit just inside their buildings' walls, which push a walker less than 1.5 m). No game file changed, so no new game check was needed.
- **Fix.** The car enters by the gate at (-25, 90) to (-25, 50) and parks at (22, 52) near the shed. Both legs were checked clear of every solid. The scenario sets `open.daySeconds` to the first second of dawn and of dusk. A single `talkStep` call now checks and clicks the conversation's option or Continue together. A failed step reports which step failed and where the car and the walker stood. Before the last picture, the walker turns to face Clinker at the gate.
- **Checks** on the base commit 1b7cf3ebf plus this change: `open-runaway` browser scenario PASS (two pictures, looked at: the ash prints, then the gate with Clinker waiting outside). `test-open-runaway`, `test-open-missions`, `test-open-threads`, `test-open-roam`, `test-open-roam-start` and `test-thread-shape` pass. Core, `test-replays`, `test-combat-replays` and `test-world-composition` pass (4/4). `npm run build` passes. No assertions changed. No words a player reads changed.
- **Seen, not fixed.** After "Take him. He's yours." the boy is not drawn walking out through the gate: no figure for him remains once the end is `handed-back`. On screen the captions lag behind, but only because the scenario covers minutes of play in a few seconds.
- **Removed:** the scenario's `talkControl` and `clickTalk` helpers (replaced by `talkStep` and `talkReady`) and its old drive from outside the fence.
