## What changed

The route integrity suite now settles a valid 60-second finish, persists and
reloads its profile, checks that another seed, lap count, and historical layout
do not read that record, then settles a faster time on the other seed. After
another save and reload, returning to the original route must still compare
against and display the saved 60-second best. This replaces a comparison of
`bestKey(result)` with `bestKey({...result})`, which always passed without
reading a record.

The core suite now uses two named players through `createPlayer`,
`replacePlayerProfile`, `savePlayers`, `loadPlayers`, and `selectPlayer`.
It records a 45-second finish for Player 1, checks that Riley has no matching
best before and after reload, and checks that switching back restores Player
1's best. This replaces a check of a new empty `createProfile()` that never
selected a second player.

No production source or behavior changed. Both suites use local memory-backed
storage; they do not read or write a real player save.
