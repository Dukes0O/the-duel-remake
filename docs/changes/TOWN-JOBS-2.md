# TOWN-JOBS-2: the town's jobs that are not fights

**What changed.** Five step kinds (`tow`, `repair`, `fence`, `clear`, `hold`) in `src/world/jobs/steps/`, each a pure rule with no clock and nothing to lose, and the Road Rats rule they share (`town-rats.js`). Twelve jobs as rows of `src/town/content/jobs.js`, built by `town-jobs.js`: chapters 11 to 16 (the flour run with pies back, Dot's fire with the Rats, her fence, the chalk run, the goats with the lent show truck, the tanker, Pim's crane) and section 7's five (rescue in the dust, pipe patch, road clear, and one road fight with `side`: the fuel escort, where a stolen truck turns into a chase, and the truck raid). Each hands the town one result (`kind:'job'` in `town-missions.js`), settled once per run id and only while that job is open. All of it is behind `wasteland-town` (dev).
**Checks.** `tools/test-town-jobs.mjs`, 29 tests: every job won 10 of 10 on Easy by a seeded child-pace driver on the real engine, and the water run driven for real (8.1 minutes to Deck Town). Lane tier on f7b6714 (integration c62a679 merged in): 144 of 150 passed. The fixes after it ran `--changed --since f7b6714` on this commit: 28 of 30 passed. `npm run build` passed. No assertion in an older suite changed, and no replay fingerprint or world signature moved.
**The failures are not this card's.** `test-arsenal-runtime`, `test-everburn-job2` and `test-heli-mode` fail the same way on untouched `origin/integration/wasteland` in the cloud; `test-deep-save` needs git history a shallow clone lacks; `test-full-check` and `test-salt-flats` are laptop-only.
**Save review (independent subagent).** Reading the jobs writes nothing, results apply once, and every new record survives a save round trip. Its three faults are fixed and tested: a run that went back for spare sacks was refused, a result with a missing amount counted as zero, and a result for a job that was not open was accepted.
**Sound cues.** Hook on and off `world.bellows.load`; hook lets go `world.fence.snap`; wrench on `repair.start`; joint done `repair.complete`; knocked down, or pushed out of a ring, `repair.interrupted`; fence bundle off `world.bellows.load`; nose meets a wreck `vehicle.crash-impact`; wreck off the road `world.bellows.load`; wreck flattened `vehicle.crush`; pump starts or resumes `town.fill`; Rats arrive, or take the fuel truck, `raider.warning`; Rats leave `rider.breaks-off`; every step done `interface.bonus`.
**Could not run here.** The laptop-only suites (`test-course-kit`, `test-earned-cars-ui`, `test-medals`, `test-drive-through`, `test-full-check`, `test-salt-flats`, `test-salt-flats-art`); the long suites the lane tier left to the night (`test-convoy-tanker-art`, `test-everburn-easy`, `test-field-battle`, `test-first-person-polish`, `test-old-smoke`, `test-opponents`, `test-run-gas-medium`); every browser scenario, look and frame check.
