# BOWL-RIVALS: five rivals, Side by Side and Last Truck Rolling (titan-bowl, dev)

**What changed.** `src/bowl/bowl-rivals.js` holds five rivals as data (name, way of driving, start delay, pace in mph from Easy to Hard) and who drives on each night. Side by Side races the night's rival on the duel's rival rules, held to that rival's pace, on the course's two lanes (`course.features.bowl.lanes` through `bowlLayout`); each truck finishes one full lap from its own start. Last Truck Rolling (nights three to five) loads the Bowl as the arena's own fight with four trucks: its teams, computer drivers, start slots and wreck credit, no weapons or crates, the same show armor for every truck, and a wrecked truck knocked out for good. The two-minute clock decides it: the last truck rolling wins, or at the clock the hardest hitter still rolling. Rivals hit at 0.2, 0.6 and 1 of full strength by level. The player's nose does not tip in the air there (`bowl-air.js`). The arena's HUD, pause card and weapon bar stand aside for the show's (`screen-arena.js`, `combat-hud.js`), and no weapon fires (`game.js`). Big Mo names the rivals and each knock-out; the HUD's goal names the trucks still rolling. Sound cues: a knock-out by the player plays `arena.wreck-credit`, any other `bowl.mo` with Big Mo's line. Open points: `docs/board/questions/BOWL-RIVALS.md`.
