# SKIN-WASTE round 2: Wasteland finishes drawn by code

What changed: `src/paint-finish-look.js` draws the eight finishes in the car's own paint material: rust at edges, seams, arches and low panels; welded, riveted patches; chevron stripes that follow the body; bleach strongest on top; soot low and behind the wheels; also dust, blasting, brushing, flames and blisters. One look table at its top holds every number. Patterns use positions and normals in the car's own metres, not UVs. The hook in `src/vehicle-paint.js` adds the shader the first time a drawn finish is chosen, after the wear shader, so dents still draw on top. The eight presets gain `pattern`. Prices, the gate and Ember are unchanged.
Cost per car per frame: 0 extra draw calls, materials or meshes; 7 noise samples (8 for Boneyard Silver) and about 150 arithmetic steps per painted pixel; shadows unchanged; one shader compile per car the first time a drawn finish is worn, none on later changes.
