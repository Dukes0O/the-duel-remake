# WASTE-TRUCKS-FIT: the Longhaul against a wall

- **Cause.** In the Scrapdome case of `test-arena-shove` the Longhaul did not pass through the wall: it moved 1.87 m inward, away from it. The floor limit lets a car's middle reach 18 m from the centre line, and the wall piece's inner face stands at 19.5 m, which leaves 1.5 m. The widest released car is 1.4 m from its middle to its side; the Longhaul is 1.593 m, and its corners reach further on the curve. A pinned Longhaul therefore overlaps the wall piece, and `_staticContacts` (`src/sim-contacts.js`) pushes it back out each tick by the overlap and, for a computer driver, turns it 0.25 rad a hit, so it drives off the wall.
- **Fix.** No game code changed. The arena events refuse the Workshop machines (Kyle, 27 November 2026), so the Scrapdome case cannot happen. On a Wasteland outing the floor limit is the whole land, and the same scenery rule holds both trucks against the wall. If the arenas are ever opened to the trucks, the Scrapdome floor must be sized for each car's width first.
- **Seen, not changed.** A computer-driven car shoved into Tin Town's 20 cm gate fence jumps 1.2 to 2.1 m back off it in one tick. Released cars do the same, so this is the general scenery rule; the trucks are never computer-driven on an outing.
- **New check.** `tools/test-wasteland-truck-wall.mjs`: the Longhaul and the Bully, as the player's car on a `world-job` outing, parked 5 cm from four real solids (both faces of Tin Town's gate fence, a Dead Mall wall, a Boneyard wreck at an angle) and rammed side-on at 20 and 40 mph by each of the 15 arena cars: 240 rams, each truck stays on its own side. With a 0.6 m gap instead, the 40 mph rams push the truck right up to the wall and it stops there, so the check sees real motion.
- **Checks** on d247aad0f (current `integration/wasteland` merged in): the rule 23 set with the card's suites (`core`, `test-replays`, `test-combat-replays`, `test-world-composition`, `test-wasteland-truck-wall`, `test-arena-shove`, `test-wasteland-cars`, `test-wasteland-vehicles`): 8 passed, 0 failed; `npm run build` passed. No game code changed, so the lane tier was not needed.
- **Changed assertions:** none. **Removed:** nothing; the card's item 2 closed with Kyle's ruling of 27 November.
