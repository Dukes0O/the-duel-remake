# BOWL-SOUND-CRUSH: more crush sounds in the Bowl

- **What changed:** a crush in the Bowl (behind `titan-bowl`) is now a set of nine cues from 19 CC0 Freesound recordings, recipes in `tools/audio/build-bowl-crush.mjs`, 18 Vorbis takes (370 KB) in `public/assets/audio/bowl/`: `bowl.crush.car` (four takes in turn, each piece at its own pitch), `.bus`, `.stack`, `.glass` (0.25 s after the bite), `.groan` (held while the wheels are on the wreck), `.off` (clank as the truck comes off), `.flat` (a wreck run over again), `.pancake` (crushed from the air), `.ooh` (bus, RV, stack, Bus Pile). Chooser `bowlCrushCues` in `src/bowl/bowl-lines.js` for BOWL-CRUSH's `bowlCrush` event to call. Other courses keep `vehicle.crush`.
- **Levels as played** (bank gain 4.2, master .42), against the car-to-car crash (-12.2 to -13.1 LUFS, peaks +0.6 to +1.1 dBTP): car -11.1 to -11.5, bus -10.2 to -10.6, stack -10.2, pancake -9.1 to -9.5, glass, clank and ooh -16, groan and flat -17 LUFS; every peak at or under the crash's (+0.3 dBTP at most).
