# The Far Side: a goat, a boring machine and the place where it rains

Claude, 5 November 2026. A new strand, as `docs/story/the-long-dry.md` section 12 allows, and the first story of The Teeth (`docs/MACHINES.md` sections 6 and 13). Every line and every job. Kyle and Gratian can overrule anything, and this is a good one for Gratian to change, since it is his machine.

**It waits for TUNNEL-TRIAL's go.** Nothing here gets built until Claude has played that trial and passed it. If it fails, this page's people and jobs stay, and Claude rewrites jobs 4 to 7 around another way over the mountains.

## 1. The story in one breath

A goat turns up in Old Tam's kitchen. It isn't his. It has a bell on and it's wet, and nothing has been wet out here in living memory. Brian explains why deserts sit behind mountains: the mountains take all the rain. So on the far side of The Teeth, it is raining. It always has been. Kestrel One can't get over and no truck can climb it, so you build the biggest thing you have ever built, from a dead ship's propeller shaft and the rams off an airliner, and you give it a name, and you go through.

Partway in you hit steel. Somebody else tried this, long ago, and left a boring machine in the dark with a lunch tin on the seat and the name PRUDENCE on its side. Then you hit what stopped it. You get everyone out, you plug it, and you carry on. And one day there is a dot of light, and then there is rain on your windscreen, for the first time in the game.

The people of Highwater are very surprised, and very damp. Water is the one thing they have far too much of, and they will not stop complaining about it. You help them when their old pond nearly takes the valley. Then you run a pipe home through your own tunnel, turn it on, and every tap in town explodes, because that pond is 120 metres above you, and ten metres is one bar.

**What it is about.** All of the Wasteland is about water being worth everything. This is the place where it is worth nothing. A child who has played for dozens of hours in the dry should feel that first rain.

**It stands on its own.** It can happen any time after chapter 15, before or after Mrs Vane, the Mule or the sea. A line that needs one of those has a version for each case.

**Side job, after job 8: The Apple Run.** Carry a crate of apples from Highwater to your soup kitchen, through the tunnel, without bruising them.

**The work between (each a real site, and an apprentice can run it).** Parts for the machine at the Scrapyard (`docs/MACHINES.md`). The middle of the tunnel, which your apprentice bores while you do other things. Plugging the water with grout and laying a drain. A road down the Far Side. The pipe through the tunnel. Long play comes from how much there is to do, and nothing here asks anyone to wait.

**How far the tunnel really is.** The story tunnel is about 1,200 m. The map only has so much room, so the first 120 m or so is a real tube bored into the Ridge on the Scablands, and the last 120 m is a real tube on the Far Side. The middle is a dark stretch with ring lights where the game changes maps (FAR-REGION). Nobody should be able to see the join.

## 6. What the new pieces do

- **The Far Side** (FAR-REGION). A new region of new terrain, in a file of its own, added as data so more can follow. Valley, river, the Long Bottom's mud, Highwater, the mill, the Top Pond above it all and more of The Teeth around the edges. About 1 km by 1 km to start, with no fixed limit. `editable`, so machines work here. No town grid at first. Green, wet and dark, and it must look nothing like the Scablands.
- **Going through.** A car that drives into the tunnel's dark stretch with ring lights comes out on the other map, with its speed and damage carried over. Nobody should see the join. Before `far-side-open` the Scablands' ground is byte for byte as it is, apart from what the player has bored.
- **Rain** (FAR-RAIN). View only: rain on the windscreen, wet ground, puddles, a grey sky. It never changes a race rule or a replay on any released course. It's used for the Far Side, and nothing stops it being used later anywhere the story needs it. This is the first rain anyone has seen in the Wasteland, so it deserves care: it should begin as a single drop on the glass, and the sound should come in slowly.
- **Mud** (FAR-REGION). A ground material on this region that slows narrow tyres and spins them, and that wide tyres cross more easily. The Titan has the time of its life.
- **`bore`.** MACHINE-BORER's. This page only adds `sayAtMetres`, `apprentice`, `breakthrough` and `region`.
- **`apprentice-bore`.** The tunnel's middle: the rule in `docs/MACHINES.md` section 2, with the apprentice working at 15 m a day, as well as you showed it. The player goes and does other things. TOWN-PACE decides how the clock runs.
- **`heliceiling`.** Kestrel One's real flight with no load, climbing against her real falling push. The step ends when she can no longer gain height. With no pilot, `noPilot` sends Dune.
- **`loadLengthMetres`** on `haul`. A long load swings out behind on a turn and clips things it passes, and each hard hit counts toward `shedLimit`. It must feel funny, never unfair.
- **`tunnelStretch` and `tube`.** Places given as a share of a stretch of tube, because the tunnel's shape comes from how you bored it. PRUDENCE and her gallery are placed beside your tube, wherever it runs, at about 640 m.
- **`waterRises`.** View and a slow drag on the car in deep water. If the car stops, the step restarts from the last ring. It is never lost, and there is no clock, though it should feel urgent.
- **`trench`.** MACHINE-TRENCHER's flags, in the rain, with the pond's level shown as a picture. The level never goes over. It rises to just under the lip however slowly the child goes, and stops there. The fright is real and the failure is not. Say nothing about this on screen.
- **`fountains`.** Water spraying out of taps, pipes and the bathhouse roof in the town during this job, view only.
- **Goat.** Fourteen stands in Old Tam's yard from job 1 on, with a bell that rings.

**Sound.** Reuse banks first. New cues: `far.rain` (light to heavy), `far.mud`, `far.bore-steel` (a cutter on steel, and it should make your teeth hurt), `far.inrush`, `far.bell` (a goat bell).
