# Night 2: the old lines and the new, side by side

Claude, 19 November 2026, card STORY-DIALOGUE-PLAIN, for Kyle to read before the new lines go into the game.

Kyle found the lines of Cinder and Auntie Solder clipped and hard to follow, and did not understand the opening
("four embers, four nights"). Every spoken line, thought and choice of Night 2 is rewritten below as whole sentences
that a person would say out loud, and each line has one job: what is happening, what it costs, or who this person is.
Rell's lines were already the clearest, so they changed least. Nothing else about the night changed: every choice
still costs exactly what it did, and every choice leads where it did.

## The opening: spoken at the hearth

**My pick: Auntie Solder and Cinder say it at the hearth, in the game, with the count on the screen.** Two reasons.
Kyle asked for no breaks in the story (STORY-NIGHT-2-FLOW item 3), and a page before the 3D scene is a break. And the
painted plate trial of 18 November (STORY-PLATE-NIGHT-2) found that the game looks poor beside a painting, so an
illustrated opening would make the first 3D picture look worse.

The four facts are said in order, one to a line: the hearth burns one ember a night; the store holds four, so the town
has four nights of fire; Big Bess goes for gas at first light; twenty units of gas bake one new ember.

## What did not make sense, and what changed

Each item below contradicted something else in the night or in the design. The fixes are in the lines above and in
the design page (`docs/story/five-cold-nights.md`).

1. **Who has fire.** The Walkers came to Tin Town because they had none, so their camp has firelight only where they
   got an ember: the gift, the watch trade, Dace's stolen embers, or one carried out in the night. On a refusal the
   camp is dark, and a new thought on the tower says so, with the rider circling it (this agrees with
   STORY-NIGHT-2-FLOW item 6, which Codex is building).
2. **The count at the close.** The design said "Three embers" after a gift, but Auntie banks the hearth first, which
   burns tonight's ember, so two are left. Her closing line now says that the hearth is banked and how many nights are
   left. The Day 3 and Day 4 examples in the design were recounted to match.
3. **"Bess at first light" at the close.** After a lost fight at the Wash, Bess leaves late, so Auntie now says "in the
   morning".
4. **Walking for the Mall.** When the Walkers turned Clinker down, they always "walked for the Mall", even if nobody
   had told them about it. They now pull their carts away into the dark; they go to the Mall only if you told them.
   Rell, when told, now says the Walkers will rest at the Wash tonight and make for the Mall in the morning, which is
   why they are still at the Wash when Clinker comes.
5. **Auntie Solder at the gate.** She stays at the hearth all night, but her lines after the gift and in "Come inside"
   were written as if she stood at the gate. They now come over the radio, like her line at the gate.
6. **Taking Dace to Rell.** The player said "We're going to see Rell", but the scene happens at Tin Town's gate. The
   player now says "we're going to find Rell", and the design says Rell has come looking for Dace.
7. **Dace reaches the Wash.** The design put Rell at your gate at dawn, but the game shows her camp. The design now
   says you reach the camp too late and Rell meets you by her fire.
8. **The salt at dawn.** Nothing said where it came from. Dace now says Rell sent it to pay for the ember, which keeps
   Rell's promise to pay what she owes.
9. **Cinder on the fourth night.** "Nobody was on our fence" was untrue on the branch where Dace kept watch by the gate
   and fell asleep. Cinder now says nobody was keeping a proper watch.
10. **"The first hearth smoke on this road in a year."** Tin Town was only cold for eleven days. Rell now says it is the
    first hearth smoke the Walkers have seen since they set out.
11. **Rell lending an ember back** (Day 3 and Night 5 in the design). An ember burns one night, so the one she was
    given burned on the second night. Rell now says so and offers her people on the fence instead. Dot lends from the
    embers you gave her oven.

The plain-prose pass also covers Night 1, Day 3, Day 4 and Night 5 in the design page, where these were fixed too:
the Walkers' lantern and hot stones at the ford are lit at Tin Town's hearth (they have no fire of their own); Dot's
pie, given a moment after her oven is lit, is half baked and Auntie finishes it; the player counts "about a dozen"
people far off, not eleven; Auntie's "the man who threw ash in my soup" (Clinker never did) became the man who stood
on the wall pointing raiders at the lamps; speeds are in kilometres an hour, like every distance; and nobody calls
the player "he" or "a man".

**One question left open.** If the fight at the Wash is lost and no Ash Man reaches the camp, nothing says what the
Walkers do next. Claude's pick: they stay Cold and walk on at dawn. That needs a line on Day 3, not a change tonight.
