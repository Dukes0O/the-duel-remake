# The dry pump

A storyline of the open Scablands, written in full (card OPEN-WRITE-1, 26 November 2026). Its form is
`docs/story/open-scablands.md` section 21, and it follows that page's rules and `five-cold-nights.md` section 2: talk
bubbles, thought bubbles, and nothing shown that a person standing there would not know. Its data is
`src/story/threads/content/dry-pump.js`, which holds every line below exactly as it is written here.

Kind: a need. The choice at its heart, from section 19: spend the day's outing on somebody else's water.

## 2. When it can begin, and its sign

**It can begin** on any night from the second, once a camp is found whose people are Strangers or better, and the
player has banked a hearth since last visiting them. The director draws for it each night at an even chance. It begins
once.

**Its sign is a person's words** (section 18): a request, as the first row of the camp leader's talk table. What the
player meets on driving in is something seen and heard first: the camp's pump turning over a dry trough, with the cue
`open.pump.dry`. Nothing is marked on the map. The need stands for four nights, which is as long as the camp's water
lasts.

## 3. Its cast

- **The camp** is drawn from the found camps that qualify. Its lines are written once, with the leader's name put in,
  so the Glasshouse, Radio Hill and the Bus Ring can each be the camp. A few lines belong to the Glasshouse alone and
  are said only there.
- **A friend** is a people who owe the player a favour, and are not the camp's own. The role is empty when nobody owes.
- **The place is named, because the storyline is tied to it:** the Dead Mall.

**People in it.** Auntie Solder, Cinder and Rell, as they are. At the Glasshouse, Marigold and Finch, as section 21 has
them. The Ash Men, as two guards with lamps. New: **Mr Penny**, the night watchman of the Dead Mall, which he still
calls the Seaview Centre. He is about seventy, wears his cap, and has locked up every night for thirty years because
nobody has told him to stop. He is exact, polite and lonely, and he would give a stranger his last cup of water and
enter it in the book.

## 4. Where things stand, in this example

It is the third day of Five Cold Nights, at first light. The player found the Glasshouse on the second day and mended
its wind pump, so the growers are Friendly. The Walkers are Friendly and owe two nights' watch. The Ash Men are
Watching. There are two embers in the store and 45 scrap in the car. Bess is due to go for gas today. The player has
never taught anybody the gas run (section 23), so a day's outing spent on anything else is a day with no gas.

The scenes follow a player who drives out to the Glasshouse before the gas run, says yes, goes in by the roof, goes up
for the second valve and is not seen.

## 5. The scenes

### Scene 1. A pump that turns

The player drives in to the camp, out in the open, on any visit after a banked night. The pump's sails are turning in
the wind and its rod is going up and down, and the trough under its spout is dry. Two people are at a barrel with a
cup, filling a row of bottles one cupful at a time. The cue `open.pump.dry` is a pump clanking with nothing in it.
Within sixty metres, two thought bubbles come.

> *The pump is turning, but the trough under it is dry, and they're sharing out water from a barrel with a cup.*
>
> *Nobody shares out water with a cup unless they're counting how much is left.*

The leader walks out to meet the car, as the people of a Friendly camp do (section 17).

## 6. Losing, leaving it alone, and what it changes

### How you can lose

- **You spend the day.** In an act with a clock, tonight's ember burns with nothing baked to replace it.
- **You are seen in the hall.** The Ash Men's feud rises, they know Mr Penny's door, and the second valve is out of
  reach.
- **You are caught in the shoe shop.** You are put out with nothing, and the day is gone.
- **Your car is wrecked on the road out.** The valve goes back to the Ash Men.
- **You take the old man's water.** Nothing is lost at Tin Town or at the camp. He counts steps.
- **Your friends come back empty.** What they owed is spent, and so are two of the camp's four days.
- **You break the promise.** The camp thinks less of you, and its leader says why.
- **You say no.** On the fifth day the camp loads its cart. The site is on the map as "The Glasshouse (empty)". Mark:
  "The Glasshouse packed its cart and left, because its pump was dry." Some nights later any trader says it, at the
  gate or on a road.

  > **The trader:** I met a line of people on the road with a handcart, heading south for the pans. They said their
  > pump had failed. They said somebody with a car had been to see them, too, and I didn't like to ask who.

Nobody dies, nothing saved is wiped, and no screen says the game is over.

### What it costs never to follow the sign

If the player does not visit the camp in the four nights that the need stands, the three strongest of its people walk
to the Dead Mall themselves. Mr Penny gives them his own valve. They are nine days about it, and the leader says so on
the player's next visit. Nobody's word moves. Nothing is learnt about the Mall.

> **Marigold:** The pump failed a while back. Three of us walked to the Dead Mall for a valve in the end, and it cost
> us nine days and most of our feet. You weren't to know. I only wish that you had.

### What this changes later

- A camp that is Sworn sends a car when called, and speaks for the player to strangers (section 17).
- The way into the Dead Mall is the player's for good. Any later trip there may start at the tunnel door.
- Mr Penny is a person the land holds: a man who has kept a watch for thirty years and has asked to be relieved.
- The Ash Men at a higher feud come to fight more often, and in Five Cold Nights they come over the wall in greater
  numbers. With no feud written, being seen in the Mall raises it by one step all the same.
- An ember that was not baked is a night of fire that the town does not have.

**What the join with the town will add** (not built here; Kyle, 21 November 2026). A tower needs a keeper from the
ledger, and Mr Penny is one, once somebody can relieve him. The tank under the fountain is water that a site could
grow round.
