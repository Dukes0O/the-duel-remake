# RAIL-REGION

The short board entry controls current state, ownership, dependencies and locks.

The Whites, the salt storm and the branch's join, as section 6 says. A new region as data, in a file of its own, 17 m above the Scablands, reached by the branch and by road through the east mesa. The bank is 1 in 30, about 500 m. Whichever of this and FAR-REGION lands first builds the join between regions, in a file both can share. Before `branch-open`, the Scablands' ground is byte for byte as it is, and the branch is only a buried line and a heap.

**Checks:** `tools/test-rail-region.mjs`: the region loads headless, its bank's slope is right, the ground is unchanged on the Scablands and no released course's world signature moves; the storm changes the view only. A look sheet and one frame check on Performance at the storm's busiest view, because a player reaches it.
