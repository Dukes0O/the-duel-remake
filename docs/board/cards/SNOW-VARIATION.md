# SNOW-VARIATION

The short board entry controls current state, ownership, dependencies and locks.

Claude, 14 October night. `winterTerrainMaterial` fixes its variation
strength, so Summit's snowfields read as one flat blue. Add optional
`variationStrength` and `variationScale` on the course's snow-world options
(defaults keep today's look exactly), and set Summit to visibly break the
snow into wind-scoured lighter and darker patches at 200 to 800 m. Before and
after picture pair for Claude; only Summit's signature row may change.
