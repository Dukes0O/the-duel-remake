# BOWL-SHOW-3-LOOK

The short board entry controls current state, ownership, dependencies and locks.

Claude draws and listens. Design: [BOWL-SHOW-3](BOWL-SHOW-3.md). Needs BOWL-TOW and BOWL-MISSILE (their plain shapes are
what this card replaces). Gritty, never toy-like (`docs/ART_LINE.md`); code-drawn first.

## What to draw

- **The crowd ramp:** its exact place on the Crush Straight (seen from the floor, out of the racing line), dressed as the
  floor's other dirt ramps are, with tyre marks up it once used, and nothing that points at it. World signatures change
  only with this card's reviewed before-and-after picture.
- **Towed things in the crowd:** the scatter round a car, a bus and a knocked-out truck lying on the terraces; the cable
  drawn tight over the wall top.
- **The missile:** a roof tube that fits the show truck, the missile, a smoke trail that hangs for 2 seconds, the burst,
  the smoke on a hit truck.
- **The sight:** cross hairs, the 4 degree ring, the lock ring closing and turning red, the range and the load gauge,
  readable over the floor and the crowd.

## Sound

Choose and listen to every cue named on BOWL-TOW, BOWL-MISSILE and BOWL-REPLAY, so that no one plays a stand-in; the
seek beep and the lock tone can be told apart with eyes shut. One line on this card for each cue changed after listening.
