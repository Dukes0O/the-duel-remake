# LESSONS-MORE-ROADS

The short board entry controls current state, ownership, dependencies and locks.
Design: `docs/DRIVING_LESSONS.md` section 14 (all of it) and 14.13.

Kyle (27 October): the lessons have "lots to expand on". Claude decides
(28 October, night): more roads for the same four lessons, on courses players
own now, so each lesson has at least two places to practise.

- **Lesson 1 Braking, Ardennes Forest:** a section with the big stops (La
  Source, or the Les Combes and Bruxelles stretch); choose the stretch with the
  hardest brake from high speed.
- **Lesson 2 Racing line, Ardennes Forest:** Eau Rouge and Raidillon with the
  run onto Kemmel, or the fastest flowing stretch the course has.
- **Lesson 4 Snow, Summit Run:** one of its snow hairpins with the run into it
  (Glacier Hairpin, Devil's Elbow or Last Breath), on the course's snow.
- **Lesson 3 Gearbox:** only if a stretch suits it (a slow corner then a long
  climb); otherwise leave it.

Every new row meets 14.2 and 14.3: 30 to 75 s, 3 to 9 corners, a straight
start at the instructor's speed with at least 6 s before the first braking
point, its own road (no other row of the same lesson shares more than two
corners), and 14.12's room to beat him. The lesson board lets the player pick
the course as it does now. Names come from the course's own track-script
pieces. Tests: the every-car instructor suite covers the new rows (all 14
cars, Casual and Pro), plus test-drive-lesson-2/3/4 and the view suites; lane
tier and build. Show the briefing map of each new row in one picture.
