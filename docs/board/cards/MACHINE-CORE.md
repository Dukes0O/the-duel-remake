MACHINES section 9. `src/machines/machine.js`, `kinematics.js`, `tools.js`, `content/machines.js`, `demo.js`. A machine is data: drive kind, mass, joints, tools, control sets. Forward kinematics from joints to tool position. Tracks as differential drive; wheels through the same interface. Tools act on the ground through `ground-edit`: bucket, chain, blade, bed, chute, cutter, hook. Loose material: volume and material in a bucket, bed or hopper; tipping makes a heap or feeds a recipe; weight changes handling. `demo.js` records joint angles and drive at a low rate and measures speed, accuracy and spill. `tools/test-machines.mjs`: a scripted excavator cycle moves the right volume from ground to truck; a new machine works from data alone; identical inputs give identical results at 30, 60 and 144 frames a second.
