The short board entry controls current state, ownership, dependencies and locks.

Claude plays the whole strand on a memory-only save, from the first stop to the whistle, and fixes what does not work. The test: does the player sit in the cab during the whistle and want the old man to come down? If not, change the pacing and not the words. Then switch it on and release (rule 18).
