# WILD-HIT

Kyle, 19 November 2026: hit the animals beside the road, crush them in the monster truck, and leave a small heap with a
bit of red blood. Built the same day by a Claude session and merged behind the dev switch `wildlife-hits`. The design
is `docs/HELI_WILDLIFE.md` section 3.5.

A car whose middle comes within 2.4 m of an animal on the ground (the monster truck, 3.4 m) knocks it down: it is
thrown a little way on and lies on its side with a small matte red stain under it, flattened under the monster truck,
and stays for the race. It is drawing only: the car feels nothing and no race rule, replay or pay changes. The player's
own hits play `vehicle.crush`.
