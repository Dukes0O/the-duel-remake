# RAIL-PROPERTY

The short board entry controls current state, ownership, dependencies and locks.

`LOST_PROPERTY_JOBS`, listed on the Tin Town board and the map once `lost-property-open` is set, each signed for in Pim's book. Rewards as section 6 says: Quill's long division on the airliner, Dot's sign, Solder's gloves and the tin car. Where a person's line depends on a warlord you drove off and did not make peace with, the delivery still happens wherever that person now is. Anyone not in the world yet has the case wait on the shelf, and the list says so.

**Checks:** `tools/test-rail-property.mjs`: each delivery wins headless; `property-hollis` opens job 10 whatever else is done or not done; the Tin Car only opens after it; nothing is paid twice.
