# ARDENNES-STAVELOT-STONE

October 2026: "if Belgian, then Belgian look"). Today they read as pastel
American suburban houses with porches.

- Restyle the Stavelot village houses as Ardennes stone: grey-brown rubble
  or slate-grey stone walls, steep dark slate roofs, small deep windows, no
  porches; keep the church. Gritty and real, never toy-like.
- Same footprints, positions and counts, so the road, run-off and race do
  not change; only materials and house shapes. Not solid changes: none.
- Look rounds per SPEC 0.11 (at most three), one sheet per round under
  `docs/board/looks/ardennes/`, verdict on this card.
