# WATER-EASY

Part of The Water Road, `docs/story/wasteland-water-road.md`.

Prove packet section 4: on Easy the child-pace test driver wins each chapter's job and its warlord fight at least 8 times in 10, using only each warlord's own Easy settings, never the shared difficulty numbers.

## Claude answers

1 November 2026, after the measurement (`docs/changes/WATER-EASY.md`). The claim is not
met: a player who is never better than an Easy computer car wins 0 to 6 in 10 across the
15 cases. A stronger player using the game's own aiming help wins both races 10 in 10 but
still loses most arena games and fights. This is the live game's Easy arena balance, not
something the Water Road adds. The story's fights are the warlord ladder that is already
live, and a chapter's job is optional (a chapter is done when its warlord is beaten). So
the Water Road goes ahead unchanged: it makes nothing harder and gives a young player
clear next steps. The measuring tool merges, and the work moves to a new card,
EASY-YOUNG-DRIVER: an Easy that a young driver can win in arena games and warlord fights,
with these numbers as its baseline. That card settles whether the change is a story-only
Easy (Ambush Alley and the Convoy Raid first, which no player won) or Easy for every game.
