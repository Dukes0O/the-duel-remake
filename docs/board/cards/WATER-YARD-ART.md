# WATER-YARD-ART

Part of The Water Road, `docs/story/wasteland-water-road.md`.

A Blender recipe in `tools/blender/` for the camp's water works in the Scrapdome Yard: dry well, slack pipe, pump house, boiler, truck bay and a water tower with eight fill levels, each stage shown by chapter. Gritty, never toy-like; three-round cap; pictures to Kyle with a pick. Packet section 2 and 11.
