# WILD-ART-WASTE

The short board entry controls current state, ownership, dependencies and locks.

Part of the animals line (`docs/board/cards/WILD-ART-SRC.md` has the full history and rules). Real species, natural and gritty, never cartoon. Reuse the recipes, donors and sheet tools in `tools/blender/wildlife/`: most animals are adapted from the licensed Quaternius and Poly donors already there, and `art-library/` holds their licences. Do not download anything that needs an account. Each animal: metres, feet on the ground, natural colours and markings, an Idle and a Walk or Run clip (birds a flight loop), at most 5,000 triangles (birds 2,000), texture at most 1K. Runtime models go in `public/assets/wildlife/`, with credits in the credits file there. One sheet per round, JPG at most 500 KB, under `docs/board/looks/<card in lower case>/round-N.jpg` with `round-N-review.md` beside it. At most three rounds. Look at your own sheet and fix it before you submit: one submitted sheet is one round. An animal that cannot pass is left out and named in the change note, as the wildcat was. Not placed on any course here. `tools/test-blender-output.mjs` entry, `docs/changes/<card>.md` with a Removed section. No Chrome. No frame timing.

## Animals
Goat, chicken, scruffy camp dog, turkey vulture, big desert monitor lizard, scorpion (large enough to see at 10 m). The goat, chicken and dog live in the player's town and must look like working animals on a poor farm: thin, dusty, not pets. The dog can come from the wolf donor, the goat from the deer donor.
