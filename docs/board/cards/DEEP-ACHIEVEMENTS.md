# DEEP-ACHIEVEMENTS

The short board entry controls current state, ownership, dependencies and locks.

THE_DEEP 6.9. A small **Achievements** shelf in the garage beside the cup
trophy case. First set: Raised from the Deep, She runs again, Wreck racer
(finish any race in the Sea wreck finish), Coast Cup as found, Restored,
Coast Cup restored. Each earned one shows the date and a line about how;
unearned ones show their name and a gentle hint, never a spoiler. Earned
once per player from what the save already proves where possible (raised
parts, owned car and finishes, settled results), with a small optional
`achievements` list for the ones a result has to record (Wreck racer, the two
Coast Cup wins). Old saves load untouched and earn what they already show.
Saves: one independent review. Tests, lane tier, build.
