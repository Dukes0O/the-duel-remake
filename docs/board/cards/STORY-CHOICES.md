# STORY-CHOICES

Kyle's decision of 18 November 2026 (`docs/board/decisions.md`, "The story is rebuilt around choices"). Claude's card:
design only until Kyle has read it. Nothing is built from this card before that.

## What Kyle asked for

A story a player steers. Decisions that change what happens later. People who talk. Things that can be lost. Danger on
the way to anything worth having. He rejected the Everburn twice as a fixed route of tasks; a third fixed route, however
well finished, would be rejected again.

## What Claude owes on this card, in this order

1. **The form.** Which kind of game the story is modelled on, in one page, with the reason and what was weighed.
2. **The ledger.** What the game remembers about a player's choices (who was helped, who was refused, what is owed, what
   is scarce) and how a later scene reads it. Small enough that Codex can build it once and every chapter use it.
3. **The map of choices** for the first act: every decision, every branch, where branches join again, and what each one
   changes later. Drawn, not only written.
4. **One chapter worked in full:** every line of dialogue, every choice with its consequences, how the player can fail
   and what failing costs, and which driving or fighting each branch leads to.
5. **What is reused** from the Scablands, Tin Town, the forge, Big Bess, the Ash Men and the remade jobs, and what goes.

Kyle reads 1 to 4 before any card for Codex is written. After that each scene becomes a card with its own checks.

## Fixed points

- One keyboard, one player at a time; Kyle's and Gratian's own saves. Nothing wipes or hides saved progress.
- The driving and the fighting stay the action. Choices decide which action happens, against whom, and at what stakes.
- A choice shows its cost before it is made, and its consequence when it lands ("because you gave Rell the ember").
- Chance may decide an outcome, from the save's own seed, and the player is told it was chance.
- Every chapter can be lost, and the player can see how.

## The design, first draft (Claude, 18 November 2026)

`docs/story/five-cold-nights.md` holds items 1 to 5: the form (a hearth to keep, a road to run, people who remember),
the ledger, the first act's map of choices (five nights, four endings), the second night written in full with every
line and branch, and what is reused. It ends with three things for Kyle to rule on. **Waiting for Kyle to read it.**
Nothing is built from it yet. Still owed after his reading: the other four nights written in full, as the second is.
