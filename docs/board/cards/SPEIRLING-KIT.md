# SPEIRLING-KIT

The short board entry controls current state, ownership, dependencies and locks.

The Lab-built McMurtry Speirling needs its fitted Wasteland armour kit before
the `lab` switch goes on (LAB-FAN's review), as every other car has (the
Sirena's was SIRENA-KIT). Follow SIRENA-KIT's recipe and tests
(`docs/changes/SIRENA-KIT.md`): three armour tiers fitted clear of the wheels,
fans and canopy, built in Blender as a recipe, within the kit budget; one
comparison sheet under `docs/board/looks/speirling-kit/`. Lane tier, build,
the armour-kit visuals scenario.
