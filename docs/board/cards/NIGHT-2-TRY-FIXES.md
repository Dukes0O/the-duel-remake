2. **The direction arrows** in the markers ("The gate 49 m", "YOUR CAR 73 m") read as plain triangles. Draw a clear arrow

   ("Two riders got away"); a rider must never vanish in view.
4. **Long pauses** at story moments, above all getting into the car and arriving at the walkers' camp to chase off the
   Ashmen. Measure each pause and remove it, or show something during it.
