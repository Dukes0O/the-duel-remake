- **The five signs:** painted arrows on an old logging track; a painted
  start grid; a ring of camp-fire pits (lit, with smoke); a chalk start line
  on a harbour wall; an upward-facing lit lighthouse window. Flat and
  real, readable from 60 m up, nearly invisible from a car.
- **The Table:** a lone red-rock mesa with sheer sides and a flat top
  (about 400 m across), an old steel fire lookout tower, a 1950s single-engine
  bush plane wreck (faded paint, a readable tail number), a short dirt strip,
  and the logbook on the lookout's table.
- Reuse first: the desert rocks, the Wasteland's weathered metal, the fire
  and smoke effects. Recipe `tools/blender/pilot-map-props.py`; one sheet per
  round in `docs/board/looks/pilot-map/`, at most three rounds; Claude
  judges. No frame check until it is reachable (rule 9). Budget about 5 MB.
