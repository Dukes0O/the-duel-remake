## Builder decided (Claude to review)

- **Where a vehicle is named.** A job names its vehicle in `job.vehicle` (an id, or `{id, loadKg, tankL}` to start part full). `initializeWorldJob` keeps the running vehicle at `state.arena.worldJob.vehicle`, or `null` when the job names none or `wasteland-town` is off. It goes with the arena, so nothing carries into a later race. The hook in `src/game.js` is two reads: the `car` getter returns the vehicle's prepared `car`, and `_vehicleSpec` returns its `body` for the player. `game.js` imports nothing from `src/town/`.
- **Rows are data, machine-shaped.** `src/town/content/work-vehicles.js` (the design's rule that vehicles live under `src/town/content/`). Each row passes MACHINE-CORE's `defineMachine`. Masses, engines and tanks are 1950s work trucks, with the sources written on the file; wheelbases and bodies are measured from `trucks-1.glb`. The tanker holds 5,600 litres because its drum measures about 5,660.
- **Weight is physics, not a setting.** Pull is engine power over mass, divided by the arena's launch scale so the pull is the real one (0 to 30 mph: empty flatbed 7 s, fire truck 12 s, loaded flatbed 20 s). Stopping from 30 mph takes 12 to 18 m. Heavier is a little slower at the top and has a little less grip.
- **Turning circle.** The arena's steering is a car's and lets a truck turn on the spot. `workVehicleSteer` holds the steering to the truck's real circle (wheelbase over the tangent of 35 degrees at full lock), and the input is read so that several steps in one frame are one press.
- **The bell is B, and Space is kept for the tools.** Every machine works its tool with Space (the dump truck tips, the trencher cuts, the drill feeds), so the tanker's pump, the flatbed's bed and the tow rig's hook will want it too; the bell takes B now so that it never moves. B, once a camera key, sends `town-bell` in the car context and does nothing in any other car; each press rings once (cue `town.bell`, appended to the bank behind `wasteland-town`), sounding from the truck. Space fires no nitro in a truck: the nitro tank is held empty. H stays the fire truck's spray (TOWN-FIRE-JOB). The help screen does not list B yet; the fire job's own words should say it.
- **The picture.** `src/town/view/work-vehicle-view.js` draws the truck's part of `trucks-1.glb` in the garage car's pose and hides the car, through one line before drawing in `src/render3d.js`. The truck stands its tyres where the car's are, turns its front wheels with the steering and rolls all of them; the flatbed hides the supply truck's load, and the supply truck shows it only when loaded. It is drawn on its own layer, so the mirror's camera, which sits inside the cab, does not see it. If the model cannot load, the garage car is drawn rather than nothing. Seen in the game, the chase camera sat inside the truck's height, so a driven work vehicle takes the Titan's taller camera: one more term on the existing `tall` line in `render3d.js`.
- **Not on this card:** the truck's engine sound, and the dozer, ambulance and Vane's trucks (TOWN-ART-TRUCKS-2 has landed; the ambulance is one row, the dozer needs the tracks path).
