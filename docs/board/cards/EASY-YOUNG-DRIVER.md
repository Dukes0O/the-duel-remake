# EASY-YOUNG-DRIVER

From WATER-EASY (`docs/changes/WATER-EASY.md`, 1 November 2026). On Easy, a player who is
never better than an Easy computer car wins 0 to 6 in 10 across the Water Road's 15 jobs and
fights; a player using the game's own aiming help wins both races 10 in 10 but still loses most
arena games and fights, and nobody won Fuel Run, Ambush Alley or the Convoy Raid. Design an Easy
that a young driver can win at least 8 in 10 (`node tools/test-water-easy.mjs --rates`), starting
with Ambush Alley and the Convoy Raid. Settle first, in writing: a story-only Easy in the files
that own each job and fight, or Easy for every game. Keep these fun: Summit hillside driving and
monster truck big air (`docs/board/decisions.md`).
