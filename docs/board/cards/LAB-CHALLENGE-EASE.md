each challenge is passable by an 11-year-old without perfect play. Claude's
calls (thresholds and helpers are Claude's to set, LAB-FINISH):

   reset to 5 m, so three lengths are never recorded. Fix it; test it.
2. **Projectile (the water drop on the buoys).** Zero hits in 11 minutes of
   simple play. Keep the lesson (a drop keeps the helicopter's forward speed,
   so you release before you are over the target) but make it learnable:
   after each drop a splash marker shows where it landed against the buoy
   ring for a few seconds, and the buoy hit circle widens to about twice its
   radius. Measure with a sensible script (approach at a steady speed and
   height, release a little before the buoy, correct from the splash): it
   must pass inside about three minutes.
3. **Fire.** Not put out in simple play (the road reached at 150 s). Measure
   the number of scoop trips a steady pilot needs, then set the time allowed
   (or the fire's size) so a steady pilot passes with a minute to spare.
4. **Wax.** The lesson is choosing the right wax. Pass = finish the wax run
   on the right wax for the day's snow; missed gates add time only and do not
   fail it. Keep the medal and best-time rules for ordinary runs unchanged.

the reason. Then rerun the scripted player pass for all 16 lessons: all must
