before step 2 builds their rules, so that step 2 can place them. In the look of step 1's tin fence and gate
(`src/world/open/looks/home-ground-look.js`), gritty and worn, never toy-like, drawn in code first
(`memory/code-drawn-art-first`):

- **Two Tin Town Brown hens,** from the wildlife line's chicken (`src/wildlife-models.js`, `chicken.glb`): a real brown
  laying hen's colours, a hen's size (about 40 cm tall), pecking, scratching and walking, and sitting in a nest box.
  Each must read as a hen from 10 m and as one of his from 2 m (a small leg band in a colour he can change later).
- **A nest box made from an old crate:** stenciled slats, straw, room for one hen, and an egg that shows when laid.
- **The first coop, in tin:** the size of a garden shed (about 2 by 1.5 m, 1.8 m to the ridge), a small door with a
  ramp, a perch inside, a window with wire, rust at the foot. Level 1 is tin; timber bracing comes at level 2.
- **A brown egg** that can be carried and set down.

Checks: a look stand on the pattern of `tools/open-look-stand.js` and a `look-home-hens` scenario (the coop and nest box
on his ground by day and at dusk, a hen at 10 m and at 2 m, an egg in the box); a small suite that the named parts exist
and stay within their budgets; the rule 23 set and `npm run build`. One comparison sheet per round in
