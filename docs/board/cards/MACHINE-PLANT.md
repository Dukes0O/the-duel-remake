## Builder decided (Claude to review)

Settled in writing before code, on the pattern of MACHINE-DRILL and MACHINE-ROADS (Claude session for Kyle's push of 19 November 2026).

- **The split.** (1) The plant's rules live in `src/machines/plant.js` with their numbers in `src/machines/content/plant.js`. They take a place and a goods ledger and need no town. (2) The town's side: every stored kilogram (stone in the hopper, gravel and sand in piles and bins, cement in the silo) moves through TOWN-GOODS `storeGood` and `takeGood`. `tools/test-machine-plant.mjs` runs the same rules against a town made with `createTown`, and the stock survives a save round trip. No new saved field: stockpiles already exist, and one good, `cement`, is added to the goods list. (3) The yard: the plant stands in the machine yard behind `wasteland-town`, with a yard-only ledger kept for the page's lifetime as the road work is. Nothing reads or writes a save.
- **Water is not a good.** A town's water already has its own store (wells and pipes). The batch plant draws from its own tank in litres; joining that tank to a town's pipes belongs to the card that puts the machines into a town.
- **The chain, in kilograms.** Rock dug from a blasted-rock heap is tipped from the dump truck into the crusher's hopper. Its mass is the mass the truck carried (the machines' own density table). Rock and gravel go to the jaw; topsoil and clay are screened out by the grizzly and land on the ground beside the hopper. The jaw crushes 200 tonnes an hour onto a 20 m belt running at 1.5 m/s, and the gravel drops onto a pile at the belt's end. The loader scoops 2 m³ (3.2 t at 1,600 kg/m³ loose) from the gravel or the sand pile and tips it into the batch plant's bins. The plant is a central-mix plant: with the mixer truck backed under it, holding Space weighs and mixes 2 m³ a minute into the drum, in the mix below, and stops with a sentence naming whatever runs out. The truck carries up to 6 m³, its drum turning. At the forms, the chute swings with A and D, and holding Space reverses the drum and pours 2 m³ a minute. Concrete that misses a form, or overflows a full one, is spilled and counted.
- **The mix,** per cubic metre: 350 kg cement, 175 kg water (water to cement 0.5), 750 kg sand, 1,100 kg gravel, so 2,375 kg. This is a common general-purpose mix by mass.
- **Setting.** A form's concrete sets over 120 seconds from the last pour, and its surface goes from dark wet grey to pale dry grey as it does. Pouring into a form less than half set keeps it fresh. A form half set or more refuses more concrete, with a sentence.
- **Forms.** Two pads from `formwork-1`, drawn at half size across and 0.3 high: 3.18 m square inside and 0.28 m deep, so 2.83 m³ each, about two buckets of rock and one scoop each of gravel and sand.
- **Machines and keys.** The loader and the mixer truck are data rows; their parts are posed by the plant's own view. In the Q list they come after Mabel, so earlier scenarios that count presses to reach a machine still work. The loader drives with W, A, S and D; hold Space with the bucket against a pile to scoop and hold E to lift and tip. The mixer drives in drive mode; Tab gives work mode, where the truck stays still, A and D swing the chute and holding Space charges under the plant or pours anywhere else.
- **Sound cues** (own oscillator table in `plant-sounds.js`, through the game's mixer): `plant.crusher` (running), `plant.jaw` (rock in the jaw), `plant.belt`, `plant.gravel` (gravel landing on the pile), `plant.hopper` (rock tipped into the hopper), `plant.scoop`, `plant.bucket-tip`, `plant.batch`, `plant.drum`, `plant.pour`, `plant.set`, `plant.refuse`.
