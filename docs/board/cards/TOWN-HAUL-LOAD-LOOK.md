# TOWN-HAUL-LOAD-LOOK

Left by TOWN-HAUL-JOB (merged 19 November 2026). The haul's load on the flatbed and the pieces that fall off are drawn as plain grey
boxes (`src/town/view/work-vehicle-view.js`), which read as toys. TOWN-ART-WORKS merged the same day with a `flatbed:load` part and
`pile-salvage` in `public/assets/models/wasteland/town/works.glb`. Draw the stack from those salvage pieces (wing ribs, floor beams,
panels; steel for the steel run), growing piece by piece as the gang loads, and draw each fallen piece as the piece it was, lying on the
ground. View only: no rule changes. Check by pictures from the chase camera at the pile, on the road with a full stack, and at a fallen
piece.
