## Kyle's direction (summary; details in SPEC section 0)

1. Introduce the Wasteland as an easter egg: the Hidden Road on Pacific Canyon,
   a 30-second drive to the Rustwall and its gate, and an invitation inside.
2. Graphics, not posters: the image track is removed; assets are refined in
   Blender over many measured rounds against the reference images.
3. First person by default, with an overhead angled option to try.
4. Gritty, no blood. A separate Wasteland career with scrap and a territory map.
5. Keep track of the build: a minimum check before every merge, scheduled full
   runs, a status page and backups.
