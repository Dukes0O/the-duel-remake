# SKI-HELI: questions for Claude

I am carrying on with the recommended pick in each case. Say if any should change.

**1. The card's thin-air test is out of date; I test the corrected doc.** The card says a full group on a full tank "cannot hover at the summit pad" and with half a tank "can". `docs/HELI_SKIING.md` section 3 (corrected 24 October, SKI-RUNS question 2) says the opposite for the world as built: at the summit pad (3,586 m) the full group (475 kg) and a full tank weigh 2,105 kg against 2,217 kg of most thrust, so it hovers with 112 kg (5%) to spare; with the Lab's altitude tune 257 kg; with half a tank 312 kg. The test checks those numbers from the helicopter's own rules (`heliMass`, `availableThrust`) at each pad, nothing is tuned to bring the old lesson back, and the thin-air radio line plays when the collective is at its top and the helicopter still sinks above 3,500 m (which a heavy load, a damaged-air day or a climb with the group does reach). Recommended: keep.

**2. The group's weight rides on the flight state's existing mass override.** `heli.upgrades.emptyMassKg` (the Lab's light frame already uses it) gets +475 kg while the group is aboard, so `src/heli/heli-flight.js` is untouched, thrust and mass come from the same rules as everything else, and with the group not aboard (and with `heli-skiing` off) the state is exactly what it was.

**3. The group job uses the existing job save, with no new save field.** Taking it writes `jobs.taken = 'ski<day>'` through `takeJob`; delivering writes `jobs.done` and the pay through `finishJob` (1,200, Real 1,500), so it pays once a day and the board's "finish or drop the job you have" rule covers it. The group itself is not saved: a new visit with that job still taken finds the group waiting at the home pad again (the same rule as the customer's car). It is offered on one play day in three from the seeded generator (`makeRng`), not on the powder run (it has no guide to follow), and the group figures beside you are the guide's ghost with delays and side offsets. The group gets out at the drop pad (the weight comes off for the hop and the run) and boards again at the pickup, so the flight home is the heavy one. Paid when the helicopter sets down on the home pad with the group aboard after skiing.

**4. The hop to the pickup is not flown.** Stepping out freezes the helicopter where it is (no fuel is burnt, as the card asks); climbing in places a fresh helicopter on the ground at the pickup pad with the same fuel, group aboard again. The scene during a run is the ski zone, not the Summit world, so there is no flight to show. Stopping in the pickup circle for half a second ends the run; "restart from the drop" and "leave the run" (the helicopter fetches you) are free.

**5. Ceiling and pads.** The three pads already lie inside the Summit area's x and z (checked); only the height is short (the flat ceiling is 314 m, the summit pad is at 739.7 m). With a ski drop or the group job in hand the Summit area gets `aboveGround: 300` (the same rule the rescues use), for those flights only. Without `heli-skiing`, or with no ski flight in hand, `courseSite` and the area are what they were.

**6. The drop and the board.** `skiBoard()` (new, separate from `boardJobs()` so that stays as it is) offers "Heli-ski drop at Summit Run" (your own run, free) and, on a group day, the group job, at the home pad once the landing lesson is done and Summit Run is owned. Landing on a drop pad with one of them in hand offers the runs of that pad (`skiOffers()`): Devils Playground slalom, giant slalom and moguls; the pickup pad aerials (only when `aerialsOpen`); the summit pad powder.

**7. The Lab's wax.** With the Wax kit the pick of hard, medium or soft is judged against the day's snow (`skiWaxFor`, the day is the save's play day count). The Lab's wax challenge (beat the slalom's silver time with the right wax for the day's snow) is a slalom run started with `challenge: 'wax'` and a pick, allowed before the kit is bought (the kit is the reward); a finish inside silver with the right wax passes the Lab lesson. That needed `heli-skiing` added to the Lab flags in `src/lab/lab-heli.js` (`passLabChallenge` only knew `lab` and `helicopter`; the Snow lessons also need the skiing switch).

**8. Radio lines.** I emit `{type: 'radio', line: 'ski.radio.<name>'}` for SKI-AUDIO to record under those ids: `ski.radio.drop` (landed on a drop pad), `ski.radio.clear` ("Clear to ski", on stepping out), `ski.radio.pickup`, `ski.radio.group.call` (the job taken), `ski.radio.group.done` (paid), `ski.radio.fetch` (a run left: "No harm done, I'll come and get you") and `ski.radio.thin-air`. The cue `ski.step-out` is emitted on stepping out. None is registered or recorded here.

**9. What is left for SKI-VIEW.** `createHeliSession` takes `heliSkiingEnabled`; `src/app.js` passes it (and draws the board, the run menu, the zone and the result card) in SKI-VIEW. The session offers `skiBoard`, `takeSkiDrop`, `takeSkiGroup`, `dropSkiFlight`, `skiOffers`, `skiStepOut`, `restartSkiRun`, `leaveSkiRun`, and `view().ski` (only with the switch on).
