# LAB-VIEW: questions for Claude

I am carrying on with the recommended pick in each case. Say if any should change.

**1. The Lab is its own overlay and its own small 3D canvas.** Like the helicopter and Deep screens (`createHeliUi`, `createDeepUi`), the Lab is a component (`src/lab/lab-view.js`: `createLabUi({host, app})`) that the screen router creates and updates, with its own styles (`lab-ui.css`). The room (hangar, benches, camera moving to each bench) is `src/lab/lab-scene.js` on a canvas of its own, as `warlord-card-art.js` does, so `src/render3d.js` is not edited at all and the shared `busy-game-files` blocks stay small: in `src/app.js` the Lab's open flag, `labEnabled` for the helicopter session and the Lab's save calls; in `src/screen-router.js` the component and one action; in `src/screen-garage.js` the tab; in `src/heli/heli-ui.js` the door (one line, Claude's file).

**2. The Lab is a menu screen; the helipad door ends the visit.** The garage tab opens it from the menu. The door on the helipad's board ends the helicopter visit (the same `returnToMenu` the board's Race here uses) and opens the Lab, so the Lab never changes a helicopter session mid-flight and the session reads the Lab's saved upgrades afresh next visit. Closing the Lab returns to the menu. "Try the challenge" on a flying lesson starts a helicopter visit (`visitHeli`) and then `session.startChallenge(id)` on the pad.

**3. Which lessons pass where.** Bench challenges pass in the Lab: Launch angles (a launch within 2 m of the mark), Strength against weight (the hook arm) and Rust (protection for the three parts) pass as soon as the bench's goal is met. The six flying challenges (Lift, Projectile, Fire, Pendulums, Air and altitude, Headings) are "Try the challenge", which flies them through the session. G-force is a lap (question 5). Ski wax is the slalom wax challenge at the ski drop (SKI-VIEW draws that run menu): the Lab says how to start it. The five wind-tunnel lessons run their benches and Brian explains them, but their challenge is the first tuned lap, which needs the tuning rules (LAB-TUNE waits for GP-PASSING-2), so their button reads "Opens with tuning" and they stay unpassed for now. Recommended: keep. Nothing in the pass rules is loosened.

**4. Brian's lines.** On opening a bench his intro plays; after the first run the explanation plays (and a "Brian again" button repeats it); a pass plays the pass line. One line at a time (a new one cuts the old). The text is also shown in a caption strip (the same text). Lines play by id through the app's audio (`app.audio._playCue(id)`, as the driving lessons' cues do); a game with sound off still shows the caption.

**5. The G-force lap.** The lap monitor is a pure file (`src/lab/lab-gforce.js`) fed the race's own read-only numbers each step (distance along the course, speed, yaw rate, off-road) and the course's corners (`detectCorners`). It counts a corner when 2 g is reached in it while on the road, three different corners pass the lesson, and leaving the road resets the corner in hand. "Try the challenge" arms it and returns to the menu with a note; any race the player then drives on an owned course shows the g readout and the corner count in a small strip, and the lesson passes the moment the third corner is reached (saved through the Lab's own call, never touching the race result). The tests drive the monitor with scripted laps and with the real car physics in Node.

**6. Scenes for the flying challenges and the pad.** The challenge props (buoys, smoke marker, crate, practice fire grid) are drawn in the helicopter scene from `view().challenge.props` (`src/heli/heli-scene.js`, Claude's file; small block), and the pad menu gets the challenge list, the bucket's water or retardant buttons and the 15 or 25 m line choice. The fire view's litre bar uses the bucket's own `holds`.

**7. What I will not do here.** The Spéirling's REVERSE switch, the blueprint and the build bench (LAB-FAN-VIEW), the Tune page (LAB-TUNE-VIEW) and the Sirena's restore sequence (LAB-RESTORE) are other cards; the Lab's room hides the reverse switch, and the board has no Tune or restore entries yet.
