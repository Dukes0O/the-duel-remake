# OPEN-WRITE-4: questions for Claude

Claude (cloud), 26 November 2026. Each has my pick, and the page and the data file are written with it.

1. **When the Ash Men's turn can begin in Five Cold Nights.** The card says only after the raid on the fourth night,
   section 18 says the director begins nothing on the act's last night, and `burned-camp.js` asks for two nights before
   an act ends. Read together, the turn could never come in the act. Options: (a) let the draw at the banking after the
   raid begin it, with one night needed, and write what the fifth day can hold; (b) never in the act, so the parley has
   nothing to read. **My pick: (a).** The file's sign asks for one night. On the fifth day the camp and the scouting
   can be played, and a player taken that day is sent home on foot by Clinker without the car, so the Long Cold is
   never missed. The talk at the hearth waits for the first dusk after the act.
2. **The mark for the raid.** The Ash Men are cast only after the mark `ash-raid`, which nothing writes yet. **My
   pick:** the main story writes `ash-raid` on the fourth night when the Ash Men come over the wall. Another name is a
   one-word change in the file.
3. **"There's no pen here" on the false trail.** Section 21 has the player find no pen at the Grey Camp, but "The
   runaway" gives the Grey Camp a standing pen of ash banks. **My pick:** the pen is there and empty: "The pen is
   empty, and there's no water cart anywhere. Whoever burned the Glasshouse, it wasn't these people."
4. **The third choice in the pen.** Sump's offer is a toll on the Mall Road, which is the Road Rats' own. **My pick:**
   Clinker's offer is that Tin Town lights no lamps on one night of Old Smoke's choosing, a promise in place of two
   embers. In the act Old Smoke calls it at the parley for the Long Cold itself. It is a design number of mine, so it
   wants your review under rule 17.
5. **Tally's answer about a burned camp** (`stranded-hauler.js`, her gate) describes many cars and a cart's ruts, which
   is the Road Rats' turn. **My pick:** at the fold, an Ash Men line beside it under `when: {cast: 'enemy', is:
   'ash-men'}`: "I came through there the morning after, to sell them salt. I saw two heavy trucks going out one way,
   and footprints in a line between them, all grey with ash." I did not edit that file, which this card does not own.
6. **Copied choices.** Two choices are section 21's word for word (`raise-party`, `stay-inside`) and one is section
   21's with its words made general (`cannot`), so that this turn can be walked alone by `thread-crossings`. **My
   pick:** keep them until the fold, which keeps one of each. `notes.sameAsRoadRats` names them.
