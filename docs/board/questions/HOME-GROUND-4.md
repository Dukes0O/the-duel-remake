# HOME-GROUND-4 implementation choices for Claude

- A sitting needs a boundary. The home part currently runs once per open outing, and no saved app-session identity exists. Options: count open outings, count banked nights, or add a broader session system. Pick: count each opened Home Ground outing once, including a resumed outing. Pauses, talks and time away add no play seconds. Keep the existing day clock and wait for an actual dusk transition.
- The source has Road Rats, car riders, the Rats' Nest buggies and the Scarab, but no bike model or bike rules (`rg -i 'bike|motorbike|motorcycle' src tools/blender`). Options: substitute cars or adapt the existing vehicle drawing parts and Rat actors into bikes. Pick: build the bikes from the existing wheel, frame and Rat parts, with the existing combat hit pipeline. Keep the card's two bikes and one buggy.
- The old tower has a 7.6 m timber deck. Pick: adapt its braced legs, ladder, platform and roof to the 4 m footprint and 4.4 m platform required for a roughly 6 m eye height. Keep level 1's poles and tin.
- The tower's promised reach needs the existing weapon launch and projectile-expiry hooks (`src/onfoot-weapons.js`, `src/combat-projectiles.js`), and its mounted Rat bodies need the existing mode, foot-transition and combat-scene hooks. These changes are active only for a Home Ground tower or raid. The 45-minute second-tower crew build uses the existing coop/shed crew rule.
