# LAB-SNOW: questions for Claude

I am carrying on with the recommended pick in each case. Say if any should change.

**1. What "the right wax glides 3% faster" is true of.** I measured the ski rules (the right wax is friction 0.045, the wrong 0.06, no wax at all is the plain snow's 0.05). On the real slalom line (the guide's best line, `guideRun`) the right wax is 2.7% faster than the wrong one and 0.9% faster than no wax; the wrong wax is 1.8% slower than no wax. On the bench's straight 100 m glide at 10 degrees the figures are 5.9% (right against wrong) and 1.9% (right against none). So "3% faster" is true on a slalom run, but only against the wrong wax. Recommended: change the Wax kit line in `docs/LAB.md` 4.3 to "the right one glides about 3% faster than the wrong one on a slalom run, and about 1% faster than no wax", and let `tools/test-lab-snow.mjs` check the sentence against the numbers, as LAB-BENCHES did for its explanations. Nothing is tuned: 0.045 and 0.06 are the ski rules' own.

**2. What the free Aerials reward opens.** The card says "the aerials tips", LAB.md 4.3 says "the aerials kicker run in heli-skiing". Recommended: both. With the Lab on, the Aerials run is offered only once `lab.owned.aerials` is true (it is granted free when Launch angles is passed); with the Lab off it is offered like any other run. The tips are a few lines built from the ski rules' own numbers (the air, form and landing shares, the 40 degree landing window, the 30 degree finish of a spin, the zone's height and distance targets), so they cannot drift from the rules. The ski session (SKI-HELI) and its screens (SKI-VIEW) do not exist yet, so I build the pure rules (`aerialsOpen`, `aerialsTips`) and put one line each on the SKI-HELI and SKI-VIEW cards.

**3. The wax pick and the day's snow.** With the Wax kit, the pick before a run is one of hard, medium or soft; the right one is set by the day's snow: dry powder (-15 C) hard, cold snow (-5 C) medium, wet snow (0 C) soft, which is the mapping the bench already teaches. The day's snow comes from the play day through the seeded generator: a temperature drawn for the day, with the band it falls in giving the snow. The pick is not saved (the card says "before each run"). With no kit, or with the Lab off, no wax is set and the skier is exactly today's plain snow. The rules are `waxFor(profile, flags, day, pick)` for `createSkier({wax})`, `snowOfDay(day)` and `waxRunResult(...)` for the challenge (beat the slalom's silver time with the right wax for the day's snow). Lines go on the SKI-HELI card for the session to call them.

**4. The launcher's landing sound.** The card reuses `lab.splash`; the launch bench lists it as its cue.
