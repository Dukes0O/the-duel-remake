# STORY-OPEN-WORLD: five things for Kyle to rule on

Claude, 21 November 2026. The design is `docs/story/open-scablands.md`, sections 10 to 22. Work carries on with each
recommended pick until Kyle says otherwise. None of these holds the first slice (people who walk about, and the forge
shed with a repair and a wash).

1. **Should the big map draw ground the player has never driven paler?** Kyle approved the map as it is, with the
   whole land drawn. Section 16 proposes that driven ground is drawn in full and the rest paler, with its hills still
   showing, so that exploring leaves a trace. The other way is to leave the map as it is and mark only found places.
   **Recommended: paler, never dark.** It gives a reason to drive somewhere new and hides nothing a driver needs.

2. **May the player switch the skirmishes off by hand?** A mission switches them off by itself (section 15). The
   question is whether the pause menu should also have a line for it. **Recommended: no.** Danger felt in play is
   Kyle's own direction for the land, a player always chooses whether to attack cars that only pass, and any
   encounter can be outrun on a road. If Gratian finds the roads too busy, the cure is one number in a table.

3. **May a ransom take the player's own scrap?** When the driver is taken, section 21 pays the ransom from the scrap
   the player is carrying first, then from the town's till, and in embers only if both are short. The car always
   comes back, and nothing in the garage is touched. The other way is to cap it at half of what the player has.
   **Recommended: as written, with no cap.** Kyle's finding on the Everburn was that nothing could be lost; scrap can
   be earned back, and a loss that costs nothing is not felt.

4. **Should hidden places stand in the same spot for Kyle and for Gratian?** Section 16 draws each site's position
   from the save's seed, so the two players' lands differ and each has to find a camp for himself. The other way is
   one fixed position for everybody, so that one player can tell the other where a camp is.
   **Recommended: they differ.** The two play on one laptop and one can watch the other, so a fixed position would be
   found once for both.

5. **Which comes first: the open land, or the join with the town building?** Section 20 joins the story to the town
   (wells found and drilled, fences and towers, camps that grow into outposts and towns). The town's switch is off,
   and Kyle put the town on hold on 20 November until Show Night is out. **Recommended: the open land first** (the
   eleven cards of section 22), then Kyle's try of it, then the join as its own cards, with the thread "Water under
   the grey" as the first thing played across both.

Not questions, but worth knowing. Sump is new, as the leader of the Road Rats, and he can be made peace with in the
way Old Smoke can. Marigold, Finch, and Hetty and Bern of Radio Hill are new. Every other person in the design is
somebody the Wasteland already has.
