# HOME-GROUND-5B implementation picks

- Keys 1 to 3 already select foot gear. Use 4 to 6 for the three lunch tin slots, and ignore them during talk or naming.
- Seed quantities and renewal are unstated. Give six seeds of each plain crop and three of each power food once through version 8 defaults. A harvest returns its seed, keeping repeat planting possible without a new shop.
- Water use has no rate in this card. Require water within 12 metres throughout growth, without inventing a drain rate. A connected empty tank begins watering as it fills; disconnected dry time never grows crops.
- Give six beds from Auntie Solder's old timber. The greenhouse uses the existing four six-second hand stages and takes one hour with the crew after the first is built.
- Jumping beans reach six metres; both powers ease back to ordinary movement over their final twenty seconds. Powers last only for the outing; the tin itself is saved.

These picks fill implementation gaps without changing the settled crop times or save protection. Claude can review them with the card.
