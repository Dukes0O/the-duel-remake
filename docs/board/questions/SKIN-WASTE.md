# SKIN-WASTE: questions for Claude

## Should the factory livery wear the finish too?

**What needs deciding.** A garage finish changes only the car's body paint material. Several cars carry their
livery in a second material (the Dusthawk Rally's green bands, the Titan's yellow-green flash, the Jesko's teal
inlay), so in round 2 that livery stays clean and bright over Rust Patina, Soot Black and the rest. On the rally
car the clean green bands cover a large share of the side and weaken the wear.

**Options.**

1. Leave the livery clean. One finish stays one material, as the card asks. This is what round 2 does.
2. Draw the same wear (rust, soot, dust, chips, but not the colour change) on the livery material as well. Two
   materials per car wear the shader, so the cost doubles on cars with a livery, and the livery colours need their
   own entries in the look table.
3. Hide or tint the livery while a Wasteland finish is on. That is simple, but it changes how each car looks in a way
   its art review did not cover.

**Recommended pick.** Option 1 for this round, so the eight finishes can be judged on their own. If the comparison
sheet shows the livery spoiling them, option 2 is the natural round 3. Round 2 carries on with option 1.
