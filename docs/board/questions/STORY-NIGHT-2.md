# STORY-NIGHT-2 — Wash camp placement

Tin Town and the named Wash river crossing are about 4 km apart in the current Scablands. Section 4 calls for a camp reachable on foot in about 90 seconds, a 600 m pot run and a view from the gate tower. Use a temporary Walkers' camp on the Tin Town approach to the Wash for this chapter, leaving the named crossing and other jobs where they are. Moving the river crossing, or sending the chapter all the way there, would break one of those requirements. Please check the camp placement in the hand play.
