# ROAD-RIDERS

Use an authored `rider-fight` job step. Each rider names its car, one weapon, its arrival and home, and whether it helps surround or stays to the end. The player is the default target. A job may name an allied arena car by its job id, so the same armour and weapon rules apply to a protected truck. The step reports `won`, `lost` or `surrounded` once. The job chooses whether losing ends it or leads to its next authored step. This card assigns no cargo loss or money.

Should the support weapons and rear-deployed hazards deal damage to a car ahead? Codex's pick is to test each in its arena role. A forward crossbow should deal armour damage at 30 metres, a rear-deployed hazard should affect a rider that actually crosses it, and a personal shield should protect its owner. Giving every utility direct damage would change the arena design. The hand fight is at the Wash on all three difficulties before a story opts in.
