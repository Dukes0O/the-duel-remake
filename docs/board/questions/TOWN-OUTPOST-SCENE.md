# TOWN-OUTPOST-SCENE: questions for Claude

Written by the cloud session on 19 November 2026. The work carried on with each pick below; reverse any of them in the card's
"Claude answers".

1. **Where the outposts are solid.** Options: (a) in the land's own solids for every Scablands job; (b) in a town drive only, from the
   drive's layout. **Picked (b):** they are town things, the town drive is the only drive that carries a town, and (a) would add solids to
   every Everburn job and move their replays. A later card that sends an Everburn job past Dot's can pass the layout to that job too.
2. **When an outpost stands.** Options: (a) from the start of a town, before you have met its neighbour; (b) only once its chapter has
   opened. **Picked (a):** Dot, Miss Quill, Tam, Imre and Pim live out there whether you have met them or not, and a building that
   appears from nothing when a chapter opens looks wrong. The chapters still say when you are asked to go.
3. **Where each one is.** None stands on its place's own ground (the card's rule), so each is just outside it: Dot's Diner on the
   Boneyard Road 820 m short of the Boneyard; the school at the Boneyard's south edge, 130 m from its middle; Old Tam's hut on the
   Ridge's flat top, 100 m from the Ridge's camp; Deck Town 130 m from where the Ships in the Sand lie; the Rail Halt beside the Iron Line
   170 m north of its station, its platform to the rail. SHIPS-SCENE may want to put Deck Town on a ship; it can move the row.
4. **The town's own building rules.** The town region's reserved shapes do not cover the outposts, so a player could one day plan a
   building on Dot's porch. **Picked:** leave the sim alone here (this card changes no rules) and let TOWN-WORLD's owner add the outposts
   to the reserved shapes in a card of its own.
5. **People.** Drawn from the story cast (`src/world/jobs/story-people.js`), view only, never solid (the land's rule for people). They step
   aside when a car comes within 9 m and go back once it is 22 m away. Bo is the cast's child figure, `wim`.
6. **Sound.** The scene adds no game event, so no new cue. A later card that rings Deck Town's bell would use `town.bell`.
