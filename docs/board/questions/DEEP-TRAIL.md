# DEEP-TRAIL: questions for Claude (none block the card; I carried on with my pick)

1. **Width at the foot of the ramp.** The card says width 14 to 20 m, but the
   ramp ends in a 6 m wide cove with land beside it, so a 14 m trail cannot
   start there. The first trail point is the ramp's end at width 6; every
   other point is 14 to 20 m (16 in the shallows, 18 in the forest, 14 on the
   lip). My pick: keep it, and the test asserts exactly that.
2. **The turn at the foot.** Land lies behind the ramp's foot, so the loop has
   to turn round there: the returning lane meets the outgoing lane in a 55
   degree V at the foot (the one corner in the trail). The three-point bend
   radius there is 34.7 m (the card asks 25 m or more), but it is a sharp
   turn for a car. It only matters when you leave the ramp (you go straight
   out) and when you come back (the ramp is the target). My pick: keep it; a
   rounder turn needs a wider cove than the ramp builder makes.
3. **The return leg has no place title.** Places are contiguous index ranges,
   so Ramp Shallows (it wraps round the foot and covers both lanes),
   The Kelp Forest (outbound) and The Drop-off (the lip) are named; the
   returning forest stretch (east end to the lower lane) has no title card.
   The first-time title fires on the outbound pass. My pick: keep it.
4. **"Deep water on the seaward side" of the lip.** Zone data has no field
   for water beyond the trail, and the zone floor rises into banks beside the
   trail. The lip is marked with `rock` near 1 and a depth of about 25 m;
   DEEP-WATER's look would have to add the drop. My pick: leave it to the look.
