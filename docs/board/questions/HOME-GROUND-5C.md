# HOME-GROUND-5C: interpreting the raid sample

The twenty-seed ground sample wins 12/20 without food and 12/20 with each of super spinach, night carrots, iron beets
and stink garlic. It keeps the same stationary defender and three rockets at 8, 11 and 14 seconds. Spinach never gets
within shove range, the script does not use the extra sight, and step 4's home hit bodies do not shoot at the defender
(`nextShotAt: Infinity`). This sample proves the six-in-ten baseline and no certain win. It does not prove that using
food well raises the win rate. Separate behavior checks prove the shove, sight, damage reduction and exclusion radius.

Options: keep step 4's fight rules and have Claude judge food use in the planned hand drive; or give home raiders new
attacks and design a different balance protocol. My pick is to keep the settled raid rules and the comparable sample.
I have not added attacks or artificial damage bonuses to make a measurement improve. Claude's hand drive should check
whether each food makes the fight easier in practice before this step goes live.
