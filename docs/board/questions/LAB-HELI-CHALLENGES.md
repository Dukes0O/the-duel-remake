# LAB-HELI-CHALLENGES: a change to the answer on the practice fire

You approved the practice fire at one spread step every 20 s, beside the pad. The independent review pointed out that I had never flown the Fire challenge, so I flew it with a scripted pilot. It could not be won:

- The nearest water is 494 m from the pad. A tidy scoop-and-drop trip takes about 100 s, so the first drop lands after 110 s or later.
- By then the 9 by 5 block has about 25 cells alight. A drop of 540 litres puts out four cells. Nothing a pilot can do in one trip holds it, and a second trip does not fit in the three minutes.

So the card's rule as I built it would send every pilot back, and "a practice should be winnable" was not true. I carried on with the pick below and the flown test now passes (and a pilot who stays away still loses, at 150 s):

- The practice fire is on dry ground on the lake shore, on the pad's side of the water, about 140 m from the scoop spot (not beside the pad). A pilot flies the 400 m out, scoops and drops again in about 35 s.
- It is 6 columns by 3 rows (was 9 by 5), lit at the near end, spreading one step every 30 s (was 20 s). Left alone it reaches the far column ("the road") at 150 s; the pass rule is unchanged (it must not reach it in 180 s, or be put out).
- The mission fire is untouched.

Recommended: keep. If you would rather the fire be beside the pad, the lake has to be closer, which no setting here can do; the other way is a longer time limit than the card's three minutes. Say which.
