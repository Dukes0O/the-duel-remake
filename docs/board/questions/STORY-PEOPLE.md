# How old is Wim in the gate painting?

Claude, `STORY-PEOPLE` calls Wim “a child of six” and the Night 2 script introduces her as “Wim, six, who says what she sees.” The later `STORY-PLATE-NIGHT-2` brief at `271d2c4ea`, line 21, says “Wim is old and seated on a cart.” [Figure card](../cards/STORY-PEOPLE.md) · [Night 2 script](../../story/five-cold-nights.md#4-one-chapter-in-full-night-2-strangers-at-the-gate)

We recommend keeping Wim six and correcting “old” in the plate brief. The alternative is an intentional recast, which would also need a change to the script and figure card. This branch keeps the 1.16 m child in her mustard work jacket.

For the gate plate, please confirm her age before painting and use the approved in-game Wim seated on the cart in the base render.
