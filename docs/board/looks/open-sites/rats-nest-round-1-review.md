| What | Verdict | Why |

| The cutting | Passes | From the rim it reads at once as a hole cut in red earth, with beds in the wall and a dark seam of old scrap. The scale is right for looking down into |
| The pen | Passes | The ring of wrecks, some stacked, with the tarpaulin and the gate, is the best thing here. From `rim-north` a player will know it is a pen before any thought says so |
| The drain | Passes | A concrete pipe mouth with a bent grating, plainly tall enough to walk into |
| The yard at night | Passes | Firelight on Sump's shack and the far wall, and dark between. It is the first picture in this land with a mood |
| The smoke | Does not pass | Each column is one dark flat sheet, seen as a curtain from the rim and as a bar against the night sky. Smoke from tyres rolls and thins. Cross two or three sheets, break the edges, and let it pale as it rises. From 1.2 km the three thin sticks do read as a tell, and should be a little thicker |
| The yard by day | Does not pass | Fourteen people live here and the floor is bare. It needs what living leaves: paths worn pale between the shacks, the fires and the pen; scrap sorted into heaps by kind, because sorting scrap is what the captives are for; drums, a line of washing, a table. The red blotches on the floor read as paint |
| The water cart | Does not pass | From `rim-middle` it is a small pale thing in the middle distance, and the glass on it cannot be seen. | The wrecks close up | Pass from the rim, not close | From above they are cars. Beside the drain one is a box with stripes. Round 2 need only mend the ones a player walks past: by the drain and at the pen's gate |
| The tyre tracks | Do not pass | They are drawn as the rungs of a ladder. Two unbroken ruts |
