# TOWN-ART-WORKS Old Ground, round 1 (Claude, 19 November 2026)

**Verdict: passes.** A small burial ground, 21.1 m square inside its 3 by 3 plot, 5,384
triangles of 9,000. Dignified and not gloomy: warm buff stone, silver-grey wood, earth mounds
ringed with pale stones, a lit lantern at the gate.

- **Wall:** a sound dry-stone wall 0.9 m high, traced from its photo; each length starts at a
  different place in the photo, so the courses do not repeat.
- **Gateway:** posts, pegged lintel and the hanging lantern traced from the gate photo, with
  `old-ground:lamp` glowing inside the lantern.
- **Graves:** `old-ground:marker-1` to `-12`, each a whole grave (mound, stone ring, marker)
  with its origin at the grave's head, so the game shows as many as the town has buried. The
  first four use all four marker shapes from the photo; none carries writing or a symbol.
- **The dead tree** stands in the far corner with a plank bench in its shade
  (`old-ground:tree`, `old-ground:bench`). It is three cut-outs of the photo's own tree, so from
  the side and from part-way round it shows the photo's twisted trunk, boughs and twigs.

Fixed before this round was counted: the graves first wore a metre of studio floor (their
picture function added the grave's centre twice); the tree was built twice as round boughs
along the photo's limbs, which smeared its grain into bands and pale streaks both times, so the
method was changed to cut-outs. A swept sand path was removed: it read as a strip of paper.

Short of the brief: straight down, the tree is a star of three thin planes; the ground inside
the wall is the town's own ground, with nothing of its own; the bench is small for the space.
