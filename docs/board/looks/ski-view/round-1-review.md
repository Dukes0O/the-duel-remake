# Heli-skiing in the game, round 1 (SKI-VIEW, 25 October 2026)

Sheet: `round-1.jpg` (309 KB; removed after the verdict). It showed eight moments from the browser
scenario `ski-view`: the real game on the private QA page, with a
memory-only save, High quality and a software graphics device.

How the run was made:

- The drop was taken on the panel's ski box.
- A scripted pilot flew the helicopter to Devils Playground through the
  session's own step.
- The slalom was chosen on the run menu.
- A scripted skier (the session tests' driver) skied it in the game's own
  loop. It ran 0:19.20 with every gate clean, for gold and 500 credits
  through the game's own save.

The frames:

1. **Home pad.** The HELI-SKIING box above the routes: the free drop, and
   (a group day) the group job with its pay.
2. **Devils Playground.** The run menu: the day's snow ("Cool snow, -5 °C")
   and the three runs from this pad. This career has no Wax kit, so there
   is no wax pick.
3. **Top of the slalom, looking down the run.** This is the frame for the
   backdrop seam.
   - The zone's snow runs to its brow.
   - Past it is the Summit world, captured once into a cube from 40 m above
     the pad and turned to the pad's real valley (305 degrees): the green
     valley, the road and its trees, and the ridges.
   - Also in frame: the start arch, the netting down both edges, the orange
     drop circle and the loading card.
4. **Chase camera.** Gates 2 of 50, the poles of the next gates and the
   netting.
5. **Low chase (C).**
6. **Side view (C).**
   - The netting follows the snow, and the poles stand on it.
   - The guide's ghost is the pale figure behind the skier.
   - Behind it, the backdrop's nearby mountain shows as a streaky white
     cliff. That is the Summit world's own near terrain, seen from the
     backdrop's eye.
7. **Result card at the finish.** The time, "Every gate clean", GOLD, "500
   credits · new best", and the green pickup circle ahead.
8. **Back in the helicopter at the pickup pad.**
   - The result card stays for 8 s after the pickup. On this slow machine
     the 8 s had run out before the frame (an earlier run shows it over the
     panel).
   - The pickup's own run menu now offers Aerials.
