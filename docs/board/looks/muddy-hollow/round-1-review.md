# Muddy Hollow art, round 1 (26 September 2026)

Sheet: `round-1.jpg` (High quality: overview, ridge, pits,
jumps, log ramp, rock garden). Sources: Kyle's picks, Brown Mud 02 (Poly
Haven, CC0) and the Quaternius Ultimate Nature Pack (CC0); details and
checksums in `tools/art/catalog.json`.

Verdict: accepted by Claude as design lead.

- The Hollow ground now uses the course terrain material, tint and texture
  scale, so it matches the ground around it. Before, its colours were
  brightened twice and it read as a pale mint slab.
- The three mud pits read as dark wet mud (tinted, slightly glossy).
- The pond draws the whole area the Titan splashes through: level over the
  deep middle, a thin sheet over the shallows. Before, only a 13 m patch was
  drawn inside a wide dark ring that the car treated as water.
- The seven garden rocks are drawn exactly over their collision boxes. Before,
  they were solid but invisible.
- The log ramp is twelve logs laid side by side up the rising face, bedded
  into the ground, one draw call.

Open: from straight above the pond's shallow margin still reads darker than
the grass; acceptable as a muddy shore. The Hollow sits beside the High
Country lake, so its outer rim falls to the water; that is the settled layout.
