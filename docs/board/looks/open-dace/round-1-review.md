## Not right yet

- **The Walker stones from the car.** The card asks that a player can spot them at 40 m. They can be found, and they
  do not catch the eye. A fourth, paler capstone or a taller stack would help more than colour; the stones are already as
  pale as stone goes before it reads as plaster.
- **The lamps' light is judged at dusk only.** It multiplies what it falls on, so by day it would show as a pale patch
  ahead of the truck. The storyline lights the lamps only while the pickup drives off.
- **Rocks and sand read smooth.** The rocks under the Crow's Step and the drift at the Deep Sign are rounded lumps with
  grain. They pass at the distance of the pictures and would look soft from two metres.
- **The pickup close up is a shape of flat panels.** From five metres it holds; the cab and wings have no curve in
  plan, the tyres have no tread on the sidewall, and the glass is plain. A photographed surface on the doors and the
  bonnet would be the next step if the truck is to be looked at closely for long.
- **The Hangar's inside is dark against the sky** from the open end, so its remaining sheets read as dark patches.
  That is how such a roof looks from inside; a paler floor or light through the gaps would soften it.
- **Nothing was judged in play.** I took the pictures on the look stand, in Light the Town's daylight and dusk. In the
  storyline the pickup has people standing at it (Dace sits on the cover at the spot), and I have not seen them there.
