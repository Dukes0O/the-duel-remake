## What changed

- The stones went from 1.3 m to 2 m, with a fourth, flat, dark stone across the top and a small marker on it.
- The lamps are lit at dusk and in the dark whenever Dace is with the pickup: as he drives off, and while he waits with it
  by the road. By day they stay dark. Their light is laid on the ground once for each place the truck stands.

## Not right yet

- The stack is found by its outline, and the picture has an empty skyline. Against a hill or among boulders it would be
  harder to find; I have not tried that.
- From a car the chase view is higher than the 1.2 m of this picture, and the stack may then fall below the skyline. The
  dark top against pale dust should still show, and I have not seen it.
- Everything in round 1's "Not right yet" that is not about the stones still stands.
