# First look at the photographed Walkers

The matched gate frames use the existing procedural on-foot fallback and the new cast in the same eleven places. Rell stands 20 m from a 1.62 m eye with a 72-degree vertical field of view. The smaller strips retain the original pixels. All frames came from the exported meshes posed by Three.js and rendered offline. [Comparison sheet](round-1.jpg)

The original photographic approach gave Rell a solid piece of the studio floor between her boots. It put pale studio stripes along Wim’s limbs and painted a second face on Dace’s cheek in profile. Their different ages, clothes and heights survived, but those surface failures persisted at talking distance. The visual review called for separate boot and sleeve outlines and one continuous texture around each head.
