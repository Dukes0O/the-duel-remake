# Second look at the photographed Walkers

Rell’s boots now stand apart, Wim’s hair and sleeves have clean edges and Dace shows one face as he runs. Each articulated limb follows its measured photographic outline. The shared atlas wraps a single face from the front, around the temples and into the back of the head. [Comparison sheet](round-2.jpg)

The separate coat tubes made Rell’s long coat read as wide trousers. The carters’ newly shaped hands also missed their original fixed grips by as much as 14 cm on the tallest walker. The next pass called for one flared coat, shafts fitted to each wearer and physical supports for the resting poses.
