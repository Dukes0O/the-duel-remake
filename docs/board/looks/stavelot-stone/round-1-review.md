# Stavelot in Ardennes stone, round 1 (Claude, 15 October 2026)

Card ARDENNES-STAVELOT-STONE. Sheet `round-1.jpg`: today's village on the
left, round 1 on the right (from the road at Stavelot, a village street at
eye height, close by the church).

Rubble walls, steep dark slate roofs, no porches. The stone ran the wrong way
on some walls and read as planks, the stones were too even and beige, and
the window surrounds were solid blocks that looked like pale shutters; a
light beige band ran under each house. Verdict: another round.
