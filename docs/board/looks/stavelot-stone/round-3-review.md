# Stavelot in Ardennes stone, round 3 (Claude, 15 October 2026)

Card ARDENNES-STAVELOT-STONE. Kyle: "if Belgian, then Belgian look". Sheets
`round-1.jpg` to `round-3.jpg`: today's village on the left,
the round on the right, from the road at Stavelot, down a village street at
eye height, and close by the church. Captured by
`tools/scenarios/stavelot-stone.mjs` (High, memory-only saves, no race).

- Round 1: rubble walls, steep dark slate roofs, no porches. The stone ran
  the wrong way on some walls and read as planks, the stones were too even
  and beige, and the window surrounds were solid blocks that looked like
  pale shutters; a light beige band ran under each house.
- Round 2: stones laid along each wall, darker and less even, with mortar
  and grime; the plinth in the same rubble; windows set deep in four-piece
  blue-stone surrounds. The gable triangles still read as tan stucco and
  the surrounds were too white.
- Round 3: stone gable ends under the slates and greyer surrounds.

Verdict (Claude): **pick round 3.** A grey-brown rubble village under steep
dark slate, small deep windows, stone stacks at the gables, no porches or
verandas; the church is unchanged. Same 46 houses in the same places, sizes
and storeys, same streets, so the road, run-off and race do not
change. Panorama's town is drawn exactly as before.

Review fix after the verdict (not a new look round): a rubble step under
each door, from the ground to the floor, because the floors on sloping plots
stand up to 1 m above the ground and the old veranda deck used to bridge it.
