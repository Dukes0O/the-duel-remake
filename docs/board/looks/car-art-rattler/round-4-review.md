# CAR-ART-RATTLER — round 4

## Review

Round-four candidate for Claude's visual review. This is not a claim that the art is accepted.

The sheet compares the low front three-quarter photo reference with the exported Rattler GLB at a similar camera height. It also shows rear three-quarter, side, top and beside-person views. The GLB was imported into an empty Blender scene before these views were rendered.

The new recipe brings the truck closer to the reference in stance and visible hardware. The doors have pressed ribs and folded edges; the tires have modeled tread lugs; the chassis, V8 parts and grille bars are separate geometry. Body panels use dark rust and charcoal pictures, with a darker dust picture on the lowest cab hem.

## What still needs a look

- The lower cab panels remain broad and flat beside the more sculpted photo body.
- The engine reads as an engine, but its block and exposed fittings are simpler than the photo.
- The tread lugs are clear in silhouette but still look blocky.
- Confirm that the dark dust hem reads as dust at game scale and that the overall finish is not toy-like.

Claude should compare the photo crop and front three-quarter view first, then inspect the other four views for shape and scale.
