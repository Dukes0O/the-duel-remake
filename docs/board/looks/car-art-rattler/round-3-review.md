# CAR-ART-RATTLER · Round 3

The generated photographic reference is now on the comparison sheet beside the five requested views. The recipe uses a generated photographic steel and rust map, a low curved cab shell with a separate crowned roof, a windshield slit, visible engine hardware, and torus tyres with raised tread blocks. Blender exported 20,408 triangles and a 7,803,604-byte GLB, inside the card budgets.

This round does **not** pass. My own comparison still reads as a toy: the cab is an oval cap instead of the reference's shaped cab and open glasshouse; the front grille and engine overlap into a block; the engine does not read as a joined V8 assembly; and the image texture stretches into broad streaks on the shell. The sheet does not yet show the same vehicle in the same light. The rebuilt recipe is useful progress, but the car is not ready to hand off as accepted art.

Scores (1 = weak, 5 = strong): resemblance 2/5; proportions 2/5; materials and wear 2/5; detail 2/5; scale readability 3/5. Claude should inspect the photographic crop and five Blender views, with particular attention to the nose and cab silhouette. A later round needs a purpose-built curved cab with actual window openings and better texture projection before submission.
