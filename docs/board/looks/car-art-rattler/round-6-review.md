# CAR-ART-RATTLER — round 6

## Review

This is the final candidate sheet for Claude's visual review. The Rattler was exported to GLB and every vehicle view was rendered after reimporting that GLB into an empty Blender scene. The bed now has separate thick plank boards, the floor has individual planks, and the grey studio floor is darker than round five. The subject remains in the same front three-quarter camera family and includes rear, side, top and person-scale views.

The round-five closed shell is retained. I inspected the original Falcone F42 GLB and tried selecting its body faces for the cab and front wings. The cutout left disconnected fragments and was removed before this export. The final cab is still too egg-shaped and its window/door treatment reads as surface marks. The bed boards are visible, but lack convincing timber grain. The tire tread is still a shallow procedural ripple, not the Bowl truck's real tread. The front mudguards are still too thin and incomplete. This is the best safe export from this lane, not a claim that the art passes.

## Export

The exported GLB contains 21,950 triangles and is 2,624,988 bytes. It keeps the `rattler` id, metre units, wheel radii, sockets and part names. Export checks require only ranged `TEXCOORD_0` per primitive and metallic factors no higher than 0.3.

> Photographic modeling reference of an original chopped 1930s rat-rod pickup on a dusty gravel lot in plain bright overcast daylight, full vehicle in front three-quarter view, closed rounded steel cab with thick pillars, low slit windshield and dented door, tall narrow oval-top grille with thin bars, exposed detailed V8 engine, round lamps on stalks, large side exhaust, short timber-sided bed, rounded thin front mudguards, narrow front tires, large rear tires with rectangular block tread and dark steel disc wheels. Weathered grey-brown bare steel with vertical rust streaks, worn bright edges and dust low down. No brands, text, watermark, dramatic shadows, extra objects or people.
