The first paint is too flat. The reference has a weathered face, layered vest, rolled jacket sleeves and folded cargo trousers; the game model reads as a toy with broad smooth limbs. The neck seam is structurally bridged, but the body remains separate primitive pieces.

Five largest differences: (1) face and hair lack believable shape and detail; (2) vest is narrow rectangular panels; (3) teal sleeves are smooth cylinders; (4) trousers lack folds and pouches; (5) boots are oversized rounded blocks. The next round tests source-guided detail inside separate UV cells, not a return to full-view projection.

The controlled browser scenario passed with 88 captures, 12 near draws and 66,568 near triangles in the 12-fighter scene. High and Performance 120-frame baseline/crowd p95 were 16.8/16.8 ms for this run. These frame intervals do not certify GPU cost or worst-case combat. No runtime warnings or errors were reported.
