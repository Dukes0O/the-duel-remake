Raising the scarf, widening vest panels, adding pockets, trouser folds and cargo pouches, and shaping taller narrower boot shafts did not raise the in-game likeness score. The silhouette still reads as hard geometric pieces and a flat human figure. The visibly raised scarf forms a square block below the chin; the vest still reads as two thick vertical straps. The short horizontal cargo ledge makes the trousers appear cut off above the knees. Round knee disks and plain rubber boots remain far from the reference.

Five largest differences: (1) block scarf and a visually detached chin; (2) vest straps in place of a broad draped garment; (3) horizontal cargo ledge and smooth pants below it; (4) round knee disks; (5) simple rubber boot shafts and weak side/toe contour. The face photo is recognizable, but head and hair geometry are still too generic. Two consecutive matched rounds did not improve likeness, so the next approach must be a reference-shaped sculpt with retopology and baked surface detail. More parameters on the current ring-and-patch model are unlikely to resolve the silhouette.

The asset passes the connected-core and neck tests with 7,700 near and 1,864 far triangles, one material, and 64 triangles across the neck/jaw seam. The focused scenario did not measure twelve-fighter frame cost or GPU time. Cost remains provisional.
