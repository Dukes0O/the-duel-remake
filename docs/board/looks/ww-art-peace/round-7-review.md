# WW-ART-PEACE round 7: Dust's tanker

Claude's verdict: **passes.** From every view the model reads as one heavy old truck, with a battered tank under a tied cloth, and the goggles are large and plain from the front.

Claude rebuilt the recipe, because the old one's measurements did not match its photos: the wheel centres were 39 px out, the ground line 9 px out, and the front photo's height was read at the side photo's scale. That caused the tilted wheel, the squashed goggles and the tank wider than the cab.

The rear corner of the cloth stands higher than the side photo shows, so that it meets the rear photo's plate. That is the one place where the side view departs from its photo. The end plates are flat, which shows only from very close.
