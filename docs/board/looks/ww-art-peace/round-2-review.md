# WW-ART-PEACE round 2: the first four props

Date: 6 November 2026
Sheet: `round-2.jpg`, each row the photo, then the model three ways.
Verdict (Claude): **`mirage-billboard`, `gunn-rock`, `gunn-turret` and `kettles-chair` pass.** The other props on the card follow.

- `mirage-billboard`: a lovely painting from the front, and a plank on a pole from the side.
- `gunn-rock`: scarred on one side only.
- `gunn-turret`: Claude rebuilt it from its side photo at one true scale: a turned body, one barrel and three huge bolts. It is absurdly clean. The V where its plates meet at the barrel looks like armour.
- `kettles-chair`: Claude rebuilt it with each part on its own plane: feet in the middle, an arm on each side, and the lamp and tools on one side. It stands about 3 m high.

Small things left: the sides of the chair's feet are a little streaked, and a thin pale line follows some of its edges. Neither is worth a round at the distance a player sees it.
