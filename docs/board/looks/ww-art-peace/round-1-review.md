# WW-ART-PEACE round 1 review

The sheet pairs each generated source photo with a render imported from the exported GLB. Blender used the Standard view transform. The billboard has a separate edge-on side comparison. No labels or borders are in the sheet.

| Prop | Would a child laugh from the car? | Would it pass for a real thing? | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mirage billboard | Yes. The polished treatment-plant painting is an obvious false promise in the dust. | From the front at distance, possibly; from the side, clearly a thin board. | Confirm the side joke and the paint reads from car height. |
| Gunn turret | Yes. Its spotless, overbuilt finish is absurd here. | No. Some photo seams show at three-quarter view, and the finish reads as deliberately too clean. | Check the three oversized base bolts and metallic response. |
| Gunn rock | Maybe. The one-sided bullet scars are a quiet gag. | Mostly. The outline is rounded, though seams show near the edge at an angle. | Check that the far face stays untouched and the seams are acceptable. |
| Kettles chair | Yes. The dentist chair on truck hardware is an immediate gag. | No. The three-quarter render has mismatched surfaces and does not fully match the front and side photos. | This is the weakest piece. Decide whether another photo/model pass is needed before acceptance. |

Generated source images are in `public/assets/models/wasteland/town/textures/`; their prompts and credits are in the adjacent `CREDITS.md`. The chair source was regenerated once because the first contact sheet did not keep one consistent design across views. The submitted sheet uses only the replacement photos.
