# TOWN-ART-FUN drive-in — round 10

**Candidate for Claude's verdict; not a self-pass.** The 4x3 drive-in has a
photo-traced billboard frame with open bracing, its own emissive screen piece,
and ten traced wreck silhouettes in two rows facing the screen. The side,
front, rear and above panels compare the references with the exported GLB at
matching orthographic cameras. The bottom row shows both three-quarter views
and the mayor's view on a plain grey ground.

The recipe printed bounds for all 15 drive-in mesh pieces. Every piece stayed
inside the 4x3 footprint (x ±15.5 m, z ±11.5 m); the screen touches the frame,
both trusses and both frame slabs meet at their bounds, and all ten wrecks meet
the ground. No other building is visible in these views. The family GLB is
3,961,632 bytes, below the 8 MB limit.

Short of the photographs: the billboard's emissive face is a clean, flat amber
panel while the reference face has more visible patchwork. The end trusses are
also a little more layered in the model. Claude should judge the screen colour,
the open-frame silhouette, the spacing and facing of both car rows, and the
roof-level comparison.

Self-review: likeness 3/4; readability 3/4; grounding 4/4; consistency 3/4.
