# Wreck ring, round 18

Candidate art for Claude's verdict. The ring uses two overlapping courses of vertically standing salvaged tyres around four individually movable photo-carved bumper-car shells. A sandy floor and darker worn centre sit inside the wall. The four model views are generated orthographic shell references; the sheet shows the whole ring from above and in three-quarter views as well as the matched straight views.

Self-review: the mayor and three-quarter views read as a bumper-car enclosure. From side, front and rear, the tyre wall obscures most of the cars; the overhead view shows all four. The shell photos are detailed and the exported shells retain their orange-red body color and cockpit, though the source's seat and steering geometry are not equally clear from every generated angle. The dusty floor has a visible lighter centre patch. Claude should judge the wall silhouette, car visibility from the road, and whether the dust reads as one continuous floor.
