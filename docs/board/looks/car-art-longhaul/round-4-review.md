## Geometry changes

- Closed the cab with painted steel door and shell panels. The paired front windscreen is about 37 percent of cab height; the dark side glass now sits visibly outside the door skin. Added thin frames, mirrors, a dark seat and wheel, and four roof marker lamps.
- Replaced the plain grille bars with a wire mesh, kept round guarded lamps, removed the middle brace, and joined the bull bar to the thick bumper with two tow shackles.
- Moved the rear axles to a 3.32 m front-to-middle gap and a 1.52 m tandem spacing. The 1.45 m side tanks sit between the front and middle tyres with at least 15 cm clearance from each.
- Added 24 raised tread blocks to each of the six tyres. Hubs are dark, and each wheel arch now stands clear of the tyre.
- Extended the bed over the rear wheels. Kept the welded spare rack and added a bed-mounted A-frame, red recovery toolbox, winch cable and chains.
- Made the review ground large and mid-grey through the full frame. Kept the approved rusted paint and dusty rubber pictures and their material settings unchanged.
