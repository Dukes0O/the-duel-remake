# Longhaul shape, round 5

The sheet compares the exported GLB with the generated reference photograph from round 4 at a matching front three-quarter angle. It also shows rear three-quarter, side, top and person-scale views. Every model view was rendered after importing the exported GLB into an empty Blender scene.

## Changes

- Lowered the roof and reinforced the front windscreen pillars. A full-width painted panel now closes the lower cab front down to the bumper.
- Lightened the cab glass and made it dusty, with the dark seat and steering wheel behind it.
- Brought the bull bar closer to the grille, rounded the round-lamp guards, and retained the mesh grille, thick bumper and two shackles.
- Replaced the four white roof blocks with two round lamps on dull metal stalks.
- Raised the bed crane into an ochre-yellow A-frame and added its pulley, hanging chain and forged hook. Bed rails remain tubular. The toolbox is darker rust red; both spares remain.
- Kept the approved side profile, wheel parts, tank placement, rusted paint and dusty rubber. The sheet uses one mid-grey ground and the previous generated truck photo for direct comparison.
