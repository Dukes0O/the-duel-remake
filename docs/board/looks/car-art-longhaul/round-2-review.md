# Longhaul shape, round 2

This round rebuilt the candidate from the photo target. The main comparison uses the same front three-quarter direction and matching vehicle scale. Review views are rendered from the exported GLB after importing it into an otherwise empty Blender scene.

## Reference measurements and model changes

- Target envelope: about 7.15 m long, 2.72 m wide and 3.53 m high. The six tyres are about 1.47 m in diameter; the chassis has about 0.56 m of clearance. The tyres extend about 0.25 m beyond the body sides.
- Rebuilt the cab-forward silhouette with a sloped windshield, dark side windows, door seams and hinges, a large barred grille, round lamps and a layered bull bar.
- Added the ladder chassis, three axles, leaf spring packs, dampers, side fuel tanks and guards, diesel detail, exhaust stacks, a braced rear service rack, two strapped spare wheels, tool boxes and a rear recovery boom.
- Replaced the round-1 smooth body masses and separate tread blocks with thin panels, real gaps, a recessed grille and chevron tread shaped into continuous tyres.
- Added two generated color pictures: dark rust beneath faded ochre enamel, and dusty cracked rubber. UV coordinates repeat each picture at about 1.4 m per tile. Both pictures are compressed JPEGs and are credited beside the model.
- Imported and rendered the exported GLB in a fresh Blender scene for the sheet. Only the vehicle is exported; the person and review ground stay in the sheet scene.

## Self-check

The truck now reads as the same kind of long cab-forward 6x6 recovery truck at the photo's low three-quarter angle: matching wheel count and size, short nose, tall cab, side tanks, twin spares, front protection and rear rack. The body panels and rust picture make it much less toy-like than round 1. My resemblance score is 4/5 at the full-vehicle view; close-up material and panel fidelity still trail the photograph. The GLB is 24,676 triangles and 1,878,576 bytes, within the 25,000-triangle and 8 MB limits.

This remains an art candidate, not an in-game result. Claude should compare the primary matched-scale panels and check the cab silhouette, wheel/body proportions, dark rubber, rust scale, connected rack and fenders, and the person scale view.
