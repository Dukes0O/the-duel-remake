# Longhaul shape, round 3

The truck was rebuilt from the low three-quarter photo reference. The reference was generated with the built-in image generator for this modeling round. Only a fitted crop is on the sheet; the source image stays outside the repository.

Reference prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: private modeling reference photograph for an original game vehicle. One realistic fictional heavily used 6x6 desert recovery truck in plain daylight, dusty and hand-welded. Front three-quarter low camera around axle height, facing slightly toward camera. Long chassis rails, broad flat-fronted cab about 2.4 m wide, 2.2 m tall, 2 m deep, nearly vertical front, inset divided windshield panes with slight backward slope, side door windows and a roof, large tubular bull bar. Three axles, six round chunky tyres about 1.4 m diameter with tread blocks and integrated wheel arches. Two 2 m cylindrical fuel tanks lying horizontally along chassis between first and second axles. Rear flatbed with compact braced tool rack, two spare tyres, chains and tool boxes within tail. Faded ochre paint over dark brown rust, dusty charcoal rubber, oily steel. Every part attached and resting on chassis. Neutral hard packed ground and pale overcast sky, soft bright even daylight, true photographic texture. Full truck entirely in frame. No people, text, logos, other vehicles, cinematic color grading or dramatic shadows. Original design, no film or named make.

## Geometry changes

- Corrected cylinder axis mapping. Both 2 m fuel tanks now lie lengthwise beside the chassis between axles one and two.
- Rebuilt the cab as a connected 2.4 m wide, 2.2 m tall and 2 m deep shell. It has paired front windshield panes on a slight slope, two side door windows, doors, roof and cab supports that meet the chassis.
- Added two full-length chassis rails, crossmembers, front and rear bumpers, and a flatbed deck behind the cab. The rack uprights meet the bed; the two spare tyres, tool chests and chains fit inside the tail.
- Joined the wheel-arch segments into continuous fenders. The six tyres keep a round 1.4 m outline with shallow tread blocks.
- Kept both approved material images and all existing material settings unchanged.
- Rendered the exported GLB after importing it into an empty Blender scene. The model is 23,668 triangles and 1,664,140 bytes.

## Self-check

Shape and attachment: 4/5. All six axle positions, side tanks, cab, rails, deck, fenders, rack and spares read together as a recovery truck. The side view clearly shows the three axles and long chassis. The proportions follow the photo more closely than round 2.

The model still has simpler panel and glass detail than the photo.
