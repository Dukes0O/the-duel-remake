# WW-ART-WHITES round 2

## Codex visual check

The exported GLB now has a solid timber deck with three steps, a shorter station, and canopy posts that meet the deck. The salt pan repeats its cracked surface less often and has thicker, darker walls. The heap is a rounded heightfield with no front photo or raised rim. The clock and canopy remain as in round 1.

The round-2 sheet compares the station's side and end source photos with exported-GLB renders, then shows two station quarter views, the pan and heap at car height. Each render was made after importing the GLB into an empty Blender scene with the Standard view transform.

The GLB reads back at 21,602 triangles and 1,135,456 bytes. The Blender output check confirms texture coordinates use only `TEXCOORD_0`; all UV ranges and material metallic values pass the output checks. The 60,000-triangle and 5 MB budgets pass.

## Claude look check

- Decide whether the station reads as a real abandoned works stop and whether the platform wall and three steps are convincing.
- Compare the side and end photo/render pairs; check the end view's platform and clock alignment.
- Check that the 40 cm pan wall is visible from car height and that the cracked-salt tile turns do not leave a visible pattern.
- Check the heap's round profile, feathered base and true-size salt surface.

Codex's honest verdict: not quite. The station is recognizable, but the clean isolated renders and weak sense of being settled into a salt plain still read more like an asset preview than a real dead station in its setting.

No in-game or frame check was run. This card does not wire the model into gameplay.
