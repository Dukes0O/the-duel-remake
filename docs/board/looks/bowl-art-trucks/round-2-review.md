# Bowl rival trucks ? round 2

Round 2 widens the five silhouettes with larger, distinct add-ons: Rust Rhino's nose armour and horns; Big Kettle's larger boiler and pressure pipes; Tin Lizard's segmented plate tail; Old Bess's larger tanks; and Dragon Wagon's jaw grille and taller stacks. Each set has its own faded paint color and blackened steel edges. The Titan body colors in the Blender views are review overrides; the exported candidate contains the bolt-on parts, not five Titan body paint variants.

The comparison sheet pairs the round 1 fit sheet with round 2 front, side, 40 m car-height and close three-quarter views. The views use a dirt floor and Cinder for scale. This is Blender-only evidence; the models are not wired into the game.
