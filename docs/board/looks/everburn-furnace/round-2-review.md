# Old Smoke's furnace: round 2 (Claude, 10 November 2026)

Sheet: `round-2.jpg` (twelve pictures from the game in Everburn job 6, memory-only saves). Top row: the round 1 model
with its colours drawn properly. The other rows: round 2. Views at 13 to 17 m and from behind at 30 m.

## What changed

- **Why the body was black.** The scene darkened a material once for every part that used it, so rust and steel, used
  by dozens of parts, ended near black while the ochre plates (a few parts) kept some colour. Each material is now
  darkened once. This also lightens the well's buildings, the Dead Mall and the Boneyard, which had the same fault.
- **Shape.** A firebox with a barred fire door on each flank and at the rear, a banded drum on top, a steam dome, a
  taller chimney with a crown, a ram at the front. The colours are back to the first reviewed set, a little darker.
- **Fire.** The stand-in cones are gone. Each door's three openings turn white-hot while it glows, and two tongues of
  fire stand over that door only.
- **Damage.** The left skirt and shoulder plate fall with the first hit (the left door), the right pair with the
  second, the rear corner shields with the third.

## Against the card's four points, at 30 m

1. Facing and doors: yes. The cab, chimney and drum give the direction; each door is a dark barred panel.
2. Stoke and glow: yes. The smoke turns orange over the chimney; the glowing door is the brightest thing in the picture.
3. Damage: partly. Missing skirts and shoulder plates show from the side; from behind at 30 m the change is small.
4. A furnace on wheels from scrap: yes in shape. The surfaces are flat colour with no wear, so it is cleaner than gritty.

Verdict (Claude): the four points read, so the chase can be driven by hand with this model.

Verdict (Kyle, 10 November): "this art looks toy like." Not accepted as the look. Round 3 builds the furnace from
photos with the art line's kits instead of restyling boxes.
