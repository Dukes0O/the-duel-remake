# TOWN-ART-WATER round 3 builder review

The sheet shows the exported GLB re-imported into a fresh Blender scene. It
includes the full site, road-height views, close views of all six buildings,
the treatment sequence and the generated desert reference.

## Scores

- Resemblance to the waterworks reference: 4/5. The tower, pump shed, well,
  standpipe, treatment stages and lined reservoir read as the intended working
  structures.
- Readability at town and road views: 4/5. The tower and pump house read at road
  height; the process order reads from above.
- Grounding: 4/5. The supports, walls and banks meet the ground in the fresh
  import views.
- Consistency with the town: 4/5. The sun-worn timber, corrugated tin, stained
  concrete and earth fit the rest of the Scablands buildings.
- Geometry and image budgets: 4/5. The GLB and each building meet the card
  budgets. No frame timing is run during an art round; runtime frame cost stays
  unmeasured until the family is wired into the town.

## Five largest differences from the reference

1. The tank tower is simpler and more orderly than the timber tower in the
   reference; its braces and tank seams repeat evenly.
2. The treatment plant is laid out as clear separate stages, but the connecting
   pipes are thin and hard to follow from road height.
3. The reservoir is very square and its water surface is smoother than the
   reference pond.
4. The pump shed has clear corrugated walls and an open service bay, but less
   visible engine clutter and repair wear than the reference shed.
5. The isolated review view has no workers, buckets in use or surrounding town
   clutter to give the buildings more scale and life.

## Verdict

The builder judges the sheet ready for the art review. This is not the Director's
art acceptance or a beta verdict. The card's look decision remains with Claude.
