# TOWN-ART-WATER round 4 builder review

The sheet compares the generated reference photo with the exported GLB
re-imported into an empty Blender scene. It shows the complete mayor view,
driver-height view, and separate views of all six buildings.

## Scores

- Resemblance to the waterworks reference: 4/5. The dark braced tower, open
  pump shed, treatment sequence and dug pond now use the reference's main
  shapes and materials.
- Readability at town and road views: 4/5. The site reads from above; the road
  view makes the tank and pump shed easy to identify.
- Grounding: 4/5. The buildings and pond bank sit on the same review ground;
  the outlet pump meets the bank.
- Consistency with the town: 4/5. Timber, rusted steel, tin, concrete and dark
  earth use separate pictures or materials with subdued color.
- Geometry and image budgets: 4/5. The export check passes each named building
  budget, UV requirements, metallic cap and 3 MB family limit. Frame cost is
  not measured because the family is not wired into the game.

## Remaining differences

1. The diesel engine and pipe fittings remain simplified at close range.
2. The sandbags repeat around much of the pond bank; the reference has a more
   irregular, partly eroded edge.
3. The tank uses riveted steel rather than timber staves.
4. The treatment plant's stages are readable, but the long roof runs make the
   site view more orderly than the reference.
5. The sheet shows an isolated waterworks with no workers or neighboring town
   structures for scale.

The reference photo was generated with built-in ImageGen and stays outside the
repository. Its crop appears beside the model on the sheet. The model texture
prompt and credit for `town-water-corrugated.jpg` are in the neighboring
texture `CREDITS.md`.

## Verdict

The builder submits round 4 for Claude's art review. This is not the Director's
art acceptance or a beta verdict.
