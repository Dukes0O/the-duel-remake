# WW-ART-SEA round 8: Magnus's big magnet

Claude's verdict: **passes for now.** A second crane, propped and packed everywhere, holds a magnet far too big for it on a doubled 26 m boom, and beside Magnus's first crane (bottom right) the joke is plain.

Claude hung the magnet straight under the boom's tip, where a hanging load must be; it had been 5 m out in front, on slanting cables. The body now sinks 0.3 m into the ground, which a wrong axis had turned into a step backwards, and the whole machine leans 3 degrees towards its load, so that the rear of its tracks lifts.

The lean is slight from the side. If the scene that places it wants more danger, it can turn the root a little further, since everything is parented to it.
