# WW-ART-SEA round 9: the water front

Claude's verdict: **passes for now.** Three segments in a row read as one long wall of churning brown flood water with dirty spray along its crest, and nothing about it is clean or blue. Segments join without a seam, and a scene makes it churn by scrolling its picture upwards at the root's `scrollV`, 0.18 picture-heights a second.

What is weak: at 25 m the picture's repeat shows across the face, and from 80 m the front looks low for its length. Its profile from the side is a thin curling sheet, which is right for a wave but shows at the two open ends of a line of segments. SEA-FLOOD's scene, which places it, should look at it from its own camera with the picture moving before it is wired in, and may stand it taller or close its ends.
