# WW-ART-SEA round 3 look notes

Date: 6 November 2026
Sheet: `round-3.jpg` (1600 x 956, 280,361 bytes)

The first row shows the gate photo, shut render, open render and three-quarter
view. The next rows show the ramp and arch photos beside their dead-on renders,
then three-quarter views from about 20 m at car height. Renders came from the
exported GLB in an empty Blender scene with flat overcast light.

## Self-review

- **Harbour gate — 3/5.** At car distance the shut gate reads as rusted steel,
  and both named leaves swing from their outer hinges. In the close view, the
  frame joints and coping look simplified. Would someone take it for a real
  thing? Probably at 20 m while shut; not up close.
- **Harbour ramp — 2/5.** The 8 m opening and falling stone deck are real
  geometry, and the three-quarter view shows the descent. The deck still reads
  like a photo mapped onto a broad slab. Would someone take it for a real thing?
  No.
- **Causeway arch — 2/5.** The opening is clear through to daylight, the inside
  is dark and the rails use the requested gauge. From the front, the arch and
  rails look too flat and simple. Would someone take it for a real thing? No,
  though it reads as a low railway arch at distance.

Claude should check the three-quarter wall joins, the gate's open pose, and the
arch's low crown and rail placement before judging the art.
