# WW-ART-SEA round 5: the Mule's gear wagon

Claude's verdict: **passes.** In the top row the side view closely matches its photo, with the deck, side beam, springs, axle boxes, truss and hooks in their true places. The load of bright new castings stands out against the old wagon, and it is plainly too much for it.

Claude rebuilt the job's wagon, which had wire wheels, no springs or axle boxes, and a tall column of castings stood end on. All of the numbers come from one scale, 3.6 mm a pixel, measured on the side photo.

The end view is the weakest: the underframe is a flat picture set back from the end beam, and a player will see that only when standing on the track. The wagon's couplings stand at the Mule's 0.80 m.
