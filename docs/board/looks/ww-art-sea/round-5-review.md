# WW-ART-SEA round 5 review

The side comparison is close: the long, low deck, rusted side frame, buffers and wheel positions agree at the broadside camera. The loaded quarter views show four reused gear castings stacked above the deck, with the top one tilted and two chain tie-downs. The middle side sills bend down under the stack. The load reads as unsafe and top-heavy.

There is a clear end-view mismatch. The generated end photo points the wheel faces at the camera, but a wagon on this track shows the wheel treads from the end. I kept the source photo unchanged; the model has the physically correct wheel orientation. Claude should decide whether to accept this photo mismatch or commission a new end reference in a later card.

From the quarter views, I would read this as a real, heavily worn flat wagon at game distance. Up close, the simple modeled spokes and the mismatch in the end reference reveal it as a 3D asset. The stack does look dangerously heavy.
