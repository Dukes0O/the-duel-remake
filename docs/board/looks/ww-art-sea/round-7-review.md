# WW-ART-SEA round 7: Mrs Vane

The sheet compares the exported model with the front, side and back source photos, then shows a face close-up, a car-height three-quarter view and a town walker for scale. Blender rendered the exported GLB in an empty scene with the Standard view transform and flat mid-grey lighting.

The face reads as a calm older woman with a slight smile and direct gaze. Front-facing head surfaces select the front reference photo through the same `carve_hull` view selection used by the town walkers. The hair and head outline still show faceted edges, and the white coat reads somewhat smooth and heavy. The envelope is visible in her right hand from the front; its shallow slab projects away from the coat in the profile view. The model is recognizable and composed, but it has a little photographic uncanny quality at close range.

The contrast that carries the character is her clean white closed coat, neat grey hair, still posture and composed expression. The lane did not run browser checks or the game look check; Claude should judge the face, side-view envelope placement and in-game scale from this sheet.
