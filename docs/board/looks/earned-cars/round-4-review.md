# EARN-CAR-1 Ferrari Enzo, Claude's original model: rounds 1 to 4 (14 October 2026)

Sheet: removed 15 October 2026, replaced by round 5's (the car in the game, since removed too)
to keep the looks folder under its 20 MB budget. It showed the front
three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter and a front close-up,
rendered in Blender from the game's own builder (`src/enzo-vehicle.js`,
exported with three.js). 60,490 triangles and 58 draws (budget 73,600 and 66).

- Round 1: four round tail lamps, glass engine cover, side scoops and the low
  red body read right; the beak did not drop between the fenders, the rear deck
  sat high and boxy, two vents landed on top of the nose.
- Round 2: beak drops between raised fenders, lower deck, longer lamps;
  headlamps still read as white panels, mirrors too big.
- Round 3: smaller mirrors; the headlamps still read as flat white shapes.

- Round 4 (Kyle allowed a fourth round): the headlamps are built as geometry: two
  dark tinted projector lenses with thin lit rings in each recess, facing
  forward, and a slim running-light strip along the outer edge; a waist crease
  along the doors. A first try of the lenses faced upward with pale centres and
  read as cartoon eyes; corrected in the same round.

Verdict (Claude): **pick.** An original car inspired by the Enzo, not a
replica: the beak between raised fenders, teardrop canopy, side scoops, glass
engine cover and four round tail lamps read right from every side, and the
front no longer looks painted on. Registration, config, voice, armour kit,
tests and the save fixture follow once EARN-SCREEN merges (card EARN-CAR-1).
