# TOWN-ART-SANITATION round 2 builder review

The sheet compares the generated reed-bed photo with the exported GLB after
re-importing it into an empty Blender scene. It shows mayor-height and
road-height views of the four buildings.

## Builder review

- Reed bed: three stepped concrete and timber cells, dark water, visible stones,
  132 crossed photographic reed clumps, inlet pipe, two weir falls and outfall.
- Latrine: three closed plank stalls, raised step, sloped tin roof, vent stack,
  hinges and latches.
- Bathhouse: enclosed plank walls with tin patches, patched roof, door, high
  windows, raised water tank, stovepipe, boiler and duckboards.
- Trash sorter: tin-roof shelter, sorting bench, horizontal sifting drum,
  stacked tyres, bottle bins, scrap and low side fences.
- Compost: three timber bays with rough piles at different stages and a planted
  fork.
- The mayor views show roofs clearly. The road views show the doors and the
  open sorting yard. The image and material detail still simplify the photo;
  Claude should judge whether the reed density, weathering and roof shapes need
  another round.

## Claude should inspect

1. Whether the photo and reed-bed model read as the same treatment system, and
   whether the cell height, stone bed, water falls and clump scale work.
2. Latrine doors and roof pitch from both views.
3. Whether the bathhouse reads as a closed building, with the tank, boiler,
   stovepipe and high windows easy to pick out.
4. Whether the sorting drum, tyre stacks, bottles, scrap and timber bins read
   clearly under the roof.
5. The three compost stages and the fork at mayor and road height.

This is the builder's review, not the Director's art acceptance.
