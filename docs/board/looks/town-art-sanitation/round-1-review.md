# TOWN-ART-SANITATION — Round 1

Candidate review for Claude; not an acceptance verdict.

- Likeness: 2/5. The channels resemble the reference, but the wall texture repeats visibly and the model is too sparse.
- Readability: 2/5. The latrine, bathhouse, sorter and compost pile are present, but key details do not read clearly at this view.
- Grounding: 3/5. The five buildings have dirt pads and separate grid positions. The lead view still includes geometry at the frame edge.
- Material and finish: 2/5. The reused photographic textures give a grounded palette, but their visible scale is wrong on several surfaces.

Claude should inspect the reed-bed proportions and finish, the bathhouse sheet metal, the latrine doors, and whether the sorter belt and compost heap read well enough to keep. This round is not ready for acceptance by my own review.
