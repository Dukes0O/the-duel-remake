# The Deep final models, round 1 of 3 (DEEP-ART-FINAL, 24 October 2026)

Sheet: `round-1.jpg` (1352 x 1244, 151 KB). One row per model: its log card (the
installed picture, on the log's water colour), three more sides, and the game's own
sea life in the look page's High water (`deep-life` scenario). Recipe:
`tools/scenarios/deep-art-final-sheet.mjs`. All four are original geometry from
`tools/blender/deep-art-fit.py`, gritty and natural: muted colour, vertex-painted
mottling, no toy shapes.

1. **Sardines.** One slim silver fish (32 cm, 567 triangles) with a dark back, pale
   belly, gill cover and forked tail. The card is a school of 34 so it reads as
   silver fish together. In the water the existing school code instances it and the
   flank flashes work on it. *Look at:* the fish is a little plain from the side; at
   school scale it reads as intended.
2. **Sea lion.** A face (sloped forehead with a tan crest, dark eyes, ears, whiskers),
   a deep chest and a thick neck, long foreflippers swept back and two hind flippers
   trailing. *Look at:* the ears are small dark cones; the flippers could be a little
   thicker.
3. **Sea otter** (no model before). Floating on its back, pale head, frosted dark fur,
   paws on the chest, hind flippers up, kelp wound over its belly. *Look at:* from below
   in the water it is a dark shape against the surface, which is true to life; the card
   shows its detail. The forearms read a little blocky.
4. **Gray whale.** Mottled slate with pale scars and cream barnacle patches, short
   paddle flippers, a low hump and seven knuckles down the tail with no dorsal fin,
   notched flukes. In the water at the Drop-off it reads as a gray whale, dark and far.

Not judged here: sound, behaviour numbers, frame time.
