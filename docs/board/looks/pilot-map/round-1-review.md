# MAP-ART, round 1 (24 October 2026)

Sheet: `round-1.jpg`: the five signs from the air and from a car's height, then the Table's mesa,
lookout, plane wreck, strip, windsock and logbook. Recipe: `tools/blender/pilot-map-props.py`.
