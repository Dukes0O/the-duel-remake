# MAP-ART-2, round 1 (24 October 2026)

Sheet: `round-1.jpg`: the Table's mesa (far, on the horizon, cliff, foot) and the bush plane wreck
(three-quarter, side, tail, gear). The earlier MAP-ART sheets are gone: their signs, lookout,
strip, windsock and logbook were accepted and are unchanged.

- Mesa: the game's own weathered red sandstone picture on proper UVs (wrapped round the butte,
  planar on top), tinted per vertex into strata of unequal thickness, vertical joints, chutes with
  varnish streaks, a sheared rim, a rubble apron, fallen blocks and three spires. It reads as rock
  now, not a cake.
- Plane: dust and rust baked into one picture (gathers in the panel seams and low surfaces, bare
  metal on edges), faded cream paint with one orange stripe, a crushed nose, a collapsed left gear leg
  with its wheel lying beside it, the airframe rolled and nose-down.
- Open after round 1: the apron was still a smooth skirt; on the fin the number ran into the rudder
  and lost its last letter.
