## Five largest differences to fix

1. The tents need more varied canvas patches and a chair that reads from both views.
2. The container roof gardens read as dark tyres with sparse twigs; add visible scrub and a water catchment detail.
3. The mayor's view needs stronger roof silhouettes on the terrace and the two container blocks.
4. The home surfaces look too evenly lit in the sheet; lower exposure and use a darker, more directional fill so rust and wear remain visible.
5. The driver's view needs a stronger distinction between the three shack entries and their attached lean-to shapes.

## Round 2 decision, before code

Keep the ten home IDs, footprints, material atlas, and road-facing -Z layout. Improve the visible roof and entry details within the same per-plot budgets: patch both tent roofs and move the chair forward, add olive scrub and a small catchment gutter to the block roofs, make the terrace roof a two-slope form, and add a distinct door-side awning to shack C. Reduce the render exposure from 0.8 to 0.25 and use a warm side key with a softer fill. This should make wear and silhouettes easier to judge without adding runtime lights or exceeding the card's three submitted rounds.

**Verdict:** Round 1 does not pass. Build and review round 2 against these fixes.
