## Five largest remaining differences

1. The reference has more realistic surface texture and weathering than the solid-material model.
2. Its pipes and access fittings have more small-scale variation.
3. The driver view looks up through the tower's large braces and hides some lower structure behind the plaza.
4. The kiosk remains a simple booth rather than a built-out shop interior.
5. The small fittings will gain more scene context when a later town card installs them.
