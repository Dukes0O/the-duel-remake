# TOWN-ART-VANE round 1 review

Builder review: not ready to pass.

## Scores

| Item | Score | Finding |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Reference silhouette | 4 | The crown tank and tapered steel frame read as a water tower. |
| Proportions | 4 | The tower and its five fittings follow the card's grid sizes. |
| Materials | 3 | White and blue read, but the white surfaces are too pale to judge well. |
| Color | 4 | The water-drop blue separates Vane from the wasteland palette. |
| Detail | 3 | The lattice and signs are present, but the landmark and fittings need more readable detail. |
| Wear | 2 | The pads show little visible dust or corner wear. |
| Readability | 2 | The driver view crops the tower; its camera aims at 14 m. |
| Grounding | 3 | The mayor view shows the plaza, while the driver view hides its connection to the tower. |
| Scene consistency | 3 | The pieces are clean but too pale and lack enough visible ground wear. |
| Frame cost | 4 | The exported model is within the stated file and triangle budgets; game timing does not apply to uninstalled art. |

## Five largest gaps

1. The driver camera crops most of the 70 m landmark.
2. Pale lighting washes out steel, enamel and concrete differences.
3. Dust banks and sand-blasted corners are absent or too small to read.
4. The tower's lower structure is hidden in the car view.
5. The kiosk bottles are not visible at its counter.

## Verdict

Round 1 failed the view and wear checks. Round 2 raises the driver camera to the
tower's midpoint, adds visible ground dust and keeps the matched front, mayor
and driver renders.
