## Five largest gaps

1. Steel braces look more uniform than the photographed tower's structural steel.
2. The lower tower is partly hidden by the plaza from the car-height camera.
3. Kiosk bottles are not visible from the service side.
4. Dust mounds and sand-blasted corners are too small to read at town scale.
5. The white steel and enamel need a little more value separation.

## Verdict

Not ready to pass. Round 3 will move the bottles onto the service counter,
increase the size and spread of the dust banks, and improve the white-steel
contrast. The matched front, mayor and driver views remain in the sheet.
