Round 2 replaces upright box silhouettes with low collapsed roofs, absent side glass, splayed wheels, bent body panels and exposed rails. It adds darker scorched surfaces and shaped flame tongues. The vehicles are grounded on a flat dirt plane, and the existing Titan model appears only in the review render. The exported obstacle contains one named root and named body, glass, wheels, frame and fire parts; the Titan and review ground are excluded.

The wide view shows the scale cue, but the wrecks remain small at 40 m. The crushed outlines still read as flat slabs, and the van, bus and RV shapes do not separate clearly at distance. Rust still covers too much of each body instead of forming localized streaks and patches. The burning heap reads as two compressed shells with stylized flames. No game scene uses this model yet, so browser readability and frame cost remain unmeasured.

Round 2 is not a pass recommendation. Claude should judge the sheet, these scores and the remaining silhouette, material, fire and scale issues.
