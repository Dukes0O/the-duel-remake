# The Lab's look: round 1 (Claude, 11 November 2026)

Sheet: `round-1.jpg`. Four pictures of the Lift lesson from its browser check, memory-only saves: two steps before
this round and the same two steps after it.

## What changed

- The shared board art (`src/lab/classroom/board-art.js`): a corrugated workshop wall under one lamp in place of
  brushed steel; the pad outside at dusk with hills and firs; Kestrel One and the Dusthawk drawn as shaded things
  with glass, lights and a rotor that blurs; a concrete floor, soft shadows and lit force arrows for boards to use.
- The Lift board (`src/lab/lessons/lift-board.js`): planked crates, a weighing pad on load cells, a brass dial, air
  that streaks down with lift, dust along the pad, the helicopter's shadow.
- No step, word, number, control or hit area changed, and no test assertion changed.

## What Claude sees

- The opening question now happens in a place. The helicopter reads as a machine.
- The subject is still small in the board, because the board's layout is tied to its controls.
- The other fifteen lessons got the wall and nothing else. None lost a label, and none is yet lifted by it.
- Brian is still the rough 72-pixel render in these pictures; his painted portraits are made and not yet wired in.

Verdict (Claude): keep. This is the example for the other boards, not the finished Lab.
