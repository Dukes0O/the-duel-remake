# The Lab's look: round 8, the stage step on Lift (12 November 2026)

Sheet: `round-8.jpg`: two steps of the Lift lesson, the whole page, before on the left and after on the right, from
its browser check, memory-only saves. Built by a Sonnet helper to the rule on card LAB-LOOK ("the board is for the
picture"); judged by Claude.

- The readouts and both tables have left the board for a strip of the page under it, in the page's own type at 18
  pixels or more. The picture of lift and weight arrows side by side stays on the board without its numbers.
- The scene is drawn 1.55 times its own size (it was 1.2). The spare crates and the parked car are drawn at the
  scene's scale, so a crate is one size everywhere.
- Every control is pressed where it is drawn: the browser check now clicks the real crate, rotor and car with the
  mouse and reads the board's own pixel at each click.
- No test assertion changed; two tests were added. Other lessons' pages are unchanged.

What Claude sees: the gain is real and modest. The helicopter is about a quarter larger on screen, not the half the
card aimed at, because the board is limited by its height and the strip takes some of that height. About 150 pixels
stand unused at each side of the board. The builder's finding is the important one: a board that is wider than
1000 by 680, so that it fills its panel, is where the large gain is.

Verdict (Claude): keep Lift as it is. Lift's own size is set by how high the helicopter must climb inside the board;
a wider board pays in the lessons whose scenes are long and low, and those come next.
