  label read "LEFT SPRING COMPRESSES". The body now leans onto the spring that compresses.
- **Grip:** the tyre was drawn turned against its own "TYRE POINTS" arrow. It now points along it.

No step, word, number or control changed, and no test assertion changed. A few labels moved by a few pixels to clear
new drawing; none is a hit area.
