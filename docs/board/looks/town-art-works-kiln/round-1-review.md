# TOWN-ART-WORKS kiln — round 1

The exported model reads as a beehive brick kiln. The dome profile follows the
measured side photo; the brick courses remain visible around the curved shell.
The chimney stands beside the dome, and the road-facing arch reads as an open,
soot-dark firing mouth with an orange emissive glow inside.

I compared the exported GLB with the four dome photos at side, front, rear and
straight-down cameras. The top camera is oriented so the firing mouth matches
the photo's bottom edge. Both three-quarter views and the mayor view show the
kiln only; the passed warehouse is hidden in the review scene. The mayor camera
is about 80 m away and 45° down. The whole 2×2 footprint fits in each view.

The separate brick stack is readable as stacked fired bricks. The woodpile is
present but reads as a small, dark stack from above. Claude should judge its
size and the opening/glow balance in the mayor and driver views.

The JPGs are original images generated for this round and are credited in the
recipe. They are full size at no more than 1024 pixels wide. The comparison
sheet is 2048×2734 pixels and 473,043 bytes.
