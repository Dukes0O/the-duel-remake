second pass of wear and a few bent sheets. The ground itself is the plain's own surface with no change of colour, so
from above the square is shown only by its fence and its grid. The first-person tool covers the lower right of the
view while he builds; hiding it on his ground is a small follow-up. Nothing here was frame-timed: a player cannot
