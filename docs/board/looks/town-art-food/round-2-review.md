## Builder assessment

- Soup-kitchen resemblance: 3/5. The hanging patched cloth, curved blackened
  cauldron, stone ring, split logs, two timber benches, back shelf and fine-rib
  wall now match the reference's main forms. The shelter and roof are still
  cleaner and more regular than the photograph, and the flames remain partly
  hidden by the cauldron.
- Family readability: 4/5. Each building has separate close views from both
  camera heights. Fruit, tools and repair stock distinguish the stalls and
  shopfronts.
- Ground and framing: 4/5. The comparison ground is an even mid-grey-brown;
  the soup view uses the round 1 driver camera. The mayor views are deliberately
  tighter than the in-town 60–120 m view so each building can be judged here.
- Geometry and image budgets: 5/5. The family has 27,864 triangles; the soup
  kitchen has 4,964. The GLB is 1,898,152 bytes, with one UV coordinate layer
  per primitive and materials below the metallic limit.
