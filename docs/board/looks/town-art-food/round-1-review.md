# TOWN-ART-FOOD round 1 builder review

The sheet compares the soup-kitchen photo with the exported GLB re-imported
into an empty Blender scene. It also shows the roof and the full food family.

## Scores

- Resemblance to the soup-kitchen reference: 3/5. The welded canopy, tin
  roof, rear wall, large pot, stone hearth and benches match the main forms.
  The pot and benches are simpler than the photo, and the model's hanging
  canvas reads more like patched panels than torn cloth.
- Readability from town and road views: 4/5. The soup shelter reads clearly
  at driver height. The family view shows all 12 buildings, though small
  shops and stalls need a closer look to judge their signs and goods.
- Grounding: 4/5. The posts, benches and hearth meet the review ground. The
  building origins and the town's 8 m grid should be checked again when these
  models are placed in the game.
- Consistency with the town: 4/5. Steel, corrugated tin, timber, canvas,
  stone, soil and goods use distinct subdued pictures and colors. The model
  remains darker and neater than its photo.
- Geometry and image budgets: 5/5. Readback checks pass for all 12 named
  plots, individual triangle budgets, the 1 MB total image-pixel budget, UVs,
  the metallic cap and the 3 MB file limit.

## Remaining differences

1. The photo has a larger soot-blackened pot and more irregular hearth stones.
2. The canvas panels have less shape and wear than the reference awning.
3. The roof and steel frame remain more regular than a hand-patched shelter.
4. The family sheet is too distant to judge the market goods and painted shop
   signs. Claude should inspect their close views in the raw renders if needed.

The source photo and square material studies stay outside the repository.
Their prompts and credits are recorded in the neighboring texture
`CREDITS.md`. This sheet is one candidate round for Claude's art review, not
an art acceptance or a release verdict.
