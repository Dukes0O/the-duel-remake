This round replaces only `soup-kitchen`. Its body is carved with `hull_model.py` from four generated orthographic photos. The front and overhead panels compare those photos with the reimported GLB at matching cameras. The other eleven buildings keep their previous models.

The soup kitchen now follows the photo's proportions, canvas, rear tin wall, shelf, pot and benches. The front image is still soft at close range because the GLB embeds 256 × 192 copies to fit the existing family texture budget. From the front it reads close to a textured photo-relief; the overhead view reads mainly as a roof. This is a better source-based start, but the oblique shape and visible openings still need review before the kitchen is called finished.
