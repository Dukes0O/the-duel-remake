# TOWN-ART-FOOD market hall round 1

This is the first market hall round. The sheet compares the stall-front photo
and exported model, then shows both three-quarter angles, the road view and the
mayor view. The sheet is 196,269 bytes.

The source set is credited in `public/assets/models/wasteland/town/CREDITS.md`.
It includes the clean side, front, rear and plan views, plus the same front
with its stalls and goods.

The open bay reads through from the road and the patchwork roof reads from
above. The front match is still rough: the carved gable and photo stalls do
not align closely, the stall pieces look too flat, and the rendered front is
smaller than the reference. The mayor view is end-on and does not show enough
of the hall's roof plan. Claude should inspect the gable alignment, stall depth
and placement, and the mayor camera before deciding whether to accept this
round or ask for another.
