The round 1 gaps are partly addressed: the stands have fewer, heavier rows; the requested views are now from the arena floor, top row and overhead; ramps have bent rails, weld marks and footings; both sides have crowd silhouettes; banners and crushed cars dress the floor. Floodlight towers, flame-jet parts and a lit score pattern are present. The scoreboard still has no readable text, smoke is not modeled, and the surfaces could use more uneven wear. No new external art was used.

The round 2 GLB has 37,436 triangles, 80 mesh batches and 1,947,708 bytes. It remains under the 80,000 triangle and 3 MB limits. The review-only truck is excluded from the GLB. Blender rendered three views at night. The comparison JPG is 74,804 bytes, under 500 KB.
