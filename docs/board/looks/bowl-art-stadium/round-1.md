## Five largest gaps

1. The Wasteland art direction board's arena is more layered and irregular than these straight terraces.
2. The scoreboard face is blank; no game text or graphics are authored on this art card.
3. The ramps and podium use clean, repeated shapes instead of visibly bent and repaired scrap.
4. Only the far stand reads as a dense crowd in the hero view; the near edge hides the opposite seating.
5. There is no smoke, active flame, banners or car wreck dressing in this first model pass.
