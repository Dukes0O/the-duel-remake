# Western mountains and Australia, round 3 of 3 (final)

Claude's round 2 verdict and the two fixes asked for are at the top of
`west-australia-round-2.md`. Only the kangaroo and the kookaburra changed;
every other animal in the group passed and is byte exact.

Sheet: `west-australia-round-3.jpg` (133,037 bytes), the same camera columns
as rounds 1 and 2: 14 m, 55 mm side and three-quarter at Idle frame 1, then
close Idle and Walk or Fly frames, from actual GLB imports.

## What changed

| Animal | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Kangaroo | Ears rebuilt as broad cupped shells opening forward, about as long as the head (0.22 m), pale cream inner fur, dark rims and tips. Head longer and deeper (0.33 m) with a soft grey muzzle, dark nose, cream chin and throat, and a visible dark eye in a pale ring. Idle stands more upright: the body tips back 14 degrees about the hips while legs, feet and tail keep their ground contact; short forearms are held out in front with dark paws. Darker grey-brown back, creamier front from chin to belly with a sharper edge. The bound is unchanged. |
| Kookaburra | Head and neck rebuilt as one round head sunk into the shoulders: a head ball almost as wide as the body, a broad nape-and-throat ball joining it to the body, and the face tapering into the bill. No loft folds, so no crease or cap line. Cream-white head and chest with light crown streaks, a dark brown stripe through the eye, a heavier bill (dark upper, pale lower mandible). Wings, tail and blue patch kept; five shorter primaries to stay inside 2,000 triangles. |

## Unsure, for Claude

- Kookaburra: the head is round and merged, but where the head ball meets
  the nape ball there is a soft hollow rather than one continuous surface.
  At game distance it reads as one head. The bill's two tones show best
  from the side.
- Kangaroo: the forearms are thin; real forearms are thicker at the elbow.
