## Sound evidence and limits

Mix peaks are reported as -14.20 dBFS High and -13.39 dBFS Performance.
Isolated cue onset estimates fall within 3 to 5 ms of dispatch. The plotted
gate stem shows distinct low drum pulses and higher chain transients without
an unexplained sustained tone. Its 400 ms loudness is often below the engine,
so the mix is not accepted yet. The 16 kHz capture and 45 to 3150 Hz plot do
not measure the complete source spectrum or every original-rate peak. This
is measured and visual sound review, not a subjective listening claim or a
whole-race audio pass.
