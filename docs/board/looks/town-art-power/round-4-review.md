# TOWN-ART-POWER round 4 review (Claude)

Passes, 6 November 2026. The windmill turns and stands up, with its worn blades and peeling vane, and the generator, solar rack and pole look like their photos. The fuel depot's tank and pump work.

Short of the photos: the depot's base is a plain slab where the photo has a hazard-striped bund wall, its flat hazard stripes are thin, and the generator's tin shelter floats a little. Worth another look once they stand in a town.
