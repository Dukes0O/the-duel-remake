Round 3 photo inputs are generated with OpenAI ImageGen and stored in `public/assets/models/wasteland/town/textures/`. The GLB embeds the one packed atlas for runtime use. The comparison sheet shows each reference beside the exported GLB's overhead and driver-seat views.

Prompts:

## Independent visual review

**Verdict: does not meet the card's visual acceptance.** The comparison sheet shows these blockers:

- The windmill is cropped in both model views, so the full rotor and mast silhouette cannot be judged.
- The generator reads as an exposed engine under a skeletal canopy. The tin shelter, black exhaust stain and fuel drums are not clear.
- The solar rack reads as four similar panels in a 2 by 2 grid, not six mismatched panels with a visible crack.
- The fuel depot reads as two tanks on a small platform. The pump, connected pipes and valves, and bund are not clear.
- The pole reads as a crooked timber pole with a transformer and wire stubs.

The reviewer could not support the earlier all-4 self scores from this sheet. This is the third and final submitted round under the card. A new comparison sheet would exceed its three-round cap, so this lane stops for direction.

## Limits

These are clean Blender renders after GLB export and reimport. The card says this art is not yet wired into the game, so no browser, frame or in-game check applies. Frame timing is not run for art rounds. This independent review is not the Director's final art verdict.
