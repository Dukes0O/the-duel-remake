**The model.** The panels are photographed painted steel: the flaking paint and rust of photos already in the game,
recoloured into the reference's reds, blues, ivories and mustards. Each panel has a ragged bare steel edge, a dark weld

copper ends. The 528 separate parts are welded into 87, and the file is 2.4 MB.

**The moments.** It sags with steam rising from its back, and a 60 m beam with a soft halo stands over the open box.
Its fire is a stream of fireballs from the mouth that lands a third of the way out, with flames burning along the orange
to the end of its reach. The cable hangs slack and pulls straight when tight. In the fall its lights die, dust rises,
the arena's jets fire together and the last box bounces along behind the truck.
