# TOWN-ART-WORKS small works and haulers, round 1 (Claude, 19 November 2026)

**Verdict: all eight pass.** Each is inside one 8 m cell and its 1,800-triangle budget.

| Piece | What it is | Triangles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `flatbed` | an old blue two-axle lorry carved from its four photos, tyres built from its side photo, `flatbed:load` a roped heap of scrap from its overhead photo; matches the photos from the side and above | 1,504 |
| `handcart` | plank deck, spoked wheels and shafts traced from its side photo, the deck's top from its overhead photo; matches both | 992 |
| `barrow` | carved from its four photos: dented green tray heaped with earth, one wheel, wooden handles; matches all four | 700 |

Fixed before this round was counted: the flatbed's rear wheel arches were cut up through its
deck and showed as dark notches from above (the rear arch is now cut smaller than its tyre);
the handcart's prop leg wore a strip of studio floor; the flatbed's load wore the photo's grey
edge.

Short of the brief: the pit and trench cannot be dug below the town's ground, so their depth is
read from banks and the dark floor; the flatbed's carved cab is soft at its corners; the pipe
stack is plain.
