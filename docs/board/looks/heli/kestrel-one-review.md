# Kestrel One, Claude's original model: rounds 1 to 3 (14 October 2026)

Sheet: `kestrel-one-round-3.jpg` (three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter,
cockpit close-up). Recipe: `tools/blender/kestrel-one.py`. 12,580 triangles,
no textures (PBR colours only), 539 KB.

- Round 1: read as a helicopter; cabin almost all glass, square engine deck
  ending at the boom, plank fin, tail lettering floating off the boom.
- Round 2: painted doors below the window line, swept fin with a navy tip,
  a faired join into the boom, panel seams. Instrument panel and cabin floor
  poked through the nose; door frames floated outside the cabin; doubled
  lettering on the deck.
- Round 3: frames follow the cabin's curve, panel and floor inside, lettering
  on the surface. Nothing pokes through.

Verdict (Claude, within the three-round cap): **pick for H1.** It meets the
model contract on HELI-ART-SRC exactly (nodes, origin, axes, size), needs no
licence or credit, and reads as a real light two-seater in clean working
paint. Weakness: simple shading up close (no texture maps); dirt and wear
come with H3's grime layer. The R22 donor download is no longer needed for
H1.
