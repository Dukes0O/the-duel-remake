# TOWN-ART-WORKS sawmill — round 2

The shed now measures 15 m along its length and 7.5 m across, with a 5.7 m
ridge and about a 30° roof pitch. This follows the side photo's length to
ridge-height ratio and the front photo's span and pitch, while staying inside
the 3×2 footprint.

The roof photo covers both full slopes. I traced the front photo's timber
members and slabbed that frame at five post lines. The openings between the
posts, braces and trusses stay open. The saw blade is a 1.2 m vertical disc
with more of it visible above the bench. The bench, logs, stacks and sawdust
remain separate pieces.

I inspected the exported GLB in side, front, rear, straight-down, two
three-quarter and mayor views. The larger blade is easier to see from the
front and mayor views. The sawdust remains a small bright mound, and the front
photo texture on the timber frame reads lighter than the source in some views.
Claude should judge those details, the roof mapping and the blade visibility
from the driver and mayor views.

The shed, bench, blade, bark and sawdust source JPGs remain unchanged. The
comparison sheet is 2048×2734 pixels and under 500 KB.
