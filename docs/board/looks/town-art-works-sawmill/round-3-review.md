# TOWN-ART-WORKS sawmill, round 3 (Claude, 19 November 2026)

**Verdict: passes.** Built and judged by Claude; the sheet beside this note is rendered from
the exported `works.glb`.

What the card's round 3 asked for, as the recipe prints it:

- Shed 20.7 m long and 9.6 m wide over its roof, inside the 3 by 2 plot (x -10.4 to 10.3, z -6.9
  to 7.0 with the stacks and sawdust).
- The roof's lowest point is 3.70 m and its ridge 6.86 m; the ridge is 6.72 m at both ends, so
  the roof does not slope along its length.
- Each of the five frames stands from 0.00 m to 6.52 m. Posts, tie beam, king post and knee
  braces are traced from the front photo and wear its timber.
- The saw blade is 1.2 m across, traced tooth by tooth from its photo, standing through the
  bench's edge with a log on the carriage half-way through its cut.
- 4,752 triangles of the plot's 9,000.

The front photo sets one scale for everything (0.0095 m a pixel). Round 2 stretched the photo
to reach a width and got short stubs; a single scale gives eaves of about 3.9 m, a ridge of
about 6.7 m and a roof about 9 m wide, which is what the card asked for.

Against the photos: the long side, the end frame and the roof's rust and patches match. Two
bays of the front slope are left unsheeted on purpose, with one sheet slipped across them, so
that the saw shows from the mayor's camera; the roof photo has no such gap.

Short of the brief: the gable ends show thin sheet edges where the end photo has a deep rusted
edge of tin; the board stacks are dark and plain beside the logs; the slipped sheet hangs
further below the eave than a loose sheet would.
