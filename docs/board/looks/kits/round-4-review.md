This round samples each production car's flank surface with ray casts between the axles. Thin overlapping door sheets follow those contacts, preserve glass and wheel openings, and use small fasteners. All nine kit GLBs were rebuilt. Focused asset, authored state, resource lifecycle and existing fallback checks pass 10/10. Independent score: likeness 2, readability 3, grounding 2 and consistency 2; frame cost remains unscored. The appearance change is modest in the matched view; red stock rear body and simple roof/rear bars remain prominent. This round fails the art gate.

Additional actual-car Blender inspection found conspicuous fit failures on the Titan and Viper. Titan's large vertical arch sheets stand clear of the purple body and its hood metal floats. Viper's arch sheets and roof assembly overwhelm its low curved body. Their ignored raw captures are `.evidence/gfx-03/round-4-titan.png` and `round-4-viper.png`. The structural asset test checks local files, named parts, UVs and broad bounds; it does not prove visible body contact. A shared arch recipe is insufficient for these car classes.
