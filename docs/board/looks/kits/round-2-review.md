The Blender-to-game axis correction puts side plating, front bar and roof cage near their intended positions. Independent review still scores likeness two, so round 3 changes the shape method. Five remaining differences: (1) plates mask wheel faces instead of following the arches; (2) the hood/roof slab still hides cabin glass; (3) separate combat weapon bars overpower the silhouette; (4) thin rails and crown lack grounded feet; and (5) the red stock body dominates instead of connected distressed metal panels. The combat bow/bumper bars belong to `combat-scene.js`, outside the new kit asset; they remain identified visual debt rather than an accepted match.

Round 3 will fit thin plate contours to actual body and axle/roof landmarks on all nine runtime models, cut around wheels and glass, and connect cage feet to body contact points. The painted metal UV should put wear on these fitted surfaces. This is a new contour/contact approach, not another size multiplier on the same rectangles. The all-car fit matrix and browser captures must include Titan and Viper. No beta promotion follows from round 2.
