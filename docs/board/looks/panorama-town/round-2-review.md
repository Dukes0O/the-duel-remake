# PANORAMA-TOWN, rounds 1 and 2 (14 October 2026)

Sheet: `round-2.jpg`: the old town from the round-3 Panorama sheet (left),
the new town at dusk from Conrod Straight (right), and a closer crop (below).
Captured by the native `p4-panorama` browser scenario (memory-only saves,
High quality, night amount 0.55 at the dusk capture).

- Round 1 (curved streets of the same shopfront kits): still small boxes;
  dropped before capture in favour of real houses.
- Round 1 houses: weatherboard houses, iron roofs, verandas, chimneys and the
  church read as a real town, but the lower street turns its back to the
  road, so few windows showed from Conrod Straight.
- Round 2: back windows on every house, larger panes, a third front window
  on wider houses, bigger lamp heads.

- Review fixes (same day): no two houses or lamps share ground, veranda
  posts reach the ground, chimneys rise through their roofs; recaptured.

Verdict (Claude): **pick.** A country town on the slope: two streets that
bend with the ground, 64 houses (about a quarter of two storeys) in eight wall and five
roof colours, a weatherboard church with a steeple on the square, 13 street
lamps, warm windows mixed with dark ones. Drawn as 11 instanced meshes plus
the church, so the whole town costs about 33 draw calls.
