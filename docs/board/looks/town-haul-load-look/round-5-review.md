# The haul's load, round 5 (TOWN-HAUL-LOAD-LOOK): Claude's verdict

27 November 2026, on fifty pictures taken in the game on the laptop (Performance quality, the scenario `look-haul-load`,
memory-only saves), each beside the old grey boxes where the same picture exists. The sheet is `round-5.jpg`. The
pictures are from the chase camera at the pile, on the road with a full stack and at the fallen pieces, as the card
asks, with close views for the review and one piece of every kind on the bed and on the ground.

**The verdict: passes. Ready for the Director's look.**

| What | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The load is real salvage | Passes | Ten kinds, each drawn in code as what the haul's rules call it: skin panels, wing rib, floor beam, seat frames, gear leg, wing spar; plate, angle iron, rail and bar for the Steel Run |
| It grows as the gang loads | Passes | Each piece comes down onto the heap as it is lifted on, and the heap keeps its shape as it grows and as it sheds |
| A full stack on the road | Passes | Eighteen pieces of salvage stand about 1.3 m over the deck; sixteen of steel about 0.7 m; nothing floats |
| Fallen pieces | Pass | They leave the stack turning in the air, land beside the road on the ground as it is drawn, and a burst bundle lies spread out |
| Nothing covers it | Passes | With the game's screens up the stack stands clear of the load line and the speed |
| Gritty, not toy-like | Passes | Dull primer, worn paint, grime and dust on aluminium; mill scale and rust on steel |

**Faults cured this round:** on sand the fallen pieces were laid at the rules' ground height, which the drawn ground
covers by a few centimetres, so seat frames and plate were buried; they now lie on the drawn ground. The steel's rust
covered every face; about half is dark mill scale now.

**Left as it is:** four pieces thrown off in one knock landed in one overlapping heap in this round's pictures. After
the sheet was made, pieces thrown off together were set to land one after another along the road (2.6 m apart);
checked by a further run of the salvage half, not on the sheet. From the chase camera 12 m back a flat piece on the
ground is small, as it would be; the moment that tells the player is the pieces in the air, the sound and the line
on the screen.
