# WW-ART-DAM-2 round 2: the old gear in its silt

Claude's verdict: **passes.** The job's regular ring of teeth was replaced with the traced old gear that passed on WW-ART-SEA, broken and eaten at the rim, tilted and half buried in one ragged heap of dark silt. From the road it reads as a great gear that has lain in the mud for a very long time.

The heap's edge shows against the pale review floor; on the dam's dark basin floor it will sit into the ground. The spillway is next.
