# FIRE-ART-FX, rounds 1 and 2 (14 October 2026)

Sheet: removed 15 October (the looks folder's 20 MB limit); the frames are
now seen in the game on `../fire-view/round-2.jpg`. It showed the atlas on a striped backing (left), and the frames
used together over the burnt and fireweed ground textures (right): a
burning edge with scorch, a smoke column leaning downwind, embers, steam and
a water drop. Recipe: `tools/art/fire-fx.py`, Kenney Particle Pack (CC0).

- Round 1: smoke nearly invisible (too dark and too faint); two flame frames
  read as candle flames; the ember was a lightning bolt.
- Round 2: brighter, thicker grey-brown smoke, darker at the base of a
  column; the pack's two textured fire blobs replace the candles; embers are
  a drawn cluster of glowing dots (every spark in the pack is lightning).

Verdict (Claude): **pick.** 20 frames in a 1024 atlas (flame 6, smoke 6,
steam 2, spray 2, ember 1, scorch 3), straight alpha, 570 KB, with
`fire-fx.json` naming each frame's pixel rectangle. The comparison over a
150 m still of Tremblant Woods comes with FIRE-VIEW, where the frames are
first drawn in the game.
