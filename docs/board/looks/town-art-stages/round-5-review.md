# TOWN-ART-STAGES round 5

## Codex visual check

The sequence now uses one fixed 3.5 m bay and the same 22 cm posts, 17 cm beams and 10 cm braces at every site size. Stage 1 has pegs, string, timber, sacks and a wheelbarrow. Stage 2 shows open foundations and spoil with no frame. Stages 3 and 4 keep the same frame; stage 4 adds full perimeter tin walls and a roof across the 4-by-4 footprint.

The sheet uses frames rendered from the exported GLB after importing it into an empty Blender scene. It shows road and 45-degree mayor views for all four stages and all street props. The person beside the street lamp is render-only. The close-up construction reference was generated for this round with the built-in image generator; only its crop appears on the sheet. Existing runtime material images and their credits were retained.

Reference prompt: “Photographic documentary reference for game art: a real rough timber building under construction in a dusty desert settlement, plain bright overcast daylight. Two storeys, about 7 m tall, 3.5 m structural bays, heavy square timber posts 22 cm thick, beams 17 cm, diagonal braces 10 cm. One wall partly clad in weathered grey-brown corrugated tin, other walls open frame. Broad plank scaffold along one side, ladder, bucket hoist, loose sheet stack and offcuts on dirt. Eye-height three-quarter photo, entire building visible, real proportions, worn and dusty, no people, no text, no logos, no cinematic lighting.”

The larger finished site reads as a broad, one-storey industrial shed at road height. Its roof reads clearly from above but looks repetitive across the full 4-by-4 footprint. Claude should judge whether the wide silhouette and repeated roof panels fit the town view. The ruin now has a taller charred frame, raised debris and collapsed tin, but its debris remains blocky at close range.
