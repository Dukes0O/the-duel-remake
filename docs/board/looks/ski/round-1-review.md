# SKI-ART, round 1 (24 October 2026)

Sheet: `round-1.jpg`: the player's skier in seven poses (stand, crouch, lean left and right, tuck, air,
tumble), the guide and four guests, a slalom pole, the two giant-slalom gates, the two timing arches, the
two pad flags, the kicker and the safety netting. Recipe: `tools/blender/ski.py`; 22 GLBs in
`public/assets/models/ski/` (2.6 MB). The bodies are the CC0 Quaternius base figure posed and thinned by the
rescue people's kit (about 3,900 triangles each); suit, helmet, goggles, boots, gloves, skis and poles are
original. The suit's materials are named `suit_primary` (the driver's colour), `suit_secondary` and
`helmet_shell` so the game recolours them; every pose has its origin under the boots on the snow.

What to judge: the figures read as real skiers (no cartoon proportions); the poses are distinct at game
size; the tuck is a proper downhill tuck; the guest and guide colours and the guide's radio; the gates'
panels; the arches' boards; the kicker's curve and wooden lip; the netting's mesh at a distance.

Open after round 1 (known): the netting is thin, as real netting is, so it fades at long range; the air
pose could tuck the legs more; the kicker's banks are plain snow without ski tracks.
