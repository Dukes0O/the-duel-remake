  suit, helmet and rounded goggles, boots, skis and poles); the tuck is a proper downhill tuck; the suit's
  materials are named for recolouring; every pose shares one origin under the boots.
- Known limits: faces are the base figure's blank faces; the suit has no seams or logos, only a belt, chest
  band and collar in the secondary colour; the kicker's snow is plain white with two tracks; the gate panels
  carry no numbers. Triangles: figures about 4,450, netting 7,700, the rest under 3,000.
