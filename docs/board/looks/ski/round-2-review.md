# SKI-ART, round 2 (the last; 24 October 2026)

Sheet: `round-2.jpg`: the player's skier in seven poses, the guide and four guests, a slalom pole, the
giant-slalom gates, the timing arches, the pad flags, the kicker and the safety netting. Recipe:
`tools/blender/ski.py`; 22 GLBs in `public/assets/models/ski/` (2.8 MB). The bodies are the CC0 Quaternius
base figure posed and thinned by the rescue people's kit (about 4,450 triangles each); suit, helmet, rounded
goggles, boots, gloves, skis and poles are original. The suit's materials are named `suit_primary` (the
driver's colour), `suit_secondary` and `helmet_shell` so the game recolours them; every pose has its origin
under the boots on the snow. The earlier sheets are removed.

Round 1's verdict and what changed for this sheet:
- **Kicker:** the timber lip is no longer a bench above the ramp: planks lie in the ramp's last 0.4 m with
  their tops flush with the snow, a board runs down the first 0.36 m of the face flush with it, and the
  sides are packed snow banks that rise from the ramp's edge, round off and slope out to the ground (lumpy,
  not a fold). Two ski tracks run down the ramp.
- **Air pose:** knees drawn up a little, the skis level and together, the arms forward for balance.
- **Netting:** stout posts, a thicker mesh on a 0.25 m grid, a solid orange band 0.34 m tall along the top
  and another along the foot, so a run holds up at range.
- Also from my own close look at round 1: the goggles are rounded lenses in a dark frame over the eyes, the
  chest band and belt are thinner, and the collar sits on the neck.

Known limits: faces are the base figure's blank faces; the suit has no seams or logos beyond a belt, chest
band and collar in the secondary colour; gate panels carry no numbers.
