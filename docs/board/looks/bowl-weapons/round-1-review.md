# Bowl weapons: round 1 (20 November 2026)

Sheet: `round-1.jpg`, taken by Claude in the real game: Last Truck Rolling on Easy, nights 3 and 5, memory-only saves,
with BOWL-WEAPONS's rules running. Drawn in code (`src/bowl/bowl-weapons-view.js`), no model files.

- **The hammer** reads best of the three. Its tell, the arm standing straight up over the cab, is plain from the
  driver's seat. Its hit is seen end on from behind, so it is carried by a flash, sparks and grit thrown up over the
  struck truck, and by that truck's bar. A miss buries the head in the dirt ahead with a ring of dust.
- **The saw** sits high enough that its top half shows over the bonnet. Cutting throws a tail of sparks back over the
  bonnet; after four seconds the disc glows red and smokes while it cools.
- **The launcher** plate snaps up on two air rams; the air gauge on the tank faces the driver and refills over the
  four seconds. Rust Rhino went over the wall and out in the drive.
- **The rivals**, each on a whole painted body: Rust Rhino's horn and plough, Big Kettle's boiler, chimney and hammer,
  Tin Lizard's tail and saw, Old Bess's spare tyre, tank and launcher, Dragon Wagon's stacks, snout and jet of fire.
  The Scrapdome's armour kits, spiked bumper and roof crossbow are no longer drawn on Show Night trucks; they were
  the clutter in Kyle's drive. The "bare chassis" from the hand drive did not appear again: every rival here has a
  whole body, also when its damage is at its worst.
- **Damage**: a panel hangs loose at three fifths, smoke at one fifth, fire at nothing.
- Still to judge by hand: the hammer's hit from the driver's seat in a moving fight; Kyle's and Gratian's try.

Verdict (Claude): keep, and switch on with Show Night.
