Sheet: `round-1.jpg` (103 KB). It shows six moments from the browser scenario
`deep-sirena`: the real game on the private QA page, with a memory-only save,
High quality and a software graphics device. Every raise and every payment
went through the game's own rules (credits 50,000 to 50,200: 100 for the badge
and 100 for the bonnet).

1. **First dive.** Ana calls ("Hello? You found the old ramp..."). The
   badge's glint flashes for half a second every 2.6 s, and this frame

2. **Sonar.** R pings: the ring with the blip dead ahead, "40 m" to the

   at a time and this machine runs the game at about two frames a second.

   it is small: a full bag is the model's 1.6 m.
4. **The bonnet goes up** under its bag; Ana: "Got it. My boat's got it."
5. **The Drop-off lip.** The door with its number 7 in the headlights,
   standing at the edge of open dark water: the dark band past the lip hides
   the flat sand beyond, so the lip reads as a drop.
6. **Garage.** The Sirena under a drawn sheet ("Needs the Lab"), above the
   sea-life log.
