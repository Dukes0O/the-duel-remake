## Result

Not passed. The face crop is cleaner and contains no sky in the straight-on
comparison. The arch, sea gate relief and reused Bowl wreck models are present.
The hut is now about 3.2 m by 4 m, with 2.5 m eaves. The GLB is within budget.

The asset still does not read as a real dam. From the wider view the face is
mostly a shallow photo surface with relief that does not fully track the photo.
The spillway vehicles still look like upright panels from the review angle, and
the chute is not visibly choked with wrecks. The salt wall shows its wet crust,
but its repeated bands still look like a texture tile. The old gear is partly
buried and tilted, though its exposed toothed arc still looks like a prop.

Would a person take it for a real dam? No.
