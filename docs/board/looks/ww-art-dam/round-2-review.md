## Five largest differences still visible

1. The basin needs steep rocky sides and a natural floor edge; the current brown shoulder beams look like braces.
2. The dam face remains too smooth and regular compared with the photo's massive sloped concrete and uneven runoff.
3. The lower rail gate and upper sea gate are separated by a large blank area.
4. The filling-shed room still reads as a patterned wall; the camera does not show the tanks as a row of industrial vessels.
5. The gatehouse and wrecked spillway appear tiny in the context view, with little terrain to explain their placement.

## Round 3 plan, settled before recipe edits

- Replace the diagonal shoulder beams and rectangular basin slab with an irregular basin floor and rocky side shoulders. Keep the basin about 150 m across and let its hills close the view.
- Keep the named dam parts and story offsets. Add no ocean or content on the far side.
- Reframe the filling-shed picture from the train path toward the tanks and salted back wall so the room's function is clear.
- Keep the required 40 m and 200 m views, then reimport and measure the delivered GLB again.
