## Five largest differences from the photo

1. The wall face is much too pale and uniform; the photo has dark, irregular water streaks and a distinct old waterline.
2. The center rail gate is absent from the 40 m view, while the high sea gate is the only obvious opening in that view.
3. The concrete lacks strong, varied buttress faces and broken edges, so the wall reads as a flat sheet.
4. The filling-shed view reads as a close concrete surface with narrow bands, not as a train-sized room with tanks, pipes and a salt-crusted back wall.
5. The shots do not establish the dam's scale against the basin, gatehouse or wreck-choked spillway.

## Round 2 plan, settled before recipe edits

- Deepen the concrete texture and make the high waterline and wet streaks broader, darker and less regular.
- Reframe the 40 m view from the shed entrance toward the wall so the rail gate, face and crest gate have a clear vertical order. Reframe the shed view from a train-cab position down the full room, with tanks and pipes visibly separated from the salt wall.
- Add more distinct face seams and damaged concrete around the foot, while keeping the wall's existing basin-scale silhouette and named part structure.
- Add a context view showing the gatehouse pad and wrecked spillway; keep the two required distance views and interior view on the same sheet.
- Re-render from the exported GLB, then recheck the delivered mesh and file budgets.
