These picks approve donors, not the finished art. Most previews are clean or stylized. All need a believable scale, restrained colours and worn materials before they fit The Duel.

Three open pieces: the Lantern Tower is a proposed assembly; no ready-made 10 m avalanche gallery was verified; the yeti's roar, chest beat and lope clips remain unverified. Those are shown below before any selection.
