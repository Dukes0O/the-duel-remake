# The road machines and the worked ground, round 2

Sheet: `round-2.jpg`. Pictures from the real game, driven with real keys in a visible window, 19 November 2026.

## Changed since round 1

Steam off the new surface behind the paver's screed. The heap ahead of the blade is the same dark earth as the fill it
becomes. Stones are pressed into rolled ground. The mat's grit is fine. The roller is lifted over the scraped stretch, so
cut ground stays cut. A second strip beside the first shows loose fill that was never rolled, and a surface laid on it.

## Verdict (Claude): better than round 1, one more round

- **Better.** Rolled ground reads as a pressed, pale band with a firm edge beside the dark loose clods round it. The
  paved mat is an even dark surface with a straight edge and no blobs. Steam shows from behind the paver. Both strips
  together show every state in one picture from above.
- **Short of right.** The cracks in a surface laid on soft fill still run in loops like worm trails; a real surface on a
  soft base breaks into small plates. Two pictures on this sheet missed their subject: the laptop ran faster this time,
  the dozer had already spread its whole load, and the "loose fill" picture from above shows bare ground, as do the two
  pictures beside the dozer. The play's pictures are now taken by what the machine carries and where the fill lies, not
  by the clock.
