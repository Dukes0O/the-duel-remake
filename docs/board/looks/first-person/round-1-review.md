## Five priorities for round 2

1. Replace the thin conical forearms with fuller anatomical wrists and sleeves.
   Add thick cuffs, rolled folds, wraps and crew-specific details from the sheets.
2. Give gloves a fuller palm, knuckles, bent gripping fingers and visible panels.
   Current digits are thin, and the exposed thumbs look like short straight rods.
3. Refine tool shapes. The wrench has angular horn-like jaws and a heavy block
   shape; match the adjustable steel jaw, bevels and rack. The RPG's large rear
   bell and flat radial bore dominate its silhouette. Improve barrel fittings,
   grip/trigger detail and a recessed dark bore while retaining the sightline.
4. Separate material identities. Vertical grain and gold/brown steel dominate
   the game views. Use restrained woven cloth, leather panels, dark steel and
   localized chipped teal/yellow paint. Avoid uniform procedural streaks.
5. Improve functional contact: hands wrap securely around both handles, the
   reload hand supports the rocket through insertion, and repair poses show
   a working wrist/tool action. Review additional bounded action phases.
