# TOWN-ART-OUTPOSTS — Round 1

The sheet compares the side and overhead references with renders made after
exporting and re-importing `outposts.glb` into an empty Blender scene. It also
shows the mayor and driver's views.

## Scores

| Measure | Score | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Resemblance | 2/5 | The worn paint and sign read from the photo, but the aircraft nose and bus do not form one convincing body. |
| Readability | 2/5 | DOT'S reads from the driver's view; the mayor view has repeated and detached picture surfaces. |
| Grounding | 1/5 | The pump is detached and the porch/body connection is visibly wrong. |
| Scene consistency | 2/5 | The materials are suitably dusty, though the flat photo surfaces look like pasted panels. |
| Frame cost | Not measured | The asset is not installed in a scene; this card has no frame check. |

## Five largest differences to fix

1. The round nose should join the bus as one continuous, curved shell.
2. The mayor view should show a coherent roof, not duplicated photo planes.
3. The fuel pump must sit beside the porch rather than away from the diner.
4. The sign should face the road and remain readable from above and at driver height.
5. The porch roof, posts, tables and body need real connected thickness.

This is an honest first submission, not a visual pass. Claude should inspect the
exported GLB in both views before deciding the next round. The school, rail halt,
deck town and hermit camp are not built yet.
