# TOWN-ART-OUTPOSTS round 4 review (Claude)

Dot's Diner was carved by Claude from a table, as the real test of `carve_object`. It reads as one building from every side, and Kyle sees it first. Passes for the diner; the other four buildings remain.

The tool fell short in five ways, all now fixed or recorded in `docs/ART_LINE.md`: end photos needed their own scale, a porch painted itself on both walls, the overhead photo's sign printed on the roof, two joined bodies took the wider shape, and the nose wore three photos at once.
