# TOWN-ART-WORKS salvage yard, round 1 (Claude, 19 November 2026)

**Verdict: passes.** A working yard, 22.7 m square inside its 3 by 3 plot, 8,812 triangles of
9,000.

- **The gang's shed** is carved from its four photos and matches them on the long side, the
  front and the roof: a single-slope roof of mismatched rusted tin, the plank door to the road,
  the shuttered window. The stove pipe stands at a different place in each photo, so it is cut
  from every mask and built as its own piece.
- **Walls** on three sides and between the bays are traced from the sleeper-wall photo, each
  length starting at a different place in the photo and a little taller or shorter.
- **Five sorted bays** along the back: tyres, steel beams and plate, tin sheets, pipe, drums.
- **The unsorted heap** by the gate wears an overhead photo of scrap, under a tripod hoist of
  pipe with a chain block. Things pulled from it lie between heap and bays.
- **Named parts:** `salvage-yard:shed`, `:walls`, `:bays`, `:heap`, `:hoist`, `:floor`,
  `:gate-left` and `:gate-right` (each turning on its hinge post), `:lamp-pole` and `:lamp`.

Fixed before this round was counted: the shed came out as a cushion at a coarse carving grain
(0.12 m) and stood 6 cm off the floor; at 0.07 m its corners are rounded by a hand's width.
The heaps showed pale wedges of the studio's floor at their feet until their pictures were
taken from the middle three-quarters of each photo.

Short of the brief: the big heap is one smooth mound wearing its photo, which reads from the
mayor's camera and looks draped from inside the yard; the yard floor is dark and even; the
shed's front is a little wider for its height than its photo.
