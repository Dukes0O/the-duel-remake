water colour), with its grey shape below for a creature not yet found. 13
pictures, 2.8 to 18 KB each (116 KB in all), rendered from DEEP-ART-SRC's
working models by `tools/scenarios/deep-log-pictures.mjs` (the look page's own
Three.js, transparent background, each model fitted to about 85% of the
frame). The otter has no picture: its role is held (DEEP-ART-SRC), so its
card shows the name and fact only.

- **Sea lion** is flat and dark brown, with little face.
- **Bat ray** is seen from above so its wings read. One wing tip is long and
