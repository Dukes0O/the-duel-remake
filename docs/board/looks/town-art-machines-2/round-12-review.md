# TOWN-ART-MACHINES-2 — round 12: crane truck

## Result

Does not pass. The side view reads as a crane truck, and the open lattice boom matches its source image. The rear view has a strong projection seam through the cab and turntable. Do not accept this as finished art.

## Scores

- Resemblance: 2/5
- Readability: 3/5
- Grounding: 4/5
- Scene consistency: 3/5
- Frame cost: 4/5 by asset budget only; no frame check is allowed for an art round.

## Five main differences

1. The rear projection stretches across the cab and turntable, with a large visible seam.
2. The turntable deck and counterweight are smooth blocks; the reference has layered, worn steel parts.
3. The yellow outrigger beams are too clean and box-like. Their hydraulic rams need more metal detail.
4. The hook is a thin cable and small hook. The reference shows a larger pulley and hook assembly.
5. Small crane details are missing or blurred in the front, rear and overhead views.

The side body, cab and photo-traced lattice boom are the strongest parts. The working pose raises the boom and lowers the hook. The scene reads correctly, but the end views and built pieces need more work before a pass.
