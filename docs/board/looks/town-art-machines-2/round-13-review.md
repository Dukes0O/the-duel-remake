# TOWN-ART-MACHINES-2 — round 13: crane truck

## Result

This is an improvement, but it does not pass yet. The round-scaled side, front,
rear and overhead views are shown beside the same source photos. The turntable
is a lathed part wearing the overhead photo, with the boom pivot tower joined
to it. The outriggers are box beams with vertical jacks and ground pads. The
mixer truck was not changed.

The rear projection seam remains obvious through the upper body. The pivot
tower is dark and covers much of the photographed turntable face from above.
The source and model still disagree at the rear cab and deck. The support legs
read as stabilizers, though their paint and detail are simpler than the photo.
