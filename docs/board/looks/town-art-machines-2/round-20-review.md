# TOWN-ART-MACHINES-2 ? round 20: roller

First roller round. The old block roller pieces were replaced by a `carve_object` body, built open canopy, seat and steering wheel, two `tyre` wheels, and a separate photo-textured steel drum on `roller:drum`. The work render applies a drum rotation. Source references are the same-object four-view set and a separate drum photo.

The sheet and exported render are **not a pass**. The carved body and built parts do not yet assemble into a convincing roller. The side, front, rear and overhead renders show large gaps and clipping; rest and work poses are small and dark, and the rotation is hard to judge. The brown and gray discs in the side render appear detached from the engine/frame. The reference photos agree with one another, but the render camera scale and framing do not produce a fair, fully visible comparison.

Claude: inspect whether the body carve axes and camera orientation are correct; whether canopy posts, tyres and drum sit against the body/yoke; whether the drum texture wraps its cylindrical surface; whether the pivot turns visibly while the drum stays grounded; and whether a rerender with matching orthographic framing changes the verdict.
