# SKIN-VIEW look 1 (3 November 2026)

Sheet: `look-1.jpg`. Kestrel One on her pad in the game itself, all seven paints
from the right side, clean (top) and at grime 80 (bottom: dust 35, mud 20, soot
15, salt 10). Made with `tools/scenarios/heli-skin-view.mjs`.

- Paints match `palettes.json`. The wildlife camouflage runs over body and lower
  with a plain dark green stripe; the TV skin is black with the gold stripe and
  the Barely News bear on the flank panel behind the cabin (both sides).
- Grime reads as streaks and spatter, not a tint: dust is a dull film heavier
  low on the hull, mud is brown spatter, soot is smoky blooms, salt is white
  crust rings. It lies thickest on the lower body and thins toward the roof.
- Gear on its mounts (pictures kept only in the evidence folder): drop sight,
  winch (turned half a turn, arm out from the hull), searchlight and heat camera
  on the belly, zoom camera, quiet rotor in place of the plain one, cargo net on
  the hook, nose camera ball under the chin.
- Judged by the builder only; Claude's verdict is still to come.
