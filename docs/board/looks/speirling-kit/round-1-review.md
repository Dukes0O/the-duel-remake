# SPEIRLING-KIT round 1

[Picture sheet](round-1.jpg), 481,365 bytes. Actual game captures, private
memory-only saves: Scrapper, Raider and Warlord, front three-quarter, side
and rear three-quarter, each in High and Performance. The black nose wedge
and roof weapon are combat equipment, not kit parts.

## Fit and appearance

- Scrapper: four breakable plates above the wheel crowns, low sill sheets,
  a nose bar and two short rear cage stanchions. No fake electric-car exhaust.
- Raider: overlapping sill armour, side saws, rails outside the canopy and
  a rear mounting ring. Nothing crosses the glass.
- Warlord: rust shell taken from the actual painted body, with openings cut
  around the canopy and both rear fans; rail teeth and a rear crossbar.
  The carbon wing, lights, wheels and fan grilles stay readable.

The new fit test checks every tier against production glass triangles,
with 12 mm clearance, and protects the fan outlets and lower-tier wheel
discs. Independent surface review checked 158 fan rays and 6,179 candidate
canopy edge segments, with no blocked fans or glass crossings. The shell
retains the original body's wheel arches. The kit has 9,201 triangles,
below the existing 12,000 limit, and all required UV checks pass.

Builder assessment: three distinct steps in the established kit style;
readability, grounding and scene consistency 4/5. The small Scrapper plates
read less strongly than the full Warlord shell, as on the other low cars.
No frame timing: this is an art round for a dev-only car.

Independent reviewer: PASS, no remaining findings. All 18 sheet tiles match
their stated tier, angle and quality. The review caught one stale Performance
image; waiting for shader warmup and painted frames fixed it, and the sheet
was replaced. One art round used; Director's art acceptance remains at
integration. The lab switch stays dev.
