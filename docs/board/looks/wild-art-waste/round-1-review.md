# WILD-ART-WASTE, round 1

Sheet: `round-1.jpg` (488,238 bytes), all six animals. Each animal has two rows: the
photograph of the real animal beside close views of the exported model, then what a player
sees: 1:1 crops of a 1920 x 1080 frame from a car (the race camera's 54 degrees, 1.6 m up) at
10 m and 30 m, and for the three town animals from the town's camera (48 degrees, 60 m up,
looking down at 45 degrees). Two drafts were looked at and fixed before this sheet; they are
not rounds.

## Claude's verdict (builder and judge on this card)

| Animal | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goat | Not yet | The outline is a goat's (hay belly, beard, horns that sweep back and out, upturned tail), and it reads as one at 10 m and 30 m. The coat does not match the photograph: the forequarters should be near black and come out grey-brown, because the material's shine lays a grey veil over a dark coat. Front legs too thin; horns too thick along their length. |
| Chicken | Not yet | Reads as a hen at 10 m. Close up the neck is as thick as a bowling pin and the head is lost on it; the plumage is one even brown where the photograph has black with rust. |
| Camp dog | Not yet, close | The outline is right against the photograph: lean, deep chest, tucked belly, pricked ears, tail curled over the back. The paws were narrowed too far and read as clubs. |
| Turkey vulture | Not yet | Standing, it is a vulture: dark hunched body, small red head, pale legs. The bill is a long white spike (an ibis's). From below the wing shows no silver flight feathers, the mark of the bird in the air; the wing is also too narrow. |
| Desert monitor | Not yet | The outline is a monitor's (long neck, low wide body, whip tail, sprawled legs). The pattern is soft zebra stripes; the photograph has broken dark bands with rows of small yellow spots. The legs are stiff pillars. |
| Scorpion | PASS | Carapace and plates, eight jointed legs with high knees, arms with swollen claws, five-bead tail with bulb and dark sting, in the straw and olive of the photographs. Unmistakable at 5 m; at 10 m it is a pale shape about 45 pixels long with the raised tail readable, which is what the card asks. |
