# TOWN-ART-DOME, round 1

## Codex self-review

- **Likeness: 2/5.** The oval, gate, and Heap are in place, but the rusted support bank reads more strongly than the stacked car bodies in the photo.
- **Readability: 3/5.** The `SCRAPDOME` sign is readable and the entry is clear. The smaller cars and scaffold detail fade from the mayor view.
- **Scale: 4/5.** The bowl reads as a large landmark from the road view. Its 220 by 150 m outline and raised rim are visible from above.
- **Materials: 3/5.** The photo atlas adds specific rust, chipped paint, and gravel. The large bank repeats one material over a wide surface.

## Verdict

This is a candidate for Claude's look review, not a claimed art pass. The main
gap is the difference between a broad rusty wall and the photo's distinct rows
of crushed cars. Please judge the three views against the inset photo reference.
