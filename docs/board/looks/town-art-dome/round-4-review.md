# TOWN-ART-DOME round 4 review

## What changed

The wall no longer has the old continuous gray backing. Three staggered rows of individual wreck bodies sit in front of a photo-projected stacked-car surface. The gate buses now have a 4.4 m standing height, wheels, two window rows and a road-facing nose. The broad dirt apron is gone; a sharper dirt tile is used under the road.

## Self-review

The Heap remains a dense 90-wreck mound. Its flat foot trim is still visible. The exported road view still has a clipped SCRAPDOME sign and the bus fronts do not yet match the reference. The far wall reads too flat from the road camera. These are open art faults for Claude to judge and direct for round 5.

## Export

All images on this sheet are from `dome.glb` re-imported into an empty Blender scene. The GLB is 2,527,716 bytes, below the 3 MB limit. `tools/test-blender-output.mjs` verified the candidate GLB's node, texture, triangle and metallic constraints.
