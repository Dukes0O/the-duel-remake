# TOWN-ART-WORKS — Round 1

## Builder self-review

This is the one submitted art round. The sheet shows both requested angles for
all 20 building IDs, a generated salvage-yard reference crop, and an 8 m scale
inset with the accepted town render's car and person.

- **Likeness: 3/5.** The yard, warehouse, saw, kiln and stockpiles read as their
  intended jobs. The generated reference has much richer clutter and surface
  detail than the models.
- **Readability: 4/5.** The two views distinguish the small tools and material
  heaps, and the larger buildings have separate silhouettes.
- **Grounding: 4/5.** Models sit on the same sand plane; the family uses the
  named cell sizes and the sheet includes the 8 m scale reference.
- **Material and finish: 3/5.** The shared photo atlas gives sharp tin, steel
  and sand. Brick and plank still look too even, and the sheet's bright sand
  makes some small pieces look simple.
