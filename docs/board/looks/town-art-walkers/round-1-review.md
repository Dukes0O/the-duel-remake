# TOWN-ART-WALKERS — round 1 review

## Scores

- Human proportions against the photo: **2/5**. The body still reads too broad and angular beside the slim real reference.
- Worn material read: **3/5**. The existing patched-canvas tile and salvage colors give useful variation, though the small geometry limits fabric detail.
- People and outfit readability at distance: **3/5**. Hats, coats, overalls, the trader's pack and Vane's blue mark separate the cast; faces and limbs remain coarse.
- Render and comparison: **4/5**. Bright neutral light, full figures and an exported-GLB import make the comparison easy to judge.

## Notes

The model uses the town's existing fabric and salvage pictures. It is 650 KB. Readback counts are 348–448 triangles per person, with all thirteen IDs and both swing-leg parts present.

The generated photo is a documentary-style adult survivor in a patched dust coat and overalls, standing on dirt in bright overcast daylight. The photo crop appears only on this sheet. The render is the exported GLB imported into an empty Blender scene.

The model still looks stylized beside the photo, especially at the shoulders, arms and face. I would not call the photo match finished. Claude: please judge whether this is useful as a low-cost town-distance figure or needs another art round.
