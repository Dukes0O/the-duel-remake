## Changed since round 1 (Claude's notes)

- One tall beam at a time. Not carrying: only loaded fuel pads show a warm white
  beam, no depot shows one. Carrying: only your own depot shows a beam, in its
  colour; rival depots are a coloured disc and ring on the ground. A beam
  brightens and pulses within 40 m.
- The floating 3D arrow is gone. A large arrow in the depot colour now sits
  top-centre on the screen, turned to the way the car faces, with the colour
  name and distance (`CYAN DEPOT · 122 m`), whenever you carry fuel.
- Walls: the Scrapdome outer wall now stands at the drawn face, 0.5 m from a
  car at the floor edge (was 1.5 m); the Pit wall and its drawn hulk wall were
  moved in by the same amount (gap 0.44 m, was 0.98 m).

## Matters of taste (not changed)

- The arrow is large and sits over the middle of the top of the view; it can be
  made smaller if it crowds the callouts.
- Salt Flats wall containers read as dark, blocky boxes (unchanged art).
- The inner (Heap) wall of the Scrapdome stays at 21 m: it bends the other way
  and a long car would snag a closer one.
