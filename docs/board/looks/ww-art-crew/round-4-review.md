# WW-ART-CREW round 4 review

Date: 6 November 2026
Sheet: `round-4.jpg`
Status: **Gerald rendered; harpoon and piano still pending.**

Gerald is one photo-mapped relief made from the overhead mask. It includes the
head, four feet, toes and tail. Nose-to-hip measures 0.30 m; the tail is about
as long again. The low side render follows the relief recipe's 45 mm spine.

| View | Photo mean brightness | Reimported GLB mean brightness |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Overhead | 130.0 | 121.7 |
| Low side | 127.8 | 120.4 |

Both views are within 7% of their reference brightness. The overhead reads as
a real lizard with its own skin. The low side reads as a shallow photographic
relief because of the specified 45 mm maximum height; Claude should judge that
view against the side photo. I would not mistake the overhead for a toy. The
side view is too shallow to read as a whole animal without the paired overhead
view.

The sheet contains Gerald only so far. The remaining two rows will be added to
the same round 4 sheet after their GLBs are rendered and inspected.
