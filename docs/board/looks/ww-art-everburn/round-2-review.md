## Changes

The recipe now builds an approximately 9 m flare tower with welded collars,
guy wires, a ladder and rest platforms. The well has surface rust patches,
weld beads, pipe attachments and a heavier clamped cap. The burning state has
orange and yellow layers, a pale core and separate flame tongues. The ground
uses an uneven scar and mismatched pipe and plate scraps. The scale worker is
present only in the review renders and is excluded from the runtime GLB.

The GLB is 954,064 bytes and 13,044 triangles. It stays under the 1 MB and
20,000-triangle limits.
