# Scarab, round 3

The accepted round-two body proportions, open frame, tyre size and stance stay
in place. This round uses a generated daylight photo and close-up photographic
material textures, then compares the same buggy views beside the photo.

## Changes

- Darkened the welded tube finish and tiled the generated oily rust texture.
- Added the generated dirt-caked rubber color texture. The integrated paddle
  lugs have wider swept faces and sharper separation; the tyre size and wheel
  centers remain stable.
- Kept four coil-over shocks and made the springs easier to see at each corner.
- Added restraint straps and seat bolsters, carburetor intakes, filter ribs,
  ignition leads and additional oil-pan detail.
- Applied dust to the lower suspension links and skid plates.
- Kept the actual crew model in the scale view only; it is not exported.

## Self-check

The round-three sheet has the generated reference crop beside front three-
quarter, rear three-quarter, side, top and scale views. The overall stance still
reads as the same long-travel buggy. The rust and ground-in tyre dirt now read at
sheet size, and the paddle edges and coil springs are visible. Blender export:
24,570 triangles and 1,773,568 bytes, within the card's 25,000 triangle and 8 MB
limits. The eight vehicle sockets and +Z forward / +Y up convention are kept.

This is a Blender art candidate, not an in-game capture. Claude should compare
the round-three sheet with the daylight reference, with extra attention to
whether the rust is dark and oily enough, the paddles read as real sand tyres,
the four coil-overs and rear engine remain legible, and the low dust belongs on
the vehicle. No browser or in-game look verdict is claimed.
