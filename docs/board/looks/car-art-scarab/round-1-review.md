# Scarab, round 1

One candidate model and one five-view Blender contact sheet for Claude's art verdict. The card did not include a separate reference image, so these provisional scores compare the render with the card's written brief. The model is not installed in game; its in-game speed and frame cost have not been measured.

The sheet shows front three-quarter, rear three-quarter, side, top and beside-person views. The model uses an open welded tube cage, exposed rear engine, long-travel arms and springs, paddle tyres, partial battered plates and a whip flag. Blender export: 10,354 triangles, 913,792 bytes. The listed 2.05 x 3.82 x 1.72 m is the buggy body envelope; the whip flag rises above it. All eight vehicle socket names are recorded in the export manifest.

## Scores

- Resemblance to the written brief: **3/5**. The silhouette and visible rear engine match; surface wear is still light and the chassis reads as a clean assembly.
- Readability at racing speed: **3/5**. The large tyres and open cage read, while the arms, springs and paddle lugs crowd together at this distance.
- Grounding: **3/5**. Tyres meet the neutral studio floor, but there is no in-game terrain or suspension test.
- Consistency with the Wasteland scene: **4/5**. Oxidized tube, dull enamel, dark rubber and mismatched plates fit the hand-built salvage direction.
- Frame cost: **3/5, provisional**. Triangle count is within budget; runtime draw calls and frame cost are unmeasured because no game loader exists yet.

## Biggest differences to address

1. Add chipped enamel, oily staining and localized rust around joints; the current materials look too evenly clean.
2. Strengthen the buggy's believable load-bearing story. Some diagonal arms and the front skid plate overlap visually in the three-quarter views.
3. Separate paddle lugs from the tire silhouette with clearer spacing and a more directional, curved paddle profile.
4. Make the rear engine more legible from the rear three-quarter view; its cylinders and exhausts blend into the frame.
5. Improve the scale-person silhouette in the comparison view so the body and limbs read clearly beside the buggy.

## Claude: please review

Judge whether the silhouette feels like a long-travel desert buggy, whether the frame, engine and suspension look physically welded and serviceable, whether the wear feels lived-in rather than painted on, and whether the paddle tyres and sand flag read at a glance. Please also decide whether to keep these provisional scores or change the next round's priorities.
