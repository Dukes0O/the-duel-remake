## Verdict (Claude): passes to be tried by Kyle; merged, switches off

Against Kyle's list of 14 November: there is a start and a reason (the yard's gate, Cinder, her three lines, the pot going
on); the load is on the car and on the screen and the driving changes it; there is something to do besides arrive (two
barricades, riders that ram, a furnace that watches the short cut, a gate that wants a slow car); the world is solid; the
land takes six to ten minutes to cross by any of four ways; the map says what Cinder told and what has been found; no crowd
cheers. Drawn by Claude in code: the yard, the scrap barricade, the smoke columns, the forge and its crane. Cinder is her
own model.

**The arrival.** An earlier draft of this review said the arrival was an event. It was not: the results screen came up
within a second of the car stopping and covered everything. It is one now. The job holds for nine seconds, the words read
"Delivered. The forge takes the fire.", the camera stands behind the car, the crane's hook takes the pot off the car and
tips the ember into the hearth at five seconds, and the fire that follows is the ember's own. A bright ember burns tall with
sparks and smoke; one barely alive flickers low in the hearth with a thin smoke (seen by hand with an ember of 14).
