# Everburn remade, round 2: the yard on the land (job 1's start)

Sheet: `round-2.jpg` (40 m inside the east gate, where job 1 starts; looking back from the East Road, 65 m out; from 150 m,
off the road; from 500 m). Pictures from the real game in a visible window, on the QA page with memory-only saves, Performance,
`scablands-realism` on, 15 November 2026, with the yard put into the scene by hand for the pictures. Drawn by Claude in code
(`src/world/scablands-yard-look.js`): no model files.

## Verdict (Claude): passes as the place job 1 starts in; not yet in the game

From the car's seat at the start there is a gate ahead with the yard's name on a steel plate, two towers of stacked containers,
two hanging lamps and a wall of scrap sheet running off to both sides; the East Road runs out through the gate. Looking back
from the road the yard is a walled place with a dome and a mast, not a point on a plain. That answers Kyle's "no start point".

## What it is

A ring of scrap wall 170 m across round the meeting of the six roads, each sheet its own height, lean and colour, with
containers standing in the wall and an opening for each road; the east gate (12 m between its towers, 5.8 m clear under a truss);
a dome of girder ribs skinned in patched sheet with panels missing; a lattice mast 40 m tall with what is left of a flag.
About 10,000 triangles in one piece, and a second piece for the name plate. The solid shapes come from the same plan
(`yardPlan().solids`).

## Short of right, for a later round

- The shaded side of the wall is dark; the sunlit side is a little pale. One more pass at the paint.
- Nothing stands inside the wall but the dome and the mast: no stacked wrecks, drums, tyres or lamps on poles.
- From 2 km only the mast shows. The smoke of Cinder's brazier (job 1, step 2c) is what will mark the yard from far off.
- The first colours were drawn too dark on Claude's test page and then too pale in the game's sun: judged in the game from now.
