# The Scablands as a place: round 1 (12 November 2026)

Sheet: `round-1.jpg`: three fixed viewpoints in the game, before on the left and after on the right: Tin Town from
the road, the gate, and the Boneyard's planes. Built and photographed by a Sonnet helper; judged by Claude.

- Before: Tin Town and the rocks drew white, because their model files carried no colours. The Boneyard's planes
  stood on end against a 180 m slab of dirt, because the model was exported turned a quarter turn. The ground was one
  flat colour.
- After: every place has its colours, with rust, runs, soot and dust drawn over them in code. The planes lie flat.
  The ground has drifts of pale and dark sand, cracked earth, gravel, and rock on steep ground. The road is a flat
  ribbon on the land in place of a tube that stood up as walls. Tin Town has a gate with its name on it.
- The far ground no longer pokes through the near ground.

What Claude sees still wrong:

- The cliffs were striped like a tiger in these pictures (the strata were too even and too strong). Claude softened
  and bent them after the pictures were taken; the next sheet must confirm it.
- A pale band of sand ripples crossed the foreground at a low angle. The ripples now fade where they would be finer
  than the screen can draw; also to be confirmed.
- Tin Town is small under a very large cliff: a few buildings, about as prominent as its own gate.
- The gate and fence have no collision; cars drive through the posts.

Verdict (Claude): keep. This is the largest single improvement to the Scablands so far, and it came from three
plain mistakes being put right plus two pieces of drawing code.
