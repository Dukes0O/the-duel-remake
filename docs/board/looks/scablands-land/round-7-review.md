## Verdict (Claude): merged, behind switches that are off

- **The small map can be used.** It sits on a dark panel, shows the ground, the roads, the river
  and the named crossings round the car, turns the car's arrow with the car, and says "TIN TOWN
  2.9 km" with an arrow when the town is off the map.
- **The big map shows the whole land** with every place, road, crossing and kind of ground
  named, and lists four ways to Tin Town with the length, the ground and who is on each, worked
  out from the land's own data.
- **It is one picture of the land made from the ground's own data**, so it follows the land when
  the land changes. It reads as a worn field map, not a toy.
- **Changed by Claude:** the furnace's sight was a heavy red hatch that hid the ground under
  half the small map near the bridge. It is a light wash with a dashed edge now.

Not right yet:

- **Road names crowd the crossings' names** in four places on the big map (the Salt Road over
  the Pipe, the Boneyard Road over the Halfway Camp and the Wash, the Mall road over the South
  Ford).
- **The salt pan and the scab plates have edges like a box** on the big map. That is the land's
  own outline for them, and it is redrawn with natural edges in slice 2c.
- **The rises read as soft pebbles.** They become hills in slice 2c.
- **The ways list tells the player everything before any of it is learnt** (the held bridge, the
  barricades, the furnace's sight). What a player is told at the start is settled with the
  job's lead-up, in EVERBURN-REMAKE.
- **A job with several places marks only the next one** (the three lamps, the five flags). The
  old numbered rings went with the old map; several markers come back when those jobs are
  remade.
- **The picture fills in from the top during the first second of a job.**
- A burnt barricade stays drawn, because nothing records that it burnt.
