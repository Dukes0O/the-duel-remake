## Verdict (Claude): the rules are merged; the faces are not drawn yet

- **Things stop a car.** The barricade, the wreck and the gate post each hold the car where it
  met them. This is what Kyle asked for: nothing drawn can be driven through.
- **The Girder Bridge and the Pipe read as crossings now.** The bridge's frames stand well over
  the car and the Pipe is a rusted pipe on trestles with a deck beside it.
- **A fall shows.** The car after the drop into the bed is burning.

Not right yet:

- **The four scarps read as a dark or pale band across the land, not as broken rock.** A face
  12 to 20 m high is drawn with the plain's own cells (23 by 17 m), which cannot hold its shape.
  The faces get cells and a rock surface of their own in slice 2c, with the mesas.
- **The Steps do not read as steps.** From the plates they are a thin line with a small frame
  for the marked way. Slice 2c.
- **A bank seen from the bed is a flat pale band.** It does not say "you cannot climb here".
- **The boulder at the well is a faceted lump** and reads as a toy. Step 3 of the realism trial
  draws the boulders.
- **Tin Town's gate fence leaves one opening and an alley 2 to 3 m wide**, so a car can only
  stop at the town. The town's front is opened up when its job is remade.
- These pictures are in the old light. The scarps, the Steps and a bank are pictured again in
  the realism trial's light when slice 2c draws them.
