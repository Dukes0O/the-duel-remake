# Scablands land, round 5: regions, roads and the dry river

Sheet: `round-5.jpg` (twelve pictures from the real game in a visible browser window on the QA
page with memory-only saves, Performance). Taken 14 November 2026 with scenario
`tools/scenarios/scablands-regions.mjs` (run with `SCABLANDS_SHEET=1`), after Claude's change to
the burnt ground. Built by a Sonnet helper for Claude; reviewed by Claude the same day.

## What the pictures show

Top row: the scab plates, the salt pan, the river bed between its banks, the sand. Middle row:
the burn, the Girder Bridge on the East Road, the Pipe on the Salt Road, the Steps. Bottom row:
the East Road, and three ways seen from above (the road over the bridge, the scab plates at the
Steps, the river bed at the Wash).

## Verdict (Claude): merged as the land's base, behind switches that are off

- **Each ground reads as a different ground at a glance.** Black broken plates, white salt with a
  crust, grey bed, pale sand, ochre flats. The cracks no longer repeat as a grid.
- **The roads read as roads:** graded dirt with ruts and a lighter crown, not a dark band.
- **The river reads as a cut in the land** from the ground and from above, and the road is seen
  to cross it on a bridge.
- **The burn was wrong and is changed.** The helper drew glowing orange cracks, which read as
  lava. Claude redrew it as burnt ground: black soot, grey ash drifted over it, pale ash in the
  cracks, nothing glowing. It is better and still too much of a net of lines; it is drawn again
  with the vents when the Everburn's job is remade.

Not right yet, each with the slice that owns it:

- **Every picture is still a flat plain with one thing in it.** Nothing stands beside a road
  (slice 3) and the land has no hills (slice 2c, which Kyle asked for on seeing this).
- **The small map is a bent line lying on the ground** with no panel. Kyle: "entirely
  ridiculous". Slice 4, started beside slice 2b.
- **The Steps are not drawn as steps** and have no way down marked (slice 2b).
- **The sand is a flat pale sheet** with nothing to steer by (dunes, slice 2c).
- **From above the river's banks look like wood grain** and the bed has long diagonal stripes.
  A player does not see the land from there, but the big map will; the banks are redrawn with
  the scarps in slice 2b.
- **The far ridge is still a flat blue shape.**
- **The Girder Bridge and the Pipe are small and plain** for things a road is named after
  (slice 3).

Not a look matter, and recorded on the card: in this slice a car still crosses the river
anywhere and passes through every building.
