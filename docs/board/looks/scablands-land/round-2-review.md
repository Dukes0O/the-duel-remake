# The Scablands as a place: round 2, Tin Town and its burning gate (12 November 2026)

Sheet: `round-2.jpg`: Tin Town from 200 m, from the gate and from the air, and the gate burning from four places.
Built and photographed by a Sonnet helper; the gate's fire drawn by Claude; judged by Claude.

- Tin Town is 45 shacks on scaffolds in six clusters round the old model, in its rust and orange, with a water tower,
  a watchtower, a radio pole, chimney smoke, washing lines, lamps and a fence that joins the gate.
- The gate burns as a whole: a flame up each post, three over the beam, four along the fences, sparks, a column of
  smoke and firelight on the ground.
- The cliffs no longer show stripes, and the sand shows no pale band.

What Claude sees still wrong: from 200 m the town is a thin strip under a very large cliff; it reads as a settlement
from 120 m in. The chimney smoke is faint beyond 120 m. The flames on the fence are evenly spaced and of one shape.
Nothing on the gate chars or falls.

Verdict (Claude): keep.
