# The open land's map, round 2 (OPEN-MAP-2): Claude's verdict

26 November 2026, on `round-2.jpg` taken in the game on the laptop after OPEN-MAP-2 merged.

**Passes.** The ground driven near the Glasshouse now shows the land's own colours, with the hills, and fades into the
pale wash with no hard squares; it reads as known ground and no longer as a fault in the picture. The Glasshouse has
its small mark on both maps, clear of the car's arrow. Radio Hill's rumour circle names it. Round 1's verdict is met.

One thing to watch in a long play, not a fault now: with a little ground driven, the difference between driven and
never driven is gentle. If players miss it after an hour of driving, the wash can be made a little stronger.
