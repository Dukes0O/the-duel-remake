# SKIN-WASTE, round 2 (Claude, 20 November 2026)

Rendered on the laptop's GPU by `skin-waste-look` on the merged code: the eight finishes and the factory paint on the Titan, the
rally car and the Koenigsegg, front three-quarter (the side views are in the scenario's evidence, not kept).

**Verdict: passes for the dev switch.** Round 1's flat colours are gone: Rust Patina rusts at the edges and low panels, Scrap
Patchwork shows real patches in their own colours, Soot Black is worn and darkest low, Desert Sand and Boneyard Silver carry dust and
wear, Bowl Flame burns from the front. Short of right, for a later round: Hazard Stripe reads as wavy tiger stripes, where a hazard
stripe is straight diagonal black and yellow bands; Salt Bleach is so pale on the white cars that it hardly differs from their factory
paint; the rally car's green factory livery stays on top of every finish.
