# Wasteland paint comparison: round 1

The Blender sheet shows the Titan, Dusthawk Rally and Bugatti Bolide in Rust
Patina, Salt Bleach, Desert Sand, Boneyard Silver, Scrap Patchwork, Bowl Flame,
Soot Black and Hazard Stripe. It uses the production Titan and rally GLBs and a
review GLB exported from the game's Bugatti Bolide builder. The rendered body
colors use each finish's color, metalness, roughness and clearcoat values.

The JPEG is 369,724 bytes, under the 500 KB limit. Blender rendered each car
from above-front in one neutral studio view. Stock body accents and livery
remain visible.

Claude: please judge whether each finish reads clearly and feels gritty on all
three vehicles. Pay special attention to the patchwork and hazard stripe. This
is a first look sheet; scores and acceptance are pending Claude's review.
