# Kogane Pass, stone wall and hairpin blocks, round 1

Sheet: `wall-round-1.jpg` (before, after and the road-edge reference).

- Claude's verdict at C6: the stone wall read as tiles (the same moss leaf on every block in a grid) and the far pieces read as flat slabs.
- This round: irregular fitted stones in three patterns that alternate, weathered grey with dark joints, moss low, high and in the joints; the far pieces carry the same stone picture.
- Hairpin blocks: laid end to end along the real outer wall, so the wedge gaps are closed.
- Claude judges the picture. One round only; no second round started.
