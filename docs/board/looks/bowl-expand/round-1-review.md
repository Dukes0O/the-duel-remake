# BOWL-EXPAND round 1 (Claude, 19 November 2026, night)

`look-1.jpg` is the floor from above, drawn from the course data. `round-1.jpg` is six views at night in the real game on
a private port: two from truck height, two from the top row, one in End 2 at truck height and one from above.

**Verdict: in proportion and ready for Kyle's try.** A Titan is small on this floor: 31 truck lengths end to end, 14
across a straight. The stands rise all the way round, full of people, with tunnel mouths, aisles and four floodlight
towers; the Spine reads as a row of rusted containers with Big Mo's booth and flame jets. From the top row the jumps
read as dirt ramps of three sizes with room between them. Driven by hand, the floor does not feel cramped: the kicker
gave 1.2 s of air, the Big Jump 10 m up and 43.7 m along, End 1 was taken at 52 mph.

**Still to do (later rounds, not this card's acceptance):** the crush line's junk cars are the game's plain junk-car
props and look toy-like beside the photographed crushed buses; the banked ends are hard to see in the night light; the
floor is one plain colour with no ruts; the game's rear-view mirror shows in every picture. No frame check was run:
the Bowl is behind a dev switch (rule 9).
