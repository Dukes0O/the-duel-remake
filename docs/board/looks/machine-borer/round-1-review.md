Sheet: `round-1.jpg` (at the mesa's face with her survey line; the mouth at 20 m with her tail going in; the same mouth with
her 47 m in; from behind her five minutes in; daylight at 306 m; driven out onto the far ground). Pictures from the real

## Verdict (Claude): passes, merged, switch off

She reads as a real machine: a segmented yellow shield gone to rust, a cutter head, a trailing gantry on rails (the model is
TOWN-ART-BORER's, carved from photographs). The mouth is a mouth: a striped portal in the hill's face, steel lining behind
it, lamps down the bore. From inside, the far end is a point of daylight that grows; at the end she stands in it. Driven
through a 306 m mesa by hand on the surveyed grade she came out 0.03 m off level.

## What was measured, not judged by eye

- With her head 46.8 m in, from cameras over open ground on both sides and above: about 6,600 of her pixels drawn alone, none
  with the ground drawn. Twice Claude had read a still picture of this hill wrongly; these counts are what settled it.
- Cutting, the Dig draws 283 frames in 5 s; the simulation is on real time; 15.0 m every 30 s at a third lever.

## Short of right

- The spoil bank is a flat pale slab with no ground look. Claude's to draw.
- Without the realism switch the mesa's face is the plain's cracked ground with the hills' beds faint on it.
- Not heard: her sounds (six cues). Not seen: a bend in a bore; two bores meeting.
