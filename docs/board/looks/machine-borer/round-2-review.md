## Verdict (Claude): passes, merged, switch off

The board says in plain words who is cutting, how far, how far off the line, and how long to daylight, whatever machine the
player has in hand. "Go and see" puts her at the working face with the lever on offer. The spoil is a heap now: a ridge of
loose broken rock with sides at their natural slope, drawn apart from the plain (`GROUND_MADE` in the land's ground drawing;
the heap's cells carry how far they were filled).

## Short of right

- The heap meets the ground in a toothed line, a metre to a tooth, where its cells end.
- The board is words in a panel. It wants a picture of each bore (the hill from the side, the bore so far), as her survey has.
- Fresh cuts made by other machines now draw darker, by the same drawing. Seen only on a small heap in the yard; not judged.
- Not seen: a second bore queued from another machine and started by the apprentice; the three calls; two bores meeting.
