# TOWN-ART-TRUCKS-1, round 9: the fire engine

**Claude's verdict: pass for `fire-truck`.** It looks like its photos from the side, front and rear, and it holds together as one truck from every angle, with the wheels on the ground inside their wings. Kyle approved going past six rounds for the photo method on 6 November.

Rounds 7 and 8 built the body from flat parts, which came apart at an angle. Round 9 carves one solid shape from all four photos with `tools/blender/hull_model.py`, and each face wears the photo it faces most. It has 10,352 triangles, and the family file is 3.35 MB.

Small flaws, none worth another round: the ladder's top edge has a slight saw line at an angle, and the mirror and spot lamp on the cab roof are left out, since they were thin enough to leave a fin.

The water tanker, supply truck and tow rig are still the old models. The card stays open for them.
