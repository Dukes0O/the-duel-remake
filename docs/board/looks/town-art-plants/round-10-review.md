# Radar review, round 10

This sheet compares the four orthographic source photos and the exported GLB from their matching orthographic cameras, followed by both three-quarter views and the mayor view.

The open tower and central dish read as a radar installation, and the model stays within the 1,800 triangle budget. The match is incomplete: the side render has jagged photo fragments around the upper platform and ladder, the dish edge shows a red strip not evident in the side reference, and the overhead source does not agree with the other views. The front and rear dish faces read well, but the support platform and mast are smaller and more regular than in the references. Claude should judge the side silhouette, dish thickness/edge, and whether to replace the inconsistent overhead photo before merge.

The radar moving node is `radar:dish`; it rotates around the vertical axis through the dish centre. The moving assembly is independently parented and separate from the fixed tower.
