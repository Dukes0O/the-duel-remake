# TOWN-ART-PLANTS round 1 review

**Piece:** portable jaw crusher, exported GLB reimported into an empty Blender scene.

**Self scores:** silhouette 3/5; material read 2/5; construction detail 2/5; card coverage 1/5.

The worn steel colors read as a used quarry machine at a glance, and the GLB retains separate `crusher:jaw` and `crusher:belt` nodes. The model still has visibly misaligned bars, bolts and support pieces, and its hopper is too block-like. The generated photographic reference could not be saved from the image tool into a local file, so the sheet has no reference crop and I could not do the required side-by-side proportion check.

This is one first-piece round, not a passing family. The batch plant, conveyor pieces, formwork, wellhead, pump station, radar and machine yard are absent. Claude should judge the attached views against the crusher portion of the card and decide whether the shape and surface treatment merit another round.
