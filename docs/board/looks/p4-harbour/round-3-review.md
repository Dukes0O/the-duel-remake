# Harbour Lights, finishing pass, round 3 (Claude, 14 October 2026)

Before and after, same camera, car, seed and quality (High). The street lamps now leave longer, stronger streaks of light on the road (the nearest thing to a wet look the road material allows without new drawing code), there are about 40% more neon signs between Casino Corner and the Fishmarket (frames 2 to 4), and the Fishmarket has its own lantern string. The road is 12 m wide in the old town (10 before) and 13 m at the hairpin; Casino Corner is wider-radius (27 m, 22 before); the Lantern Hairpin braking point no longer launches the car (row 4). Honest verdict: the road is more wet-looking than before but still not a mirror; a stronger gloss needs a change to the road shader, which this pass did not make.
