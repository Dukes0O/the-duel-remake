# WW-ART-RAIL round 3: the 2:15's engine

Date: 6 November 2026
Sheet: `round-3.jpg`
Verdict (Claude): **the engine and its drift pass.** Six rounds are allowed; this is the third.

Round 2 changed the shapes hardly at all, so Claude wrote the recipe itself, with a new kit,
`tools/blender/photo_model.py`, that reads each part's outline and each lathe's radius off the photo.

- **Side:** the same machine as the photo: five wheels, rods, tank plate, dome, chimney and the open cab with its levers.
- **Three-quarters:** a heavy dead engine. You can see through the real cab openings, and the rear sheet's windows are real holes with the backhead behind them. It would not be taken for a toy.
- **Drift:** she is buried to the tank tops to windward and to about the running board on the lee side, with her chimney, dome and cab showing. This is the picture the story turns on.

Small things left, none worth a round: dead side-on the roof's top band is a little streaked; the wheels are solid discs wearing the photo's spokes, which read well at any distance a player sees them from; in a dead front view the chimney stands no higher than the roof, as the side photo has it.
