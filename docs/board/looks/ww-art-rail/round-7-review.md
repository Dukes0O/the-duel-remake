# WW-ART-RAIL round 7: Wednesday and the coal heap

Date: 6 November 2026
Sheet: `round-7.jpg`
Verdict (Claude): **Wednesday and the coal heap pass.**

Codex's photos and hull gave a body that was nearly right. Claude finished her: the hull carves only her body, and the roof, cab, chimney and dome come from the photo kit. Lessons are in `docs/ASSET_PIPELINE.md`.

- **From the side:** the same small engine as her photo, with her dented chimney, dome, brass pot, levers and rods.
- **From three-quarters:** a real, stubborn little dead shunter with a cab you can see through. Not a toy.
- **The heap:** full, hiding her completely, so that her chimney first appears as you dig, with the line `rail.3.chimney`; half; and low. It comes down around her, because she is a separate node.

Small things left: the plate behind her chimney seen from inside the cab is plain, and the sides of her buffer beam are a little smeared. Neither is worth a round.

Still to come on this card: the token and hook, Pim's mark and the track pieces, and the Lost Property props.
