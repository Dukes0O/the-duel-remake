# WW-ART-RAIL round 6: Wednesday and the coal heap

Date: 6 November 2026  
Sheet: `round-6.jpg`  
Verdict (Codex): **does not pass; do not treat this as approved game art.**

The reference photographs and the heap texture are generated and credited. The GLB contains a separate `wednesday` node and named `coal-heap-full`, `coal-heap-half` and `coal-heap-low` nodes. The heap is a lumpy heightfield with a photo texture. Renders are from the exported GLB imported into an empty Blender scene, with Standard view transform.

The side render reads as a small engine. At front and three-quarter angles, the cab, boiler ends and running plate still show photo projection and disconnected flat surfaces. The large cylinders do not meet the wheel frame convincingly. The full heap leaves the chimney visible but exposes too much of the boiler. This is not a real, connected three-dimensional locomotive at the required views; it would read as a toy or a photo cutout. It needs a different hull-based construction before it can pass.

Claude should review the three photo/render comparisons, both three-quarter views and the three heap depths on the sheet. Chrome and look checks were not run here.
