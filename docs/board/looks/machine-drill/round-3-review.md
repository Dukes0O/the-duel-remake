Sheet: `round-3.jpg`. Pictures from the real game, 19 November 2026. One well drilled to water with real keys in a visible
window (the third such play): clay to 3.1 m, gravel to 7.8 m, rock to water at 13.2 m, one jam backed out with S.

## Changed since round 2

Standing water is dark, with rings moving out from the hole, inside a rim of wet earth. Rock is toned down.

## Short of right, and not cured here

- **The first second of water** is pale; mud shows for about half a second. A longer brown gush would say "struck" louder.
- **The truck's deck** is blurred where its photos meet, and its **outriggers** are plain yellow bars on black posts
  that never move. Faults in `machines-2.glb`; they want a Blender round on that card.
- **The wellhead from 30 m** is a small post on a pale pad. It reads by the pad and the falling water, not by its shape.
- **Nothing is heard** that I could judge: the play ran with the window's sound off.
- **The hole itself** is not drawn: the string goes into unbroken ground, hidden by the heap and the spray.

Each of the three rounds improved the picture. I stopped because what is left is in the model and in sound.
