# The drill rig, round 2

Sheet: `round-2.jpg`. Pictures from the real game, 19 November 2026. One well drilled to water with real keys in a visible
window: clay to 3.1 m, gravel to 7.8 m, rock to water at 13.2 m, one jam backed out.

## Changed since round 1

Gravel is drawn as brown pebbles and rock as pale chips, with soft edges. The first water is a jet with small drops. The
water on the ground has a dark wet rim. The wellhead's pad has its concrete. A jammed string shudders. The driller's view
stands further back, with the hole and the sample row in it. The top row now has the old heap, the old caption and the old
wellhead, taken in the game as it was.

## Verdict (Claude): better than round 1, one more round

- **Better.** The sample row reads: three red, five brown, pale rock, one black. The wellhead is a thing worth leaving:
  casing, red valve and wheel, standpipe, water falling, a pale pad.
- **Short of right.** The water on the ground is still a pale grey disc with a frothy edge; it reads as foam. The rock
  piles are nearly white and catch the eye more than the water does.

## Round 3 will

Make standing water dark with the sky in it only at a glancing angle, take the froth off its edge, and tone the rock down.
