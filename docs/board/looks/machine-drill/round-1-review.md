# The drill rig, round 1

Sheet: `round-1.jpg`. Every picture is from the real game in a visible window (`?flags=wasteland-town,the-dig`, memory-only
saves), 19 November 2026. The rig was driven, its mast stood up and one well drilled to water with real keys in real time:
clay to 3.1 m, gravel to 7.8 m, rock to water at 13.2 m, with one jam in the rock backed out with S. The pictures beside
and above the rig are taken by a second camera placed in the Dig's own scene.

## What the game drew before (top row)

The rig was the trencher's tractor with a ladder of boxes. One plain heap changed colour beside the hole, the wellhead was
three grey cylinders, and the caption read "12.2 m deep, in wet." The photo-built drill truck was in `machines-2.glb`,
which the yard never loaded.

**A wording choice for the Director.** With this rig, Tab stands the mast up to drill and lays it down to travel. The
card's own words are "lower the mast", written for the ladder that tipped down onto the ground. The yard's lines now
read "Press Tab to stand the mast up" and "Press Tab to lay the mast down before driving on." No rule changed; the
rules still call the working state `mastDown`.

## Round 2 will

Draw gravel as brown pebbles and rock as pale chips with soft edges; draw the first water as a brown jet that clears and
sinks back, with small drops; draw the water on the ground as dark wet earth with open water in the middle; give the pad
its concrete; make a jammed string shudder; and move the driller's view so the sample row is in it.
