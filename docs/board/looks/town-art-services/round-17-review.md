# Town Services helipad | round 17

Built `helipad` as a separate low assembly in `services-2.glb`. The flat deck is 24 m square and carries the straight-down plate photo, including its faded yellow H and circle. Photo-mapped girders and lathed oil drums support it. The windsock, three fuel drums, hand pump, four corner floodlight posts and emissive lenses are separate children.

Four separate 1024-pixel-wide-or-smaller JPEG references and their credit file are in the town textures folder. I inspected their photo grids and the exported GLB renders. The sheet shows matched side, front, rear and plan comparisons, then both three-quarter views and the mayor view with the other family buildings hidden.

The top view matches the plate layout and H orientation closely. The side references depict a noticeably taller frame than the brief's 1.5 m maximum allows at this 24 m deck width. The model keeps the deck at 0.82 m and every piece at or below 1.49 m, so its side profile is lower than those photos. The windsock, fuel stack and pump remain small from the mayor view; Claude should judge their readability in the full-size three-quarter renders.

The export has 2,628 helipad triangles and is 2,641,460 bytes for the complete family. The piece audit printed all 42 helipad mesh bounds. Every piece is inside the 4-by-4-cell plot, touches the ground or another piece, and remains below 1.5 m. Combined extents are X -12.06..12.06 m, Y 0..1.49 m, Z -12.06..12.06 m.

The sheet is `round-17.jpg` (1,600 by 2,580 pixels, 298,980 bytes).
