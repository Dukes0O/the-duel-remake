# Town Services notice board | round 19

Built the notice board as a flat photo-faced panel with front, rear, side and top references. Two timber posts stand from grade and hold a shallow pitched tin roof on timber supports. A separate photographed timber crate sits at the front as a step.

The photos were generated as single orthographic views, kept as 1024-pixel-wide-or-smaller JPEGs and credited in `notice-board-CREDITS.md`. The board face reads NOTICE from the road; the rear shows the weathered plank bracing. The crate uses its own photo.

The recipe prints all 13 mesh bounds. Every piece touches grade or another piece and remains inside the 1 by 1 cell plot with 0.5 m clearance. The notice board totals 316 triangles. The complete `services-2.glb` is 3,835,268 bytes. The sheet is 1,600 by 2,547 pixels and 257,322 bytes.

I checked the exported model against the photos at the same orthographic cameras. Front, rear, side and above views match the board panel proportions and weathering. Both three-quarter views and the mayor view show the complete assembly on plain grey ground. The heading remains readable and the crate is visible below the board.
