## Five largest differences from the reference

1. The roof is a clean, repetitive grey plane; the reference has overlapping bent sheets, rust streaks and irregular edge wear.
2. The façade lacks thick timber framing, visible welds, and the broad central double door seen in the photo.
3. The roof monitor is a small box instead of the reference's long raised clerestory.
4. The clock lacks the photograph's exposed wheel holes, painted hands, and retaining bolts.
5. The cloth flags and notice board look flat and new; the reference shows torn fabric and weathered paper.

## Verdict

This is a useful first family candidate, not a look pass. The Hall is the quality anchor; the other service buildings are early blockouts. Claude should judge whether the family scope and this first round are ready to carry forward. The sheet does not claim art acceptance or in-game resemblance.
