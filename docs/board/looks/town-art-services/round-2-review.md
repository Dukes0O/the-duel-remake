# TOWN-ART-SERVICES ? Round 2 review

## Source and method

The sheet compares generated front views with the exported GLB. Driver views use a car-height angle from 15 to 40 m. Mayor views look down across the roofs. The recipe imported the final GLB before rendering. This is a Blender review; there is no in-game capture because the art is not wired into the town.

The Town Hall front, Forge front, Scrapyard front, and Workshop front use measured photo outlines and embedded JPEG textures. The Town Hall, Scrapyard, and Workshop also have separate 3D roof geometry or roof repairs. Fixed geometry is joined per building; the two lamps remain separately named. The exporter centers and fits each model within its 8 m cell footprint, leaving a 0.6 m margin. The GLB is 2.69 MB with 37,303 triangles.

## Five largest remaining differences

1. The Town Hall's photographed wheel and the added tyre ring overlap, creating a dark double rim. The spokes also sit over the photo's wheel.
2. The photo-traced fronts look flat from side angles. The Town Hall, Forge, Scrapyard, and Workshop need more real wall depth before a final look pass.
3. The Workshop's roof and side openings are partly covered by the shallow photo face and overhanging roof geometry.
4. The Scrapyard's crane, magnet, and car stacks need clearer separate 3D depth from the overhead view.
5. The other eight service buildings still use generic roofs and simple facade patches; their individual silhouettes and rooftop clutter need work.

## Verdict

Builder review: **not ready to pass yet**. The sheet documents a clear improvement over round 1, but the five differences above need work. Claude's art verdict is pending. No browser, gameplay, or frame result is claimed.
