# WW-ART-FAR, round 1 (Claude session, 26 November 2026)

Built by `tools/blender/far-side.py` and rendered from the exported `far-side.glb`, imported into an empty scene under
one flat grey sky. Each named part is photographed from car height (1.2 m), 15 m from its middle, with the lens fitted to
the part. The wide picture at the top sets the above-ground parts out as Highwater might stand, on wet grass with mist.
Beside it are two real photographs to judge against: Peirce Mill's water wheel (CC0) and the Hoh forest in mist (National
Park Service, public domain), both from Wikimedia Commons and kept in the art library, not in the repository.

The file: 28,880 triangles of the 60,000 allowed and 2.38 MB of the 3 MB allowed, read back from the glb. Twenty named
parts under one root, each at its own origin.

**How it was made.** Codex's picture tool is out of quota, so no new photographs were generated. Surfaces are photographs
the repository already holds (the dam gatehouse's stone, the town's timber, planks and canvas, the borer's and the
handcart's photo sets, the walkers' photo sets, the goat's coat) and pictures drawn in the recipe (slate, moss, grass,
spruce boughs, wool, bark, the old rings' rust runs). The people are carved by the town's walker rig; the goat and the
sheep are the game's own goat reshaped.

**Does it look like nothing else in the game?** Yes: dark green ground, wet grey stone, black-green spruce in mist. It is
not toy-like at the player's distance. It is not photo-real close up, where the stone repeats and the walls are flat.

**Round 2 will change:** smaller stone courses on every building; darker, rougher race water; a shorter top on the
conifers; a second picture of PRUDENCE's deck so that the seat and the lunch tin can be judged; a more broken outline
for the portal's rock face.

Not judged here: the kit in the game (FAR-REGION places it), wetness and rain (FAR-RAIN draws them), and anything
that moves (the wheel's water, lamp light).
