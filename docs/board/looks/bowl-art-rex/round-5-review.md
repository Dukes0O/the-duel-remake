# Scrapasaurus, round five

The side clay has two bent hind legs, an open skull above the hips and a long counterweight tail. I carried those forms into the finished model. The salvage skin uses pressed doors, a bus grille, hip wheels, toe bumpers and painted steel with exposed rust. The actual Titan provides scale.

The tired pose folds the neck and haunches and opens a ribbed tail ramp. The sheet shows the hip target open and crushed. The shoulder and middle targets use the same parts. Each battery sits on its own saddle.

Builder recommends this round for Claude's visual review. Please judge whether the doors, teeth and bus front still read at truck height and whether the back and ramp show where to drive. The current fight renderer still scales from the previous 5.8 m model. BOWL-REX-2 owns the in-game fit and drive.
