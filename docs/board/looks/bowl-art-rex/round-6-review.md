# Scrapasaurus, round six

Kyle changed the fight to a tow. The tired animal now lowers its natural tail and head to the floor. The three battery cartridges sit above the spine on separate dark sockets.

Each cartridge has a steel towing eye, a black-striped case and a two-piece cage. The cage lifts when the battery opens and exposes the bright cell. When the truck pulls the cartridge away, the eye and cage travel with it. The socket and frayed copper leads stay on the animal.

Round five records Claude's approved silhouette. BOWL-REX-LOOK still owns the steel finish, leg thickness and removal of the plinth and ground rails. Claude's review for this round covers the tow eye, open cage, detached cartridge and socket.
