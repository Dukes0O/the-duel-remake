# Tremblant Woods, finishing pass, round 3 (Claude, 14 October 2026)

Before and after, same camera, car, seed and quality (High). The few round orange trees that looked like toys are gone; the grove pines (a little denser, one more row and 5 m deeper) carry the autumn colour. Honest verdict: the tinted pines read as dark rust and red, not bright orange, because the tint multiplies a green crown; the orange now comes from the hills and from the leaves on the ground. Brighter autumn pines need a change to how the tint is drawn, or to the existing test that holds the tinted share at 35 to 45 percent, so it was left alone. New 150, 100 and 50 m boards stand before Lodge Bend and Final Turn (too far ahead to show in these frames). Numbers and tests: docs/changes/P4-FINISH-1014.md.
